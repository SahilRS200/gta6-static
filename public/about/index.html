About

GTA6Info.in: India's GTA 6 desk

GTA6Info.in is a fan-run site for GTA 6 players in India. We track the things that matter here: the unlock time in IST, prices in ₹, PlayStation Store India quirks, scams aimed at Indian players, and launch-night updates.

How we work

  • News is updated every few hours around launch.
  • Every story links its sources at the top. If we can't source it, we don't publish it.
  • We separate confirmed facts from reported ones, and say which is which.
  • We never link pirated copies, APKs or grey-market account sellers.

Not official

We're fans. GTA6Info.in is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony Interactive Entertainment or Microsoft. Grand Theft Auto, GTA and GTA VI are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive.
