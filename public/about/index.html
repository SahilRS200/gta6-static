About

GTA6Info.in: India's GTA 6 desk

GTA6Info.in is the Indian player's GTA 6 companion: what's new, how to do it, and how to fix it — spoiler-safe, in plain language. We track the things that matter here: prices in ₹, times in IST, Indian stores and creators, PlayStation India quirks, and scams aimed at Indian players.

How we work

  • News, guides and fixes are updated every hour. Guides and fixes are living pages: we update them in place as the game changes.
  • Every story links its sources at the top. If we can't source it, we don't publish it.
  • We separate confirmed facts from reported ones, and say which is which.
  • We never link pirated copies, APKs or grey-market account sellers, and we keep story spoilers out of titles and lists.
