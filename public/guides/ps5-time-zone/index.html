How to change your PS5 time zone (and why it won't unlock GTA 6 early)

Sourced from

PlayStation doesn't publish a PS5 date-and-time help page, so we checked the menu path against several guides. The unlock rules come from launch reporting:

  • PowerPyx — How to change PS5 date & time
  • SeekingTech — Change time, date, time zone & format on PS5
  • VICE — GTA 6 PS5 release times & the New Zealand method
  • Notebookcheck — The PSN trick for early GTA 6

  • Prima Games — Changing your region on PS5
TL;DR Settings → System → Date and Time → Time Zone. It takes 30 seconds, but it won't make GTA 6 unlock sooner. The PlayStation Store unlocks by your PSN account region, not your console clock.

Change the PS5 time zone, step by step

  1. Open Settings. From the PS5 home screen, select the gear icon at the top right.
  2. Go to System.
  3. Select Date and Time.
  4. Turn off automatic time if it's on, so you can pick a zone yourself. The label varies a little by firmware ("Set Automatically" / "Adjust automatically").
  5. Select Time Zone and choose your zone. The console clock updates right away.

Menu names can shift between system updates. If yours look different, the sources above have screenshots.

Okay, but why would I change it?

  • You moved or you're travelling, and the console clock is wrong.
  • The clock reset after a power cut or a CMOS battery swap.
  • You want trophy timestamps in your own time.

Not on the list: getting GTA 6 early. Here's why.

Myth vs fact: the "New Zealand trick"

Myth

"Set your PS5 to New Zealand time and GTA 6 unlocks 19 hours early."

Fact

The PlayStation Store unlocks a game by your PSN account's region. Your console clock isn't part of it.

Myth

"Just switch your PSN account to New Zealand."

Fact

PlayStation doesn't let you change the country or region of an existing PSN account.

What people actually do (and why we're not recommending it)

Launch reporting describes players making a separate New Zealand PSN account and buying the game there with NZ store credit. That gets the NZ unlock time, but:

  • You buy the game again, on an account that isn't your main one. Saves, add-ons and future purchases have to match that region.
  • You need NZ-region store credit, and a NZ-compatible payment method.
  • Giving false account details can break PlayStation's terms. That's your account on the line, not ours.
  • Release plans can change. Nothing about this is guaranteed by Sony or Rockstar.

Xbox is different: reports say Xbox lets you change the console's location (Settings → System → Language & location). Check Xbox Support before you try it, because store and payment rules still apply.
