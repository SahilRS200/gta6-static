Stream GTA 6 from your PS5 to YouTube & Twitch

⚠️ First stream? Start the setup now. YouTube says enabling live streaming for the first time can take up to 24 hours. Verify your channel and link your account first, so later it's just "Go Live".

Step 0 — Get your account stream-ready

▶️ YouTube

  • You must be 16 or older to live stream.
  • Verify your channel with a phone number (text or voice code).
  • No live-streaming restrictions on the channel in the past 90 days.
  • First-time enablement can take up to 24 hours. After that you can go live instantly.

💜 Twitch

  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). PlayStation says some services require it for Broadcast.
  • Have your phone handy: linking shows a code or QR to confirm at twitch.tv/activate.

Step 1 — Link YouTube or Twitch to your PS5

  1. Go to Settings → Users and Accounts → Linked Services.
  2. Choose YouTube or Twitch, then select Link Account.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts. Sign in, or scan the QR code / enter the code on your phone.
  4. When it worked, "Linked" appears next to the service name.

You can also link the first time you start a broadcast. Which services you can link depends on your country or region.

Step 2 — Make it look good

Go to Settings → Captures and Broadcasts → Broadcasts:

Video QualityResolution and frame rate. The options depend on the service.
AudioInclude your mic and/or party chat audio.
CameraPosition, size, brightness (PS5 HD Camera or PlayStation Camera).
OverlaysShow viewer comments and new viewers on screen.
Chat to SpeechReads viewer comments aloud (supported languages only).

Step 3 — Go live at midnight 🔴

  1. Press the Create button on your DualSense and select Broadcast.
  2. Pick the service you linked.
  3. Enter your stream title. Put "SPOILERS" in it, because people in later time zones will thank you.
  4. Open Broadcast Options to add your camera or party audio.
  5. Select Go Live. A broadcast card appears in the control center with mic mute, camera, Display Chat, Pause Broadcast and Stop Broadcast.

Stuck on "Preparing"? Quick fixes

  • Turn on 2FA on your Twitch/YouTube account. PlayStation lists this as a cause.
  • Check your YouTube channel is verified on a computer, and allow up to 24 hours after you first enable live streaming.
  • Unlink and relink the service in Linked Services.
  • Check the privacy settings on your linked account if viewers can't find you.

Level up: capture card + OBS (YouTube, Twitch or both)

Want overlays, alerts and a webcam frame? Run the PS5's HDMI through a capture card into a PC and stream with an encoder like OBS:

  1. Connect: PS5 HDMI out → capture card → PC, with passthrough to your TV/monitor.
  2. In OBS, add the capture card as a Video Capture Device source.
  3. On YouTube: YouTube Studio → Create → Go live → Stream tab. Copy the Stream URL and Stream Key.
  4. In OBS: Settings → Stream. Pick YouTube (or a custom RTMP server) and paste the key. Never share the stream key.
  5. Start streaming in OBS, wait for the preview in YouTube's Live Control Room, then hit Go live.

YouTube automatically archives streams under 12 hours. Capture card shows a black screen? Check Settings → System → HDMI for the HDCP option. The OBS and capture-card steps are general practice; YouTube's encoder guide covers the stream-key part.

On Xbox Series X|S?

Xbox supports broadcasting to Twitch through the Twitch app. For YouTube, the capture-card + OBS route above works with any console. Official steps: Xbox Support.
