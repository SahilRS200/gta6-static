Privacy Policy

In short: no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies, no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and we never sell or share data. To run the site we briefly see your IP address, count visits anonymously, and load fonts from Google. Your settings stay in your own browser.

Who we are

GTA6Info.in ("we", "us") is an unofficial, fan-run website about GTA 6 for players in India. The operator of GTA6Info.in decides how the little data described here is used, so for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the Data Fiduciary, and for the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation we are the controller. You can reach us as described in Contact and grievances.

Legal basis (EU/UK): our legitimate interests in running a secure website and counting visits in aggregate (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). India: we process only what is needed to provide the site you asked to use, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

What stays on your device

The site saves a few things in your own browser's storage. They don't leave your device, except the visit ID described above. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

  • Your "Hide spoiler posts" choice.
  • The time of your last visit, to mark posts that are new since then.
  • The random visit ID for the visit counter (deleted when the browsing session ends).
  • An offline copy of pages you opened, so they work without a connection.

We do not use cookies. Search runs inside your browser: we don't receive what you type into the search box.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, forms or newsletters, so we don't hold your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, and no profiling.
  • We never sell, rent or share personal data for marketing.
  • We don't collect sensitive personal data (financial details, passwords, health data and so on).

Other services involved

  • Hosting: Railway (Railway Corporation) runs our server and keeps the request logs above. Railway's privacy policy.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts. Your browser loads our fonts from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when you visit. Google's privacy policy.
  • Our X account, @gamerinfo_in, is run on X, under X's privacy policy.
  • Links to other sites (news outlets, PlayStation, Rockstar, YouTube and others) follow those sites' own policies once you click through.

Our server also fetches public information, such as PlayStation's service status and news headlines. No visitor data is sent with those requests.

International transfers: Railway and Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU/UK data is transferred, they rely on the safeguards they offer, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

In India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): you can ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you, ask us to correct or erase it, use our grievance process below, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

In the EU, EEA or UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we don't link any of this data to a name or account, we may not be able to find data about you. If so, we will tell you. We answer requests within 30 days.

Children

GTA 6 is rated for adults (18+), and this site is meant for adult players. We don't knowingly process children's personal data. If you believe a child has contacted us, tell us and we will delete what we hold.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, keeps the data it holds to the minimum above, and limits what scripts can run on its pages. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we keep very little to begin with.

Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "last updated" date at the top. Material changes will also be noted on the site.

Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, request or complaint, including as our Grievance Officer under Indian law, send a direct message to @gamerinfo_in on X. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
