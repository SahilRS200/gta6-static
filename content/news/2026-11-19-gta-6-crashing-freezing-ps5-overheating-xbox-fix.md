Some players' GTA 6 sessions are ending with a freeze, a trip back to the home screen or, on PS5, the console switching itself off. Here's what's being reported and what players say helps. No story spoilers.

## Update, 27 November: Xbox repair reports too

- **What's reported.** A console repair technician posting on X as Modyfikator89 says more Xbox Series X/S consoles are coming in for repair, with overheating, sudden shutdowns mid-game, freezes after updates and consoles that won't boot. In one case a console stopped starting after a few days of GTA 6 ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-xbox-joins-ps5-blackouts-tech-expert-explains-428656-20261127)).
- **Still unconfirmed.** This is one technician's account, with no count of affected consoles and no proof GTA 6 is the cause. Microsoft hasn't commented, and Sony and Rockstar didn't reply to GAMINGbible about the PS5 shutdowns.
- **Same advice for both consoles:** keep it in an open, ventilated spot, clean dust out of the vents and fan with the console unplugged, take breaks in long sessions, and contact PlayStation or Xbox Support if it won't boot.

## Update, 25 November: reports of PS5s shutting down

- **What's reported.** Some PS5 owners say their console shuts itself off after several hours of GTA 6, with error messages, graphical glitches and, in a few claims, consoles dying. The report that went viral came from one player's post on X showing the console drawing about 220 W at the wall ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-blackouts-begin-ps5-consoles-shut-down-293316-20261125)).
- **Still happening on 25 November.** Gizmodo reports PS5 players still hitting CE-108255-1 and other codes at startup or mid-game after the patches; a Twitch streamer says their console died on stream. Neither Sony nor Rockstar answered Gizmodo either ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/gta-vi-crashing-ps5-2000845327)).
- **Unconfirmed.** There's no count of affected consoles, and no proof the game itself is damaging hardware. Dust-clogged vents and older units are other likely causes. Sony and Rockstar hadn't replied to GAMINGbible at publication.
- **What to do:** give the PS5 space to breathe, clean dust from the vents with the console unplugged, take breaks in long sessions (Indian rooms get warm), and save by hand so a shutdown doesn't cost you progress.

## Update, 24 November: still crashing after the patch

- **Update 01.002.002 hasn't fixed it for everyone.** PS5 players on Reddit say CE-108255-1 crashes continue after the patch, on standard, Slim and Pro consoles. One player on a PS5 Digital Edition says the game crashes **within three to five minutes**; others can't get past the prologue or crash repeatedly in the **Pipework** mission ([Sportskeeda](https://tech.sportskeeda.com/gaming-news/news-gta-6-latest-patch-fails-resolve-persistent-ce-108255-1-error-ps5)).
- **The usual steps didn't help that player:** clearing the cache, rebuilding the database, reinstalling, deleting save data, and turning off HDR, VRR and 120Hz (Sportskeeda). Don't delete your save data hoping it fixes this.
- **No word from Rockstar.** It hasn't named CE-108255-1 or given a timeline. A theory blaming PS5 cooling has no hard evidence behind it (Sportskeeda).
- **What to do:** keep the patch installed, save by hand before every mission, and report the crash to [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/) so it is counted.

## Update, 20 November: PS5 error CE-108255-1

- **What it is.** Many PS5 players now see **CE-108255-1**, Sony's general code for a game or app that crashed. Reports say it hits on the first loading screen, in the opening cutscene, mid-mission or in free roam, while other games on the same console run fine ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/gta-vi-ps5-error-2000843112), [UNILAD Tech](https://www.uniladtech.com/gaming/playstation/gta-6-playstation-crash-fix-sony-support-831369-20261120)).
- **Which consoles.** Original launch PS5s seem hit most, but there are reports from Slim and PS5 Pro owners too (Gizmodo).
- **Sony's official advice:** for CE-108255-1, update the game; for **CE-107891-6** (game or update didn't install properly), go to Settings → Storage, delete GTA 6 and reinstall it. Saves should stay. If it keeps happening, contact PlayStation Support (UNILAD Tech).
- **Not a sure fix.** Many players say updating, clearing the cache and rebuilding the database didn't help. Some outlets suspect worn cooling on older consoles, but that is a theory. Neither Rockstar nor Sony has commented, and there is no Rockstar patch yet (Gizmodo).

## What players are reporting

- **Xbox Series X crashes.** Reddit users say the game freezes for about 30 seconds and then drops to the Xbox menu, for one player every 5 to 20 minutes; reinstalling and playing offline didn't help him ([UNILAD Tech](https://www.uniladtech.com/gaming/xbox/gta-6-xbox-series-x-warning-unplayable-844232-20261119)).
- **PS5 overheating and shutdowns.** A Reddit post showed a streamer's PS5 overheating soon after starting GTA 6. It isn't clear whether the game or the console's own cooling is to blame ([Sportskeeda](https://tech.sportskeeda.com/gaming-news/gta-6-reportedly-overheating-ps5-is-fix)).
- **Other launch problems** in round-ups: freezes in cutscenes and while driving, no sound on PS5, crackly dialogue through Xbox headsets, and colours turning blue and yellow ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-not-working-day-one-problems-fixes/)).

Rockstar hasn't published a known-issues list or explained the crashes.

## What to try

These are player-reported steps, not official fixes ([Business Upturn](https://businessupturn.com/technology/gaming/gta-6-not-working-on-ps5-four-fixes-players-say-solved-launch-crashes-and-psn-errors/)). None of them delete your saves.

1. **Update everything.** Install the latest PS5 system software, then highlight GTA 6, press Options and pick **Check for Update** ([why the PS5 update matters](/news/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/)).
2. **Fully power off and unplug** for 30 seconds. Rest Mode doesn't count.
3. **Restore licences** (Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licences) if the game won't start at all ([padlock fixes](/news/gta-6-locked-not-starting-ps5-fix-india/)).
4. **Give the console air.** Keep about 10 cm of space around it, off thick carpet and away from heat, and vacuum the vents with the power off. If warnings or shutdowns continue, stop and contact PlayStation Support (Sportskeeda).
5. **PS5 display tweak.** One Reddit user's workaround: turn off HDR and 120Hz, set output to 1080p, then clear the cache in Safe Mode (UNILAD Tech). Our [stutter fix](/news/gta-6-stutter-frame-drops-ps5-fix-hdcp-dead-zone/) shows how to clear the cache safely.
6. **Wrong colours?** Check High-Contrast Shaders under Display, or Restore Default. **Xbox headset crackle?** Switch audio to mono (OutputLag).
7. **Save manually and often.** There's only one autosave slot, so a crash can cost you hours ([how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)).

## Is there a patch?

**Yes.** Update **01.002.002**, the first since launch, started rolling out on 23 November: 603.8 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes/)). Rockstar's patch notes say it fixes **"several issues that caused the game to crash"** and a bug that stopped autosaves working properly, but they don't name **CE-108255-1** or the Xbox freezes ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/new-gta-6-update-patch-notes-november-23-2026/)). So install it, keep saving by hand, and use the steps above if crashes continue. [What we know about the update →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

An earlier widely shared X post claimed Rockstar had scheduled a fix for 25 November; Rockstar never announced that date, so treat it as a rumour ([iGameMag](https://igamemag.com/gta-6-reviews/), [OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-not-working-day-one-problems-fixes/)).
