Players have had GTA 6 for a day now (India since **12:00 AM IST**), and the map is already giving up its secrets. Kotaku's launch live blog has been collecting the best finds ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/live-updates-on-gta-6-the-biggest-launch-in-gaming-history-2000758030)). None of these touch the story, so they're safe to read.

## The weird stuff

- **Bigfoot is real, and creepy.** Players heading deep into Leonida's swamps have run into Bigfoot in what Kotaku calls "a deeply creepy encounter". Another player's clip shows a giant crab creature. Rockstar has been teasing Bigfoot since San Andreas, so this one was always coming. The aliens are real too: [the UFO abduction and its hidden trophy →](/news/gta-6-ufo-location-truth-is-out-there-trophy/)
- **The jet-ski dog.** Swim far enough out into the ocean and a dog on a jet ski may turn up to rescue you, Engadget's team found ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)). He pays out, too: [how to find Cool Dog and get $1,200 →](/news/gta-6-cool-dog-lucky-dog-jet-ski-1200-cash/)
- **A 2021 teaser, solved.** A mystery house that appeared only in *GTA: San Andreas – Definitive Edition* has been matched to a house in GTA 6's Belville, right down to the plants outside. Fans mapping Leonida spotted it, which suggests Rockstar slipped in a first look at GTA 6 five years ago ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-players-discover-our-real-first-look-at-the-game-was-likely-hidden-in-a-2021-remaster/)).
- **A Dexter apartment in Vice Beach.** On Washington Island, players found "Trinity Apartments", a waterfront block that looks like Dexter Morgan's home from the TV show. You can't go inside, but a kit on the roof holds a knife, tape and rope, and a nearby boat that resembles Dexter's *Slice of Life* can be stolen. Rockstar hasn't called it a Dexter nod ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-players-find-dexters-apartment-serial-killer-kit-and-can-even-steal-his-boat-3429888/)).
- **A literal Easter egg.** Climb the tallest radio tower and you'll find an egg waiting in a nest. Kotaku's writer found it too. Throw one at the road for a secret cutscene: [where to find it, plus the Fast and Furious car →](/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-fast-furious-car-tower-egg-cutscene/)
- **Tony the Bird is back.** The talking bird from *San Andreas* makes a cameo, and some players think they've spotted Rockstar boss Sam Houser in the game too, though Rockstar hasn't confirmed that ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/18-coolest-details-players-have-spotted-in-gta-6-so-far-2000758435)).

## The phone is the new meme machine

- **Doomscrolling is a real activity.** Each character's phone has an Instagram-style feed with hundreds of posts, memes and comments, tuned to their interests ([iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/11/19/gta-6-apps-social-media-playable-games/)). GamesRadar+ calls scrolling it one of the best new things to do, and reports some in-game posts poke fun at the game's own delays ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-shockingly-accurate-instagram-memes-make-fun-of-rockstars-delays-and-more-as-doomscrolling-becomes-one-of-the-best-new-activities-in-the-game/)).
- **Phone-while-driving clips are everywhere.** The phone blocks part of your view and makes the car harder to steer, so players are posting edited clips of Jason "watching" anime or checking crypto while he drives into trouble ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6s-new-dangerous-driving-and-doomscrolling-feature-spawns-hilarious-memes-3429850/)). Funny to watch, bad for your car: pull over first.
- **Other apps to poke at:** a ride-hailing app called RydeMe and a payments app called BuckMe, and an in-game console with games you can actually play ([iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/11/19/gta-6-apps-social-media-playable-games/)).

## The radio

- **Weird Al Yankovic co-hosts Vice City FM**, the station for 1980s hits. Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen recognised the voice while driving and Rockstar confirmed it. Expect an over-the-top DJ who sometimes yells about a song or a story from his "past life". None of his own songs or parodies seem to be on the playlist, and he isn't in the game as a character ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/yes-thats-weird-al-hosting-a-radio-station-in-gta-6-2000758140)).
- **PinkPantheress is one of the in-game radio DJs**, Kotaku reports. Players are spending the first hours just flicking through stations to see who else turns up. (No, Sidhu Moosewala isn't one of them: [that viral clip is likely a fan edit →](/news/gta-6-sidhu-moosewala-song-viral-clip-fan-edit/))

## Things that change how you play

- **The world doesn't wait for you.** Car crashes, robberies and even a plane crash play out around Leonida whether or not you join in, Kotaku's roundup notes. The in-game TV is good enough that players are watching it for hours, and books lying around can actually be read ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/18-coolest-details-players-have-spotted-in-gta-6-so-far-2000758435)).
- **Your body changes.** Jason can be given a heavier build through the weight system; Lucia changes less, Kotaku found. You can also throw almost anything at people, lizards included ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/18-coolest-details-players-have-spotted-in-gta-6-so-far-2000758435)). Working out is covered in our [gym guide](/news/gta-6-gym-workouts-strength-stamina-guide/).

- **HDR looks washed out?** Engadget suggests turning off your TV's automatic tone mapping, especially on high-end sets such as LG OLEDs. You can also switch units of measurement in the game's settings ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)).
- **Don't use your phone while driving.** Bloomberg's Jason Schreier and Kotaku's reviewer both found that the controls get harder while you're on the phone ([Kotaku review](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-the-kotaku-review-2000757842)). Pull over first.
- **You can pet the dogs and cats**, and it earns you points ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta6-cats-dogs-snake-pet-alligator-2000758120)). It is GTA, so players have also found much less kind things to do with them. Mind the wildlife too: stomp around an alligator's lake and it can eat you, a car you steal may have a very angry cat in the back seat, and you can keep a pet snake that you feed mice ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta6-cats-dogs-snake-pet-alligator-2000758120)).
