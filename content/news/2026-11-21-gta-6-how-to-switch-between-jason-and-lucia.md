**Short answer: hold right on the D-pad to open the character switch, then pick the other protagonist** (GFinity says R1 on PS5, RB on Xbox Series X/S). If nothing happens, you probably haven't unlocked switching yet: it opens up gradually through Chapters 1 and 2 ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6), [GFinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/how-to-switch-between-jason-lucia-in-gta-6)). Spoiler-free: no story events below, only when the feature turns on.

## When switching unlocks

- **Early on you can't switch at all.** The opening hours keep you on one character, and finishing the early mission "Gone Fishing" opens the map but not the switch ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6)).
- **Chapter 1 swaps for you.** Some missions hand control back and forth, but you still can't roam freely as Lucia ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6)).
- **Lucia joins the open world after Chapter 1 ends,** and free switching settles in during Chapter 2 ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6); GFinity puts it in the later part of Chapter 2).

Rockstar chose this on purpose: the team said swapping at any moment would feel unnatural for the story, so it is tied to progress ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-story-is-too-important-for-rockstar-to-let-you-swap-between-lucia-and-jason-whenever-you-want/)).

## Why the switch is greyed out

- **The other character is busy,** usually on a mission or during a cutscene ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6)).
- **They just left.** After a swap, the character you left may call a RydeMe and drive off, which can grey out the menu for a bit. Wait and try again ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-characters-in-gta-6)).
- **You're inside a story mission.** Some missions lock you to one character ([GFinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/how-to-switch-between-jason-lucia-in-gta-6)).

## Tips

- **J and L on the map** mark missions only Jason or only Lucia can start. Switch to the right one first ([GFinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/how-to-switch-between-jason-lucia-in-gta-6)).
- **Expect a short transition.** You'll often catch the other character mid-routine, driving, working out, sleeping or showering ([GFinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/how-to-switch-between-jason-lucia-in-gta-6)).
- **Don't leave one of them alone for days.** Time apart lowers the [relationship meter](/news/gta-6-relationship-meter-jason-lucia-levels-how-to-raise/); a quick text or a hangout fixes it.
- **Save first** if you're about to switch away mid-errand. Here's [how to save by hand](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/).
