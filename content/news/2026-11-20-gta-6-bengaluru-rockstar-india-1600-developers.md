While India stayed up for the midnight unlock, a lot of the game it was waiting for had been made in **Bengaluru**. Bengaluru South MP **Tejasvi Surya** posted on X on 18 November that **more than 1,600 developers** in the city worked on a significant part of GTA 6 at **Rockstar India** ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-has-a-bengaluru-connection-you-probably-dont-know-about.html)).

## What the Bengaluru team worked on

- **About 1,615 developers** at Rockstar's studio in **Whitefield** contributed, according to reports cited by [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2026/11/19/gta-6-meets-namma-benglooru-how-indias-tech-capital-built-rockstars-blockbuster.html).
- Their work covered **environments, assets and world-building**, including **Vice Beach** in the game's state of Leonida ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2026/11/19/gta-6-meets-namma-benglooru-how-indias-tech-capital-built-rockstars-blockbuster.html)).
- It isn't the studio's first Rockstar game: the same team also worked on **GTA V** and **Red Dead Redemption 2** ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2026/11/19/gta-6-meets-namma-benglooru-how-indias-tech-capital-built-rockstars-blockbuster.html)).

## Where the studio came from

Rockstar India grew out of **Dhruva Interactive**, a Bengaluru studio Rajesh Rao started in the 1990s with a single computer. Rockstar bought it in **2019**, the first Indian game studio it acquired ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-has-a-bengaluru-connection-you-probably-dont-know-about.html), [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2026/11/19/gta-6-meets-namma-benglooru-how-indias-tech-capital-built-rockstars-blockbuster.html)).

"We all know Bengaluru for its IT industry, but it's exciting to discover the many facets of our ecosystem," Surya wrote ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-has-a-bengaluru-connection-you-probably-dont-know-about.html)).

## What isn't confirmed

The **1,600** figure comes from Surya's post and press reports. **Rockstar has not published a headcount** or said which parts of the game each studio built, so treat the number as reported, not official. The game's end credits are the place to check once you finish the story.
