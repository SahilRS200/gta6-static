If you bought a PS5 Pro hoping for 60fps GTA 6, here's the answer straight from Rockstar: **no**. The game runs at a **locked 30fps on every console, PS5 Pro included, with no performance mode**, and Rockstar's tech leads have now explained why in an interview with [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-pro-is-not-powerful-enough-to-run-gta-6-at-60fps-rockstar-explains-frame-rate-decision).

## Why not 60fps

- **It's the CPU, not the graphics.** Rockstar's head of technology production, Marc Chambers, told Push Square the Pro's processor is only "slightly better" than the base PS5's and is **not powerful enough to run the game's simulation at higher frame rates**. He said the work fits "only just" inside the ~33 ms budget a 30fps frame allows.
- Independent tech analysts had already predicted the same CPU bottleneck ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/60fps-feels-like-a-bridge-too-far-gta-6-is-likely-to-only-run-at-30fps-on-ps5-consoles-due-to-cpu-limitations-tech-analysts-say-but-theres-hope-that-a-40fps-mode-would-be-a-good-option-if-60-is-off-the-table)).

## What Rockstar did instead

- **A full frame less input lag.** VP of technology David Hynd said Rockstar reads your controller "as late as possible" in each update, removing one whole frame of input delay compared with its past games ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-pro-is-not-powerful-enough-to-run-gta-6-at-60fps-rockstar-explains-frame-rate-decision)).
- **Snappier animations.** Dead time in animations was trimmed and they can be interrupted earlier, so moves should feel more responsive.
- **The Pro still looks better.** Chambers says the Pro's stronger GPU and upscaling are used for sharper visuals. Push Square's reviewer called Pro performance "smooth throughout", and [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-gameplay-first-look-surfaces-online-watch-videos-of-how-the-game-starts-and-graphics-look.html) reports improved visuals and ray tracing on the Pro.
