No PS5 at home and no ₹70,000 for one? If you're in Delhi or Navi Mumbai, you can still play GTA 6 **today**. **Comic Con India**, part of NODWIN Gaming, is running two launch-day gaming sessions on **Thursday 19 November, from 2 PM to 6 PM IST** ([EventFAQs](https://in.eventfaqs.com/2026/11/18/comic-con-india-to-host-gta-6-launch-day-gaming-gatherings-in-delhi-and-navi-mumbai/)).

## Where and when

- **Delhi:** Saket Social, hosted by streamer Ziya Mishra.
- **Navi Mumbai:** Vashi Social, hosted by gaming and anime creator Abhishek Ranjan.
- **Time:** 2 PM to 6 PM IST today ([Indian Broadcasting World](https://www.indianbroadcastingworld.com/comic-con-india-host-gta-6-launch/)).

Comic Con India says fans can **play GTA 6 without buying the game or owning a console**, and meet other players. The announcements don't say whether entry is free or whether you need to register, so check Comic Con India's social accounts before you head out. Business Standard also reported the two gatherings in its look at the business around the launch ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/gta-vi-arrives-after-13-years-unlocking-a-business-world-beyond-gaming-126111800950_1.html)).
