GTA 6 has its own take on Red Dead Redemption 2's honor meter, called the **Criminal Profile**. It doesn't judge you for being a criminal: it judges **how messy** you are about it. Get it wrong for long enough and one level locks in for good. Here's how it works. **No story spoilers.**

## The 5 levels, best to worst

1. **Professional** — clean jobs, non-lethal takedowns, civilians left alone.
2. **Hardened** — you kill enemies, but you don't hurt bystanders.
3. **Violent** — you go lethal often and ignore the quieter options.
4. **Psycho** — killing civilians, cops and animals for no reason.
5. **Irredeemable** — you kept going after Psycho. **This one is permanent.**

Each level has its own horned-face icon, from yellow at Professional to dark grey at the bottom ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/all-criminal-profiles-explained-gta-6/)).

## What moves it

- **Violence is the big one.** Stealth, non-lethal takedowns and leaving civilians alone keep you near Professional. Finishing off enemies who are already down pushes you towards Violent ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).
- **Rob cleanly.** Threaten shop staff instead of hurting them, cut the alarm and destroy the tapes ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).
- **What drags you to Psycho:** killing lots of cops or civilians, killing animals (hunting doesn't count), and driving over gravestones ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).
- **Self-defence doesn't count against you;** attacking people who aren't fighting back does ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/all-criminal-profiles-explained-gta-6/)).

## Why it matters

- **Focus:** at Professional and Hardened you charge Focus with stealth and non-lethal takedowns; at Violent and Psycho, with headshots. Irredeemable gets **no Focus bonus** at all ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/)).
- **The cops:** you can **surrender** up to Violent, but not at Psycho or Irredeemable, and police are more likely to shoot on sight ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)). - **The story:** guides say your profile can change how parts of the story play out. We won't say how.

## Irredeemable: how to avoid it

- **Watch for the warning.** The icon cracks in three stages before it breaks, and time slows as it happens ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/)).
- **Climb back while you can.** You can move freely between the first four levels by playing cleaner ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).
- **Save by hand before a rampage.** Once a character is Irredeemable, the only way back is loading a save from before it happened ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/)). Use the manual slots: [how to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
- **Jason and Lucia are rated separately,** so one can stay clean even if the other goes off the rails ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).

## Rockstar: it adds options, it doesn't take any away

Some players feel the system punishes classic GTA chaos. Rockstar North co-studio head **Rob Nelson** told VGC the team expected that pushback, but says it "changes what GTA can be": "You can still do whatever you wanted to do before, but now you have other options" ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/grand-theft-auto-6s-criminal-profile-system-changes-what-gta-can-be-says-co-studio-lead), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gta-6-rockstar-expected-criminal-profile-to-be-divisive/)). ## How to check yours

Open the map and press **L1 (PS5) / LB (Xbox)** to reach the **Jason & Lucia** tab, or **hold Right on the D-pad** in play to show the icons in the corner ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/all-criminal-profiles-explained-gta-6/)).
