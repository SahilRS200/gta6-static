The rumour was right: **GTA 6 critic reviews went live today, 18 November**, a few hours before the game unlocks in India at **12:00 AM IST**. And the verdict is about as strong as it gets.

## The score: 99

- **Metacritic: 99 out of 100.** That ties *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* for the highest Metacritic score ever ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-metacritic-score-makes-it-the-highest-rated-game-of-all-time-matching-the-og-zelda-ocarina-of-time/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-is-the-joint-best-reviewed-game-of-the-all-time-3429300/)).
- **Almost all perfect scores.** [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/18/the-gta-6-metacritic-review-score-is-in-and-ties-an-all-time-record/) counted 80 perfect 10s out of 109 reviews at the time, with **nothing below 90**.
- **Higher than every earlier Rockstar game.** GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 sit at 97, and GTA IV at 98.
- **Early numbers move.** The review count was still climbing through the afternoon, so the score could shift by a point as more reviews arrive. Most reviews so far are for PS5. Only a handful cover Xbox Series X|S.

Roundups from [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-review-roundup/) and [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-review-roundup/) list perfect scores from major outlets, including IGN and GameSpot.

## The complaints

Even a 99 has caveats. Here's what reviewers flagged most:

- **30 fps on console.** Rockstar has said the game targets 30 fps on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PS5 Pro, with no performance mode at launch ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-will-launch-at-30fps-on-ps5-and-xbox-for-now-confirms-rockstar-but-while-60fps-is-reportedly-possible-its-a-40fps-mode-we-really-want-to-see)). The PS5 Pro gets a cleaner image, not a higher frame rate.
- **Gunplay and controls.** Some reviewers found smaller weapons and some controls clunky, even while praising the bigger set-piece fights ([VICE roundup](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-review-roundup/)).

## Ignore the "user score"

Metacritic user ratings were flooded with fake scores before launch ([how the review embargo and the fake early ratings played out →](/news/gta-6-review-embargo-when/)). Since launch, user ratings are open and the PS5 user score opened at **5.1** amid review bombing, with many reviewers admitting they hadn't played. The 99 is the **critic** score. ## Reading reviews without spoilers

Reviews avoid the ending, but **thumbnails, headlines and comment sections don't.** If you want to go in fresh, stick to the score and the verdict paragraph, and keep `GTA 6 ending` and `GTA 6 spoilers` muted on X and Instagram.
