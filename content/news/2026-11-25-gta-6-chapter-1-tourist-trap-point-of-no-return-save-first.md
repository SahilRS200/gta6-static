GTA 6's first chapter is easy to rush: the story keeps pointing you at the next mission. But one mission, **Tourist Trap**, closes Chapter 1 for good, and a player who has put 100 hours in says it's worth slowing down before you start it ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-100-hours-advice-players-on-chapter-1-773740-20261124)). No story spoilers here: we only tell you what changes for your save, not what happens.

## Why it matters

- **It's a point of no return.** Once you finish Tourist Trap, you can't freely go back to Chapter 1's slice-of-life content (GAMINGbible).
- **Key Lento is still there, but different.** You can visit it later, yet the story has moved on and the area doesn't feel the same (GAMINGbible).
- **Your stash is safe.** Things you left at Jason's house, including hidden cash, stay there for later (GAMINGbible). [Why you should stash cash →](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)

## What to do before you start it

1. **Spend longer in Chapter 1 than you planned.** Jason can roam Leonida before Lucia is playable; explore past the story markers and enjoy the home life (GAMINGbible).
2. **Make a new manual save** in a fresh slot right before the mission, and don't overwrite it. That save is your way back to Chapter 1 later, since the game has [no mission replay menu](/news/gta-6-replay-missions-no-replay-menu-save-slots/). [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
3. **Nothing is lost for 100%.** Completion items aren't missable, so this is about enjoying Chapter 1, not trophies. [100% checklist →](/news/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist-requirements/)
