A hacker calling itself **Cyberleek** says it has put most of a **GTA 6 Xbox build** online and is holding back the one file needed to run it. It is the biggest piracy threat since launch, and it will reach Indian Telegram groups and "free download" sites fast. **Short version: it isn't playable, it isn't safe, and it isn't a PC version.** No story spoilers here.

## Update, Friday 27 November: a new fan port, 17–25 FPS on an RTX 5090, gone within an hour

- **Another unofficial port briefly surfaced.** A private project called CGBJ, built on the leaked Xbox build, had its code posted publicly on Thursday 26 November; it was reachable for **under an hour** before it disappeared. It isn't clear whether its makers pulled it or the host blocked it ([PC Games Hardware](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/GTA-6-Spiel-55239/News/GTA-6-PC-port-reports-25-fps-and-suddenly-goes-offline-1561150/)).
- **Even a top graphics card can't run it well.** Reported averages range from about **20–25 FPS**, and the developer's own test log works out to roughly **17–18.5 FPS on an RTX 5090** in a two-minute street test. No fan build has shown a steady 30 FPS, PC Games Hardware says.
- **More takedowns.** At least four more GTA 6 PC port projects have vanished since Take-Two's 24 November notice. Rockstar and Take-Two still haven't commented.
- **"Finished GTA 6 PC" downloads are fake.** Port trackers warn that offers of complete ports are fakes, and suspect malware in almost all of them. Copies passed around under the CGBJ name can't be verified either. If you want GTA 6 on PC, wait for Rockstar: [PC release date →](/news/gta-6-pc-release-date-gta-5-steam-players-rise/)

## Update, Wednesday 25 November: Take-Two's takedown notice is public

- **It was a DMCA notice after all.** Take-Two's notice against Leonida is now in GitHub's public DMCA archive, dated 24 November. GitHub disabled the project **and its whole fork network: 3,007 repositories** ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/news/gta-6s-unofficial-pc-port-lasted-three-days-before-take-two-wiped-3007-github-repos/), [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-pc-port-project-taken-down-after-xbox-executable-leaks-online-here-is-what-happened.html), [the notice](https://github.com/github/dmca/blob/master/2026/11/2026-11-24-take-two.md)).
- **Take-Two says the files were stolen.** The notice says files from an Xbox Series build of GTA 6 were stolen and publicly distributed on 20 November, and calls Leonida's "bring your own files" setup intentionally misleading, citing the game's terms against reverse engineering (Output Lag).
- **Some rival projects are still up**, Digit reports, but none runs the game. Nothing about the advice below changes: don't download "GTA 6 PC" files.

## Update, Tuesday 24 November: the biggest PC port project is gone

- **Leonida went offline overnight.** The largest fan project trying to run the leaked Xbox build on PC vanished overnight from Monday to Tuesday, European time: its GitHub repository was moved and then deleted, and its Discord of about 179,000 members disappeared ([PC Games Hardware](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/GTA-6-Spiel-55239/News/GTA-6-PC-ports-Leonida-offline-VIctory-leads-XCL-1560947/)).
- **Its lead quit.** The founder, NotRexed, says he is stepping away because of "circumstances beyond his control" and claims Rockstar had his servers taken down. Rockstar and Take-Two hadn't commented, and PC Games Hardware hadn't found a DMCA notice on GitHub by then (it appeared later; see above) ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/As-GTA-6-PC-port-gets-closer-to-becoming-playable-Rockstar-is-reportedly-taking-action-against-modders-and-Discord-communities.1458181.0.html), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-recomp-leaders-bail-out-as-take-two-cracks-down-on-illicit-efforts-to-get-the-game-running-on-pc/)).
- **Reported crackdown on Discords.** Reddit users say other GTA 6 modding and port servers were also removed; Notebookcheck calls these claims unverified.
- **Still nothing playable.** Two rival projects, VIctory and XCL, reportedly reach the game world but stay dark or stuck on a loading icon, at around one frame a second, and crash the graphics card ([PC Games Hardware](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/GTA-6-Spiel-55239/News/GTA-6-PC-ports-Leonida-offline-VIctory-leads-XCL-1560947/)).
- **More fakes.** PC Games Hardware warns of "GTA 6 for PC" downloads that are password-stealing malware, including repos labelled "Switch Version". Don't touch them.

## Monday 23 November: the missing file is reportedly out

- **The executable has reportedly been posted.** [DSOGaming](https://dsogaming.com/news/cyberleek-just-leaked-gta-6-xbox-series-x-build-executable) and [TwistedVoxel](https://twistedvoxel.com/gta-6-xbox-build-executable-released/) report that Cyberleek released it for free on Monday. Rockstar hasn't commented and nobody has independently confirmed it.
- **It still doesn't run on PC.** TwistedVoxel says a lot of technical work remains: hobby projects still have to translate the console's graphics code and rebuild Xbox system functions before anything is playable. Cyberleek's claim of 30fps-plus on PC is unproven.
- **Earlier today:** TechRadar had reported that the file was due on 23 November ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/final-piece-of-claimed-gta-6-leak-set-to-arrive-today-and-theres-hope-it-could-mean-rockstar-launches-the-pc-port-sooner)).
- **Security researchers think the files are real.** The research group VX Underground looked through the dump, found real game assets and believes the leak is genuine. Its theory, not a confirmed fact, is that Cyberleek got the build by compromising a Rockstar employee ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/final-piece-of-claimed-gta-6-leak-set-to-arrive-today-and-theres-hope-it-could-mean-rockstar-launches-the-pc-port-sooner), [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114815/cyberleek-claims-modders-could-get-gta-6-running-on-pc-within-a-week-after-reported-xbox-build-leak/index.html)).
- **Still nothing that runs.** Nobody has shown the files running as a full game on PC. TechRadar expects any early build to be rough and crash-prone, and Cyberleek says it will try a PS5 build if the Xbox one doesn't work.
- **Expect fakes today.** TechRadar warns that malware dressed up as "GTA 6 PC" builds is likely to spread around this drop. Don't download anything, from a torrent, Telegram or YouTube link.

## What has been claimed

- **A 209 GB download** that Cyberleek calls a "full November 2026 build" of GTA 6 for Xbox Series X/S, posted without the executable (the .exe), the file that actually starts the game ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Cyberleek-releases-GTA-6-Xbox-build-making-playable-PC-port-more-realistic.1454770.0.html), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-leaker-cyberleek-reportedly-threatens-to-release-the-xbox-build/)).
- **The catch is a crypto coin.** Cyberleek says it will release the missing file early if its meme coin reaches a **$15 million** market value, or on **23 November**, whichever comes first. The coin was worth about $1.5 million when Insider Gaming checked, so 23 November is the likelier date ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-leaker-cyberleek-reportedly-threatens-to-release-the-xbox-build/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/a-playable-gta-6-build-could-leak-but-theres-a-15-million-catch/)).
- **It has made a threat like this before.** About a month ago it tied a similar drop to a $30 million coin target, then cashed out, Insider Gaming says.
- **Is it real?** Destructoid says the files shared do appear to be GTA 6's, but nobody has shown that they run. Notebookcheck notes the main evidence is Cyberleek's own posts and screenshots of a file list ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/gta-6-could-be-playable-on-pc-soon-as-hacker-drops-incomplete-xbox-build/)). Rockstar has not commented.

## "Emulated inside a week"? Not so fast

Cyberleek claims PC players will be running it above 30fps within a week. **No working Xbox Series X/S emulator exists today**, Notebookcheck and Destructoid point out; the console runs its own operating system that would have to be rebuilt for Windows. That is a hacker's promise, not a fact.

## What it means for you in India

- **Don't download it.** A 209 GB torrent from an anonymous source, or a "GTA 6 PC" file on Telegram, YouTube or a "free download" site this week, is exactly where malware hides. Kaspersky has already logged over 27,000 GTA 6 malware hits ([our scam warning](/news/gta-6-free-download-apk-scam-warning/)).
- **There is still no official PC version** and no date. GamesBeat's early-2027 report is unconfirmed ([PC release date](/news/gta-6-pc-release-date-gta-5-steam-players-rise/)).
- **Dodge spoilers.** If you're still early in the story, mute "GTA 6 leak" on X and Instagram for a few days.

This is a different case from the 2022 hacker, who was sentenced to seven years on the same day, 23 November, and walked free. [That case →](/news/gta-6-leaker-arion-kurtaj-pleads-guilty-sentencing/)
