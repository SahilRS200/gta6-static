Trading cards are GTA 6's biggest collectible: **480 cards in 18 sets**, from Vice City sports teams to comics and dancers ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)). They're early money, a trophy and part of 100% completion. And now Rockstar and Panini are printing real ones. **No story spoilers.**

## Where cards are

- **They're hidden, not sold.** Cards sit in fixed spots indoors and out: wall cabinets, tables, counters, garages and unattended toolboxes. A marker shows up when you're close ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **Yellow boxes on walls hand out random cards,** and those count toward your sets too ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **Track them** under Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards. Unsold cards go into a box in your safehouse ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **Need a map?** Dexerto has pinned the 103 cards players had found by 24 November ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/collectible-card-locations/)).
- **Watch out:** Red Bull says two cards may become unreachable after Chapter 2, and some need a tool or more story progress first ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).

## Selling them

- **Take them to a pawn shop.** Common cards fetch about **$20**; rare variants such as Gold can go for **well over $1,000** ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **Selling doesn't cost you progress.** A sold card stays in your log and still counts toward 100%. Duplicates add nothing to a set, so sell them ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **Bronze, Silver and Gold versions count as separate cards** ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- Remember you lose cash when you die or get busted: [hide your money →](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)

## Trophies and 100%

- **"Don't Touch My Deck"** unlocks for finishing **three sets.** Start with the smallest: Backyard Wrestling (12 cards) and Delights Dancers (18) ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-collectible-card-sets)).
- **100% needs 15 of the 18 sets:** [the full checklist](/news/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist-requirements/) · [all trophies](/news/gta-6-trophy-list-platinum-missable-trophy-guide/)

## Real cards from Panini

Rockstar and **Panini America** are making the first official GTA trading cards, the **GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection**, due in **December 2026**. Hobby boxes hold four packs of four cards, with numbered rarities: Bronze Legend cards limited to 49, Silver Legend to 25, and one-of-one Gold cards ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/rockstar-is-turning-gta-6s-in-game-trading-cards-into-real-collectibles-3431102/)). **No price, stores or countries have been announced**, so there's no word yet on India. Buy only from official retailers once they're listed.
