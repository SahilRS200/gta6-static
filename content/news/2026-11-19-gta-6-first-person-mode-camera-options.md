If you've just started GTA 6 and are hunting the menus for the first-person toggle GTA 5 had on PS4 and PS5: **there isn't one.** GTA 6 launched without a full first-person mode. **No story spoilers below.**

## What Rockstar said

Rockstar North co-studio head **Rob Nelson** said at a pre-launch preview in Edinburgh that GTA 6 is built primarily around a **third-person camera**, and that the team put its effort into third-person animation. The comment was reported by creator Davy Jones, who attended the preview, and picked up by [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/will-gta-6-have-first-person-mode/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/no-first-person-mode-in-gta-6-rockstar-confirms/) and [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113322/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-will-skip-a-dedicated-first-person-mode-at-launch/index.html).

## Is there any first-person view at all?

Only in some moments, according to reports. VICE says you can switch to a first-person view when **aiming certain weapons**, and Beebom adds **driving**. Rockstar hasn't published a full list, so check the camera options in your settings menu; we'll update this post if Rockstar clarifies.

## Will first-person come later?

Rockstar hasn't announced anything. For history: GTA 5 launched in 2013 without first-person and added it about a year later with the PS4 and Xbox One release, as [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-drops-first-person-mode-at-launch/) notes. That is a precedent, not a promise. Ignore social posts claiming a first-person "update" is coming unless Rockstar's own Newswire says so.
