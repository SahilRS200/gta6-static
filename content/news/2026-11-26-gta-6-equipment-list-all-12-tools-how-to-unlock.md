Wondering why you can't open that crate or that locked door yet? GTA 6 gives you **12 pieces of equipment**, and most of them arrive on their own as the story moves through **Chapters 1 to 3**. Once you have one, it stays with you for good, [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-all-equipment-locations/) reports, and [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/all-gta-6-equipment-explained/) lists the same 12. Only mission names below; no story spoilers.

## Chapter 1: the basics

- **Binoculars** — scope out enemies and robbery targets from a distance, and study animals. Unlock automatically in the mission *Gone Fishing*.
- **Flashlight** — lights up dark rooms. Also from *Gone Fishing*.
- **Duffel Bag** — carries loot (marked bills, jewellery and more) to a drop-off. Comes in *Lab Fees*.
- **Headphones** — listen to the radio on foot. **Buy them** at an electronics store once *Gear Up* is done.
- **Masks** — hide your face from witnesses, cops and CCTV, which helps keep your [wanted level](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/) down. **Buy them** at Ammu-Nation after *Gear Up*.
- **Metal Detector** — finds metal nearby, and works underwater for [treasure diving](/news/gta-6-treasure-diving-scuba-kenton-shipwrecks/). Unlocks after *Island Dreams*.

## Chapter 2 and 3: breaking in

- **Slim Jim** — opens cars without setting off the alarm. Arrives in Chapter 2's *Moses*.
- **Pry Bar** and **Cut-Off Tool** — break open sealed crates and cut heavy locks on containers and some vehicles. Both come in Chapter 3's *Meet the Heders*.
- **Dye Pack Jammer** — stops the dye packs hidden in marked bills from going off during bank and payday-loan robberies. Comes in Chapter 3's *Credit Check*.
- **Key Cloner** — steals keyless high-end cars. You get it during Randy's job to steal a Zorrusso, after you meet him in Chapter 3. [How car theft works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/)
- **Snap Gun** — picks door locks without tripping alarms. It's the odd one out: finish **one of Yasmani's jewellery jobs**, which open up after Chapter 3's *Boobie Time*. Both it and the Dye Pack Jammer matter most in [bank robberies](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-bank-vault-payouts-marked-bills/).

## Tips

- **Missing a tool? Push the story on.** Most of the 12 come from story missions, so if a door or crate won't open, you are probably just early.
- **Buy a mask before your first robbery.** It helps against CCTV. [How to rob a store →](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-store-safe-code-cameras-escape/)
- **Going for 100%?** Keep the Metal Detector handy for treasure under water. [100% checklist →](/news/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist-requirements/)
