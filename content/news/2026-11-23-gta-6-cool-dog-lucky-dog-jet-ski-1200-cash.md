The dog in sunglasses riding a jet ski is one of the most shared GTA 6 clips since launch, and players call him **Cool Dog** (guides also call him **Lucky Dog**). He isn't just a joke: follow him home and he hands you **$1,200**. Here is how to make him show up. **No story spoilers.**

## How to make Cool Dog appear

1. **Get a boat and head into Gambit Bay.** The dock at your house is the easy start; the boat is only there to get you out to sea ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-find-lucky-dog-in-gta-6-secret-jet-ski-dog-location/), [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/how-to-find-lucky-dog-grand-theft-auto-6/)).
2. **Jump off and swim away from the boat.** You have to be a stranded swimmer, not sitting on a boat. If there's another boat or an island close by, swim further out ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/how-to-find-lucky-dog-grand-theft-auto-6/)).
3. **Wait about 5 minutes alone in the water.** It's a rough wait, not an exact timer. Listen for barking behind you; that's him arriving ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-find-lucky-dog-in-gta-6-secret-jet-ski-dog-location/), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-find-cool-dog-lucky-dog)).
4. **Climb onto his jet ski.** He carries you to his island hideout. Gfinity says the ride first stops at the shore, so stay on if you want to reach the island.

Your progress in the story doesn't matter: he can turn up at any point ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-find-lucky-dog-in-gta-6-secret-jet-ski-dog-location/)).

## How to get the $1,200

- **Play with him.** On the island, pick up a ball and throw it for him, then interact with him. He shows you around ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-find-cool-dog-lucky-dog), [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/how-to-find-lucky-dog-grand-theft-auto-6/)).
- **Let him dig.** He eventually digs up a hidden stash: a wallet with **$1,200** in it. You only get the cash if you finish the island visit; just seeing him at sea pays nothing ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/how-to-find-lucky-dog-grand-theft-auto-6/)).
- **No trophy.** Finding him doesn't seem to unlock a hidden trophy or achievement, so do it for the cash and the clip ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-find-lucky-dog-in-gta-6-secret-jet-ski-dog-location/)).
