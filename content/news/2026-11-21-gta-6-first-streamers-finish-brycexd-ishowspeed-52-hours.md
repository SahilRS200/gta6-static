**GTA 6 has been finished on stream, barely two days after launch.** New Zealand streamer BrycexD reached the credits first, and IShowSpeed followed after a marathon of about 52.5 hours. This post is spoiler-free: no story details, no ending.

## Who finished first

- **BrycexD: 39 hours 38 minutes.** That's the timer on his credits screenshot. Being in New Zealand, he started hours before the US and most of Europe (and India). He said he stayed awake for 50+ hours without breaks and peaked at about 14,000 viewers. Fifteen hours later he said he had "no idea" what happened in the story and plans to replay it ([Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/20/brycexd-and-ishowspeed-become-the-first-streamers-to-complete-gta-6/)).
- **IShowSpeed: about 52.5 hours.** He started playing at midnight US time, was past 80% of the story by about 40 hours, and got emotional at the end ([Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/20/brycexd-and-ishowspeed-become-the-first-streamers-to-complete-gta-6/)). His final stream had about **12 million views** when Win.gg checked ([Win.gg](https://win.gg/ishowspeed-beats-gta-6-52-hour-stream/)).

## The scare every player should learn from

About 43 hours in, the game glitched on IShowSpeed and he thought he'd lost his progress because he hadn't saved. He hadn't: he loaded a save from 88% and carried on ([Win.gg](https://win.gg/ishowspeed-beats-gta-6-52-hour-stream/)). VGTimes reports a later save mix-up after the credits ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175291-brycexd-and-ishowspeed-reportedly-become-first-streamers-to-finish-gta-6.html)).
