Started GTA 6 at **12:00 AM IST** and wondering how far you'll get before the weekend? Plan for a long one. The best figures so far put the **main story at roughly 50 hours**, against about 30 for GTA 5. No story spoilers below.

## The numbers so far

- **50 hours 42 minutes:** how long Screen Rant's Chris Carter took to finish the story, doing some side content but skipping most of the long optional activities ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-main-story-length-completion/)).
- **80 hours:** Rockstar co-studio head Rob Nelson's own latest playthrough, which he told the New York Times included "some optional goals", so read it as story plus extras, not a straight run ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-story-length-how-long-to-platinum/)).
- **Around 200 hours for 100% and the Platinum trophy**, according to reports. That covers gold medals on story missions, all 170 wildlife species, collectibles and challenges ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-long-to-beat-gta-6/)).
- **About 90 missions across five chapters**, per Beebom; Screen Rant puts it at nearly 100. Rockstar has not published an official count or play time.

## What that means for you

At **2 hours a night on weekdays and more on weekends, the story is roughly a 3–4 week job** for most working players and students in India. A few things worth knowing before you plan around it:

1. **Cheats won't speed it up.** All 61 unlock only after the credits roll ([how GTA 6 cheats work](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/)).
2. **There's no online mode to distract you yet.** GTA 6 is single-player at launch ([multiplayer explained](/news/gta-6-online-multiplayer-not-at-launch/)).
3. **Spoilers will outrun you.** At 50+ hours, most people won't finish this month, while 24-hour streams race ahead. Mute "GTA 6 ending" on social for a few weeks ([our spoiler guide](/news/gta-6-spoilers-rockstar-discord-warning/)).
