If you're in **Chapter 3** of GTA 6, hold off on the **Missing Persons** side mission in Kelly County. Players report that finishing it leaves them **locked as Lucia**: the switch to Jason stays greyed out, even though the next story mission needs him, so the story can't continue ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-missing-persons-softlock-glitch-838670-20261123)). No story spoilers below.

## Did the patch fix it?

**No.** Update 01.002.002 arrived on 23 November with seven fixes, including stuck story phone calls, but its notes don't mention this, and players are **still reporting the softlock after updating**. One of GAMINGbible's own writers is stuck ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-first-update-patch-notes-ps5-xbox-seven-fixes-442192-20261123)). Rockstar hasn't commented. [What the patch did fix →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

## How to avoid it

- **Skip Missing Persons while switching is restricted.** Early in Chapter 3 you're held on Jason for a while but can still free-roam. Players say most other side content is safe to do as Jason then ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-missing-persons-softlock-glitch-838670-20261123)). Come back to it once you can swap freely.
- **Make a manual save before you start it,** in a slot you won't overwrite. [How to save by hand →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)

## Already stuck?

- **No reliable fix yet.** One player says restarting the console, reloading the save and dying didn't bring the switch back ([Gizmo Times](https://www.gizmotimes.com/gaming/gta-6-cannot-switch-jason-missing-persons-softlock/54385)).
- **Load an earlier save** from before the mission. It's the only way out reported so far, and it can cost a few hours ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-missing-persons-softlock-glitch-838670-20261123)).
- **Or wait for a patch** and report it to Rockstar Support, noting the last point where switching still worked ([Gizmo Times](https://www.gizmotimes.com/gaming/gta-6-cannot-switch-jason-missing-persons-softlock/54385)). Don't delete your save or reinstall: nobody has shown that helps.
