Waiting for Black Friday (Friday, 27 November) to buy GTA 6 cheaper? Don't. **GTA 6 has no Black Friday sale** from Rockstar, PlayStation or Xbox, [Beebom reported on 25 November](https://beebom.com/gta-6-black-friday-sale-2026-deals/). The only discounts it found were from a few UK retailers, which don't help buyers in India.

## What GTA 6 costs in India

- **PlayStation Store India:** ₹5,999 Standard, ₹7,499 Ultimate, as [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/photo/gta-6-arrives-at-midnight-on-19-november-india-price-preload-and-editions-565630-2026-11-04) reported. [Price and where to buy →](/gta-6-price-in-india/)
- A "Black Friday GTA 6 key" far below that price is a red flag. Buy from the PS Store, Microsoft Store or a known retailer. [Spot the scams →](/news/gta-6-free-download-apk-scam-warning/)

## What is cheaper: older Rockstar games

Sony's PlayStation Black Friday sale started on 16 November, [Digit reports](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/playstation-black-friday-deals-2026-gta-v-to-rdr-2-5-sony-ps5-games-that-you-should-grab-ahead-of-gta-6-launch.html). These are the Rockstar games it listed, with the sale prices Digit gave:

- **GTA V and Great White Shark Card bundle:** ₹1,899 (was ₹3,799).
- **Red Dead Redemption 2:** ₹1,149.
- **Red Dead Redemption:** ₹1,519 (was ₹3,799).
- **GTA: The Trilogy, Definitive Edition:** ₹1,319.

Prices can change before the sale ends, so check the PS Store before you pay. Digit doesn't list any PS5 console offer.

## PS5s are selling out abroad too

GTA 6 has emptied PS5 shelves in the US a week after launch: **Amazon and Sony's own PlayStation Direct store are out of stock**, and there are **no official Black Friday discounts on the PS5 or PS5 Pro** in the US this year, [Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/ps5-sold-out-gta-6-black-friday-2026/). Notebookcheck says stock is also short in parts of Europe, including the UK, and in **Singapore**, with some sellers listing consoles above the normal price ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-release-date-causes-mass-PS5-console-shortage-before-Black-Friday-sales.1459434.0.html)). Neither report covers India. If you're buying here, pay the listed price from Sony Center, Croma, Vijay Sales, Amazon or Flipkart, and walk away from sellers asking for more.

## Our take

If you already own GTA 6, RDR2 at about ₹1,150 is the pick for a second Rockstar game. If you haven't bought GTA 6 yet, waiting for a sale won't save you anything this week. Need the console too? PS5 stock is still tight. [The numbers →](/news/gta-6-india-ps5-demand-vijay-sales-amazon-searches/)
