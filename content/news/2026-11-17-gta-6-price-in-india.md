GTA 6 is listed on **PlayStation Store India** at:

- **Standard Edition:** ₹5,999
- **Ultimate Edition:** ₹7,499

Those are the listings reported by Business Today. Prices can change and sales happen, so check the store before you pay.

## Already bought Standard?

A guide from GetPC.co.in reports an **Ultimate Edition Upgrade at ₹1,500**, so you can add the extras later instead of paying the full Ultimate price now. Those extras unlock chapter by chapter in the story anyway: [what unlocks when](/news/gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses-unlock-chapters/).

## Buying the box? Read this first

Business Today reports the **boxed version contains a download code, not a disc**. Same game, same download, so it mostly comes down to where you get the better deal.

## The full bill if you don't own a PS5 yet

Business Today spotted these Amazon India prices on 28 October:

- **PS5 Slim Digital (1TB):** ₹64,990
- **PS5 Slim Disc:** ₹69,985

**PS5 Slim Digital + GTA 6 Standard = about ₹70,989.** Console prices move a lot during sales, so treat that as a ballpark.
