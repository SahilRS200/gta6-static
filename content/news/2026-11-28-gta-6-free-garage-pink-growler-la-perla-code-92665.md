Garages are the only property you can buy in GTA 6, but players have found one you **don't have to pay for**, with a **pink sports car** already parked inside. It's hidden in La Perla, Vice City, behind a keypad code, and the code is waiting on a laptop at home. Found by players and reported by [Generación Xbox](https://generacionxbox.com/gta-6-esconde-un-deportivo-rosa-y-un-garaje-gratis-en-vice-city-asi-puedes-conseguirlos/) and [GameGPU](https://en.gamegpu.com/news/igry/igroki-obnaruzhili-v-gta-6-sekretnyj-rozovyj-sportkar-i-besplatnyj-garazh-v-rajone-la-perla). No story spoilers.

## When you can get it

- **From Chapter 3** of the story. Both outlets place it there.
- **Do it before you finish Chapter 3**, GameGPU advises, because it may be missable after that.
- **Save by hand first.** [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)

## Step by step

1. **Unlock the laptop.** In Jason and Lucia's apartment, use the laptop on the bedroom desk. Per GameGPU, the password is **MuffinsQueen15**: the dog's name is on a framed photo near the kitchen, and the year is on a "Class of '15" drawing in the bedroom.
2. **Read the message.** Open the messaging app on the laptop. A message from an unknown sender mentions a garage on Del Rio Boulevard and gives the code **92665** (GameGPU).
3. **Go to La Perla.** The garage is near **Auntie's Place**, in a small group of buildings west of the main road through the area (Generación Xbox). GameGPU puts it in the south of La Perla, in a car park behind the buildings, across the street from the **Twa Ponya** tattoo parlour.
4. **Find the small grey garage with a pink bow** painted above its keypad and enter **92665**.

## What you get

- **A free extra garage** in Vice City. The ones you buy through the Real Estate app cost $4,000 to $40,000. [All garages and prices →](/news/gta-6-buy-property-garages-prices-real-estate-app/)
- **A pink Pfister Growler** called "Vanessa's Growler". GameGPU says you can register it as your car for **$29,450** or sell it for **$20,600**.

Registering keeps it safe if it's wrecked or impounded. [How registering cars works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/)
