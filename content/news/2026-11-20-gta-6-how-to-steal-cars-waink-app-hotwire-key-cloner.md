Walked up to a shiny sports car in GTA 6, pressed the button and got nowhere? That's by design. The one-press hotwire the series used for 20 years is gone: Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told IGN that parked cars now come in layers of security, and some can't be taken until you've progressed in the story and have the right tool ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Rockstar-is-ending-one-button-car-theft-in-GTA-6.1381985.0.html)). Here's how it works on PS5 and Xbox. No story spoilers.

## Step by step

1. **Scan it with Waink.** Hold **Up** on the D-pad to open the Waink phone app, then hold **R1 / RB** to scan the car ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-car-stealing-guide-how-to-steal-sell-keep-vehicles)). It shows whether the car is locked, any alarm or tracker, which tool you need, its resale value and what it costs to register as yours ([Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/gta/news/gta-6-car-theft-security-tiers-waink-scanner-tools)).
2. **Try the door** with **Triangle / Y**. If it's locked, **smash the window** with **Square / X**. That can set off an alarm.
3. **Hotwire it:** fill the semi-circles on screen with **both sticks**.
4. **Deal with the tracker**, if it has one, and drive off.
5. **Sell it or register it** at a Pay 'n' Spray ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-car-stealing-guide-how-to-steal-sell-keep-vehicles)).

## The three kinds of security

- **Locked:** break in, then hotwire.
- **Alarm:** smashing the window can trigger it and draw attention.
- **Keyless:** modern cars with immobilisers can't be hotwired at all. You need a **key cloner**, which you only get later in the story ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-car-stealing-guide-how-to-steal-sell-keep-vehicles), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Rockstar-is-ending-one-button-car-theft-in-GTA-6.1381985.0.html)).

A car someone is **driving** is still the old way: pull the driver out and go.

## Watch the tracker

- **Standard tracker:** you can't modify the car until it's registered to you.
- **Advanced tracker** (common on expensive cars): it **alerts the police** and keeps sending your location **unless you're driving fast** ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-car-stealing-guide-how-to-steal-sell-keep-vehicles)).

If the police recognise your car, switch cars or get a respray. In a chase, an unlocked old car beats a locked supercar, because alarms and keyless locks cost you time. And check the back seat: one player stole a car with a cat inside, and it went for Jason ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)).
