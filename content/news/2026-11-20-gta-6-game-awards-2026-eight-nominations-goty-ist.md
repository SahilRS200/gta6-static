GTA 6 has been out for a day and it is already up for **eight awards at The Game Awards 2026**, including **Game of the Year**. The nominations were announced on 17 November, two days before launch ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-is-the-most-nominated-game-at-the-game-awards-2026/), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/entertainment/game-of-the-year-9.7425430)).

## GTA 6's eight nominations

- Game of the Year
- Best Game Direction
- Best Narrative
- Best Art Direction
- Best Score and Music
- Best Audio Design
- Best Action/Adventure
- Best Performance — **Manni L. Perez as Lucia**

TrueAchievements and CNBC also count eight ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/news/gta-6-leads-the-game-awards-2026-with-eight-nominations), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/gta-vi-launch-streaming-content-economy.html)).

## Who it's up against for Game of the Year

There are **six** nominees this year: GTA 6, *007 First Light*, *Control Resonant*, *Phantom Blade Zero*, *Resident Evil Requiem* and *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time*. VGC lists *Control Resonant* and *Resident Evil Requiem* with eight nominations each too, so GTA 6 is not alone at the top ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-is-the-most-nominated-game-at-the-game-awards-2026/)).

## When to vote and watch, in IST

- **Fan voting** is open at thegameawards.com until **9 December, 6 PM PT — 7:30 AM IST on 10 December**. Fans' votes count for **10%**; the jury decides the other 90% ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-is-the-most-nominated-game-at-the-game-awards-2026/)).
- **The show** is on **Thursday 10 December at 5 PM PT**, which is **6:30 AM IST on Friday 11 December**. It streams free on YouTube and other platforms.
