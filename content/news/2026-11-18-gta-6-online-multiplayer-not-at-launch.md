If you were planning to jump into GTA 6 with your squad at **12:00 AM IST**, hold that thought. **GTA 6 launches as a single-player game.** There is no online multiplayer mode on day one, and Rockstar has not announced a date for one.

## What's confirmed

- **Story mode only at launch.** Rockstar has described GTA 6 as a solo experience and has not referred to any form of online play, and the online component will not be available at first ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/psa-gta-6-does-not-have-online-multiplayer-at-launch)).
- **No GTA 6 Online date.** Nothing official yet. Push Square expects an updated GTA Online some time in 2027, but that is the outlet's expectation, not a Rockstar announcement.

## What the files hint at (not confirmed)

Dataminers digging through the preload found references to multiplayer features, including session-joining and "multiplayer activity" entries for the PS5 activity cards. One also suggested the install is split so story mode and other content could be separate downloads ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-datamine-reveals-multiplayer-references/)). These are interpretations of files, not features, and multiplayer is not expected to be playable at launch.
