Before launch it was only a rumour that rapper **Freddie Gibbs** would turn up in GTA 6 ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/grand-theft-auto-6-rapper-freddie-gibbs-soundtrack-mission-3939305)). He does: he plays a **doomsday prepper** who has walled himself into a fortified compound, and breaking in pays out in cash or a free SUV ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-find-break-in-freddie-doomsday-compound-grand-theft-auto/)). **No story spoilers.**

**Update, 26 November:** this side mission is also how you **unlock hurricanes**. Once you finish the second break-in, hurricane weather is switched on for the rest of your save and can then roll in at random like any other weather, [TheGamer reports](https://www.thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-hurricane-weather-storm-unlock-freddie-gta-guide/). Until you do it, you won't see one.

## Where it is

- **North shore of Lake Leonida, North Leonard County,** on the same road as One-Eyed Willie's Auto Shop. You can't miss it: electric fences and end-of-the-world billboards ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-find-break-in-freddie-doomsday-compound-grand-theft-auto/)).
- **Watch for proximity mines and drones** inside the fence. Use **Focus** to spot the mines, and when you hear a drone, Focus gives you time to shoot it down: [how Focus works](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/).

## First break-in: the water tower

1. Go to the **water tower at the back**, outside the electric fence, and climb its ladder.
2. **Jump from the platform onto the shed roof** inside the fence.
3. Hug the fence heading **east, then south**. Walk on the **pipes and cables**: there are no mines on them.
4. Reach the **bunker's front door**. Freddie lets you go and tells you to come back later ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-find-break-in-freddie-doomsday-compound-grand-theft-auto/)).

**Another way in:** TheGamer's route uses the **bulldozer up the hill to shove a shipping container off a hole**, then the **sewers** under the fence. Drones will try to ram you, so carry healing items. Freddie texts you about **2–3 in-game days** later; the second visit won't start without that text ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-hurricane-weather-storm-unlock-freddie-gta-guide/)).

## Second break-in: build a ramp

Some time later Freddie **texts you for a rematch**. The tower route is blocked and the mines and drones are tougher.

1. Go to the **north-west corner**, where a **tractor with a big trailer** and a **bulldozer** are parked.
2. Park the tractor and trailer **along the fence**, then use the bulldozer to **lift the trailer** into a ramp.
3. Climb the tractor, step onto the trailer and **go over the fence**, then take the same mine-free route to the door ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-find-break-in-freddie-doomsday-compound-grand-theft-auto/)).

## Cash or car?

A storm rolls in and you pick one:

- **Leave:** Freddie hands you **$6,250**.
- **Stay:** you get a **Benefactor Dubsta**, which you can register and keep ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-find-break-in-freddie-doomsday-compound-grand-theft-auto/)).

TheGamer describes the leave option a little differently: a little over $6,000 in cash, plus Freddie's Benefactor, which you can **sell for $13,000** or register. The **hurricane warning** comes on at this point, and from then on storms are part of your game ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-hurricane-weather-storm-unlock-freddie-gta-guide/)). Guides disagree on some details, such as which corner the water tower is in, so trust what you see on the map.
