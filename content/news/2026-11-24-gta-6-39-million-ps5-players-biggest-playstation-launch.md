Sony has put a number on GTA 6's first weekend. **More than 39 million different people played Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 between launch on 19 November and 22 November**, Sony Interactive Entertainment said on 23 November, and it calls this **"the biggest launch in PlayStation history"**, across three decades and five console generations ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-for-ps5-tops-39-million-players), [PlayStation on X](https://x.com/PlayStation/status/2125009125322957131)).

## The numbers

- **39 million+ unique players on PS5** in four days ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-for-ps5-tops-39-million-players)).
- **683 million hours played**, an average of **17.4 hours per player** ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/40-million-gta-6-players-ps5/)).
- **The most people ever playing one game at the same time on PlayStation**, beating earlier records set by multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player games, Sony says. It didn't give the peak figure ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-for-ps5-tops-39-million-players)).
- **70% of PS5s bought since launch played GTA 6 first**, and launch brought the biggest single-day jump in newly connected PS5s outside the year-end holidays ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-for-ps5-tops-39-million-players)).

## Read it with care

- **Players aren't copies sold.** One copy on a shared console can count more than one player, so this isn't a sales figure ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/40-million-gta-6-players-ps5/)). The only official sales number is still Take-Two's **$3.79 billion** in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before launch. [The official sales →](/news/gta-6-official-sales-3-79-billion-day-one-record/) Analysts at Alinea separately estimate about 44.9 million copies sold in the same four days. [The estimate →](/news/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-4-days-alinea-estimate/)
- **It's PS5 only.** Microsoft hasn't shared an Xbox player count ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/40-million-gta-6-players-ps5/)).
- **There's no India figure.** Sony didn't break the count down by country.
