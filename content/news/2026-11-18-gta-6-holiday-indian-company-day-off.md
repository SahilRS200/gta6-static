Planning to call in "sick" on Thursday? Some Indian players won't need to. GTA 6 unlocks in India at **12:00 AM IST on 19 November**, and at least one Indian company has made the day official.

## An Indian firm made 19 November a holiday

Interior design company **Inde by ChairsCarte** has declared **Thursday 19 November a company-wide holiday**. It covers **employees, interns and freelancers**, [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/gaming-news/gta-6-launch-this-indian-firm-declares-november-19-holiday-for-employees-interns-and-freelancers-114940.htm) reports. The founder announced the day off on Instagram. The firm said it decided after an **unusually high number of leave requests** tied to the game's release ([NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/this-indian-company-is-offering-leave-to-play-gta-6/story)).

## Logitech's version is more flexible

Logitech has announced **"Logi Game Day"**: one paid day off for each of its roughly **7,000 employees** worldwide to play games. The first falls on 19 November, but staff can take it any time up to November 2027, and Logitech's offices stay open on launch day ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114639/logitech-is-giving-all-of-its-employees-the-day-off-to-play-grand-theft-auto-6/index.html), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Logitech-is-letting-its-staff-take-a-day-off-just-to-play-GTA-6.1447714.0.html)).

## No day off? Plan your night

Most of us still have work or college on Thursday. The unlock is at midnight, so finish the pre-load and patches before you sleep, and you can play a few hours tonight or straight after work.
