Yes, you can slap people in GTA 6. It's a light tap, not a full punch, and it has a cost if you pick on random strangers. Here's how it works, per [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-to-slap-in-gta-6/), which lists PS5 buttons. No story spoilers below.

## How to slap

- **Walk right up to an NPC** and **lightly tap Square** on PS5.
- **Distance matters.** The slap only lands at the right range, so it can take a few tries.

## Slap without hurting your Criminal Profile

Slapping civilians for no reason **lowers your Criminal Profile**, the rating that tracks how reckless Jason and Lucia are. VICE's way round it:

1. **Hold L2** to target the NPC.
2. Pick **Insult** or **Provoke**.
3. **Wait for them to come at you**, then tap Square.

Because they started it, the slap doesn't count against you. [How the Criminal Profile works →](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/)

## Lucia's leg choke

Lucia has a move Jason can't do:

1. **Hold R2** to grab an NPC.
2. **Hold the left stick down and tap Square.**
3. **Keep tapping Square** once her legs lock round their neck.

If she starts punching instead, hold the left stick down the moment she grabs, or step back and grab again with R2.
