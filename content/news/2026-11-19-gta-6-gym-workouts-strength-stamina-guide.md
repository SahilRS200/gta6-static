Yes, you can work out in GTA 6, and it's one of the systems players are talking about most on day one. Engadget's team spent a good part of launch morning in the gym: the bench press and deadlift use the **DualSense triggers and vibration**, kettlebell swings follow a rhythm, and gains **show on the character's body**; skip training and they look less toned ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)). No story spoilers below.

## What training does

- **Three stats.** Strength and cardio go up with workouts; focus is raised through other activities. Cardio helps with sprinting and other hard physical work ([PlayStationInfo](https://www.playstationinfo.de/en/2026/11/19/gta-6-gym-the-best-exercises-for-strength-and-stamina/)).
- **It changes how they look.** Working out makes Jason and Lucia stronger and more muscular ([GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/gyms-and-workouts-in-gta-vi-848e)).
- **It's optional.** Apart from a few story activities, you don't need it to finish the story ([PlayStationInfo](https://www.playstationinfo.de/en/2026/11/19/gta-6-gym-the-best-exercises-for-strength-and-stamina/)).
- **Stats can drop.** Go a long time without training and strength and cardio slip back, so keep up a routine.

## Where to train

- **Free, at home.** Jason's safehouse in Key Lento has kettlebells, a bench press, deadlifts, pull-ups and squats on the back deck. Take the heavy bag from the shed underneath and hang it under the house: Shacknews calls it the fastest cardio option ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151398/working-out-gta6)).
- **Outdoors.** Running, cycling, swimming and kayaking also count as cardio.
- **Gyms.** They have more equipment, with treadmills and exercise bikes the best cardio. Most need a membership, bought at the front desk on your first visit; Jason and Lucia share it, so you pay once. Going together also counts as time spent on their relationship.

## The quickest exercises

- **Kettlebells** are the most efficient, thanks to an easy, steady rhythm. **Bench press** and **deadlifts** are also quick because they need few reps.
- **Slow ones:** leg press, cable machine and tyre flipping need precise timing or take longer ([PlayStationInfo](https://www.playstationinfo.de/en/2026/11/19/gta-6-gym-the-best-exercises-for-strength-and-stamina/)).
- **A simple routine:** three strength exercises and one or two cardio ones are enough ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151398/working-out-gta6)).

## Get better gains

- **Rest and eat first.** Training tired or hungry gives weaker results.
- **Protein bars and shakes** speed up recovery, and a drink of water before a workout gives a short stamina boost.
- **Check your phone's fitness app** for training status, hunger and body composition.

**Bonus:** the Body Armor cheat needs 100 workouts once the cheats unlock after the story, so your gym time counts later ([how cheats unlock](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/)).
