Three creators from **S8UL**, one of India's biggest gaming collectives, have shared their first verdicts on GTA 6 after launch day, [myKhel reports](https://www.mykhel.com/more-sports/gta-6-indian-creators-s8ul-first-impressions-459517.html). The short version: they think the 13-year wait was worth it. Nothing below spoils the story.

## What they said

- **Soul Aman (Aman Jain):** the realism of Leonida, with its detailed people and activities, is the biggest highlight for him. He listed decorating your home, reading, going to the gym, flying planes and fishing as things to do, and said the wait was "absolutely worth it", though the amount of content can feel overwhelming at times.
- **Pitaji Playz (Sunny Jha):** liked the freedom to tackle situations your own way, from planning a robbery to escaping the police. He says the **cops are smarter and harder to shake** than before, and the **combat feels like Red Dead Redemption 2**. He has no complaints yet, but says he hasn't finished even 5% of the game, so it's too early for a final verdict.
- **Dattrax Gaming (Harvest Dewangan):** called GTA 6 "a significant step forward" for the industry, praising the world detail, how characters react to you and the freedom you get. He thinks it could shape how future open worlds are made, but grumbled that players had to wait so long.
