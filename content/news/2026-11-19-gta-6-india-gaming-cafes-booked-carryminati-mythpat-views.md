**Update, 21 November:** two of the three most-viewed GTA 6 launch streams on YouTube anywhere in the world are Indian. Streams Charts' list, counted up to 20 November, puts **CarryMinati's CarryisLive stream second with about 17 million (1.7 crore) views** and **Mythpat third with about 15.7 million (1.57 crore)**. Only IShowSpeed, at about 65.1 million, is ahead; Kai Cenat is fourth at about 12.1 million. **Live Insaan and GamerFleet** also made the global top 20 ([Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/gta-6s-most-viewed-youtube-streams)). These are total views of each first broadcast, not people watching at once.

GTA 6 is console-only, and in India that has sent a lot of players to gaming cafes instead of the electronics store. Reuters spoke to more than a dozen gamers at cafes in Delhi, Bengaluru and Srinagar on launch day, 19 November ([Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/articles/indias-gamers-skip-crowd-cafes-115003836.html)).

## Cafes are filling up

- **Delhi:** Gaming Mania's co-owner told Reuters she took 40 to 50 calls in a day, including children asking to play, and that GTA slots are **pre-booked through Sunday**, even after a small price rise.
- **Bengaluru:** an IT professional took the day off to play on a 100-inch projector screen with surround sound at a cafe. Another Bengaluru cafe owner said much of the rush is about being seen playing it first.
- **Srinagar:** Reuters also reported from a gaming cafe there.
- **The price:** cafes charge from about **₹250 an hour**, far cheaper than a PS5, which is hard to find right now anyway ([PS5 stock in India](/news/gta-6-india-ps5-demand-vijay-sales-amazon-searches/)).

## Indian creators are pulling huge numbers

**CarryMinati and Mythpat each streamed GTA 6 for about 10 hours**, and each stream had passed **13 million (1.3 crore) views** by Thursday afternoon, Reuters says. Those are total views, not people watching at once; Mythpat's live peak on launch night was about 1.25 lakh ([streaming record](/news/gta-6-streaming-record-twitch-5-million-viewers/)).

**Update, 20 November:** Mythpat's stream, "Welcome to Vice City! GTA 6 LIVE with Mythpat", has reportedly passed **1.5 crore views** in about 24 hours ([Inshorts, citing News Karnataka](https://inshorts.com/en/news/mythpat-s-gta-6-livestream-crosses-1-5-crore-views-1795150597571)).

Reuters also notes that PM Narendra Modi's GTA-style "India is playing a whole new game!" post joined the buzz ([our story](/news/gta-6-pm-modi-post-india-playing-whole-new-game/)).

## Going to a cafe? Quick tips

- **Call ahead.** At least one Delhi cafe is fully booked till Sunday, so don't just walk in.
- **Don't play on someone else's save.** Make your own save slot and save manually before you leave ([how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)).
- **Sign out** of any PSN or Xbox account you use there.
