Hours after GTA 6 unlocked in India at **12:00 AM IST**, the game turned up somewhere nobody expected: the Prime Minister's X account. On launch day, **Narendra Modi posted "India is playing a whole new game!"** with a graphic styled like GTA cover art, marking 2013 with the GTA V look and today with GTA VI, reports [Deccan Chronicle, citing ANI](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/india-playing-a-whole-new-game-modi-uses-gta-6-imagery-to-take-dig-at-upa-2005789).

## Update: the memes, and the backlash

Replies turned the post into a meme. Some users called Modi the "coolest Prime Minister ever"; others asked for a **"Har Ghar PS5 Yojana"** (a PS5 for every home) and a national holiday to play GTA 6, reports [Eastern Eye](https://www.easterneye.biz/modi-india-playing-new-game-post-memes-political-backlash/).

Not everyone laughed. Congress communications chief **Jairam Ramesh** called the post a "desperate bid to reach out to the youth" and pointed out that the games' heroes are criminals, adding "How very appropriate", as reported by [Eastern Eye](https://www.easterneye.biz/modi-india-playing-new-game-post-memes-political-backlash/).

## What the post compares

The 13-year gap between GTA V (2013) and GTA 6 is used as a "then vs now" frame for government figures. As reported by ANI, the post lists:

- **Extreme poverty:** 27% then, 2.6% now
- **Price of 1 GB of mobile data:** ₹308 in the 3G era, ₹7.51 now
- **Railway electrification:** 20% then, 99.6% now
- **Solar capacity:** 2.82 GW then, 175 GW now
- **Sanitation coverage:** 39% then, 100% now
- **Naxal-affected districts:** 182 then, 0 now

These are the government's own figures as stated in the post; we have not checked them independently. Several outlets, including [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/india-playing-a-whole-new-game-modi-uses-gta-6-imagery-to-take-dig-at-upa-2005789), read it as a dig at the previous UPA government. The post says nothing about the game itself; it borrows the style ([Daily Jagran](https://www.thedailyjagran.com/india/news/pm-modi-joins-gta-6-craze-highlights-how-india-has-transformed-between-gta-v-and-gta-vi-10336837)).

## The Bengaluru connection

BJP MP Tejasvi Surya also pointed to India's part in the game, saying more than 1,600 developers at **Rockstar India's Bengaluru studio** worked on a significant part of GTA 6, reports [India.com](https://www.india.com/news/india/india-playing-whole-new-game-pm-modi-turns-gta-6-buzz-into-political-attack-targets-opposition-with-then-vs-now-stats-8546144/).
