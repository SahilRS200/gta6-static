GTA 6's strangest side secret so far is a **night-time trip to Hell**, with **Satan as a boss** and a unique shotgun as the prize. It's a hidden side encounter, not a story mission, and you can do it long before the credits. **No story spoilers.**

## Before you go

- **You need Chapter 2.** GAMINGbible says it opens once Port Gellhorn does, in Chapter 2 ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-secret-hell-mission-satan-location-rewards-623661-20261123)). You can do it as Jason or Lucia: [how switching works](/news/gta-6-how-to-switch-between-jason-and-lucia/).
- **Go at night.** The encounter only appears after dark; players suggest **midnight to 4 AM** in-game. If the garage is shut, come back later ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-secret-hell-mission-satan-location-rewards-623661-20261123), [The Based Otaku](https://thebasedotaku.com/guides/gta-6-hell-and-satan-boss-easter-egg-walkthrough/)). Skip time in a safehouse bed: [how sleeping works](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/).
- **Where:** a house with an **open garage in northern Port Gellhorn**, Kelly County, north of the Stash House illegal operation ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-secret-hell-mission-satan-location-rewards-623661-20261123)). The UFO event is in the same area: [UFO guide](/news/gta-6-ufo-location-truth-is-out-there-trophy/).

## Step by step

1. **Follow the music** from the garage and find the two men there.
2. **Talk to them** until "Having Fun?" appears, pick it, then answer **"It's Good"** about the music.
3. **Get in the van.** They climb into a van; interact with its back doors to follow.
4. **At the campfire,** when you're told to look into the fire, hold **L2 / LT**. You're sent to Hell ([The Based Otaku](https://thebasedotaku.com/guides/gta-6-hell-and-satan-boss-easter-egg-walkthrough/)).

## How to beat Satan

- **Round 1:** his health bar starts blacked out. Shoot the exploding enemies and animals coming at you until the bar **turns red**, then shoot Satan. Repeat.
- **You will die.** That's scripted. Back at the campsite, go to the big throne and eat to return.
- **Round 2 is fists only,** and you die again. **Round 3** works like round 1 ([The Based Otaku](https://thebasedotaku.com/guides/gta-6-hell-and-satan-boss-easter-egg-walkthrough/)).

## The rewards

- **Hellspawn Comshot 12,** a unique shotgun found by the throne after the fight.
- **A Circles of Hell top** and a **new tattoo design**.
- **A permanent max-health increase.** Guides agree it exists, but none gives the exact amount, and GAMINGbible says it hasn't been independently verified ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-secret-hell-mission-satan-location-rewards-623661-20261123), [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/gta-6-secret-satan-boss-guide-how-unlock-permanent-hp-buff-legendary-hellspawn-shotgun)). Guides also differ on the clothing and tattoo, so expect small differences.
