**Short answer: press both sticks in together (L3 + R3 on PS5, left and right stick on Xbox) to use Focus.** It's GTA 6's take on a "Dead Eye"-style ability: the world slows, targets light up and a purple meter drains. It unlocks during a gunfight in an early Chapter 1 mission ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151394/focus-mode-unlock-gta6)). Spoiler-free.

## In a fight

- **Time slows** while the purple bar in the top-left drains, and it drains quickly ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-focus-mode-ability-explained/)).
- **Red highlights are lethal shots** (head and chest); **yellow are non-lethal** (arms and legs). Hit markers use the same colours: red for a kill, yellow for a takedown, white for a plain hit ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-focus-mode-ability-explained/)).
- **Aim yellow if you care about your rating.** Killing hurts your [Criminal Profile](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/) more than disarming. At the cleaner levels, stealth and non-lethal takedowns charge Focus; at the darker ones, headshots do ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-criminal-profile-levels-explained)).
- **Shoot the car, not the driver:** Focus marks tyres, the engine and the fuel tank ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-focus-mode-ability-explained/)).

## Outside a fight

- **Doors:** white means you can walk in, red means it's alarmed ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-beginners-guide-6-tips-you-need-to-know-before-starting-gta-6/)). More in [which buildings you can enter](/news/gta-6-which-buildings-can-you-enter-interiors-map/).
- **Cops and loot:** in a chase it shows nearby officers on the minimap, and it highlights valuables worth taking ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-focus-mode-ability-explained/)).
- **Fish and animals:** it shows where fish are and helps track animals ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151394/focus-mode-unlock-gta6)). See the [fishing guide](/news/gta-6-fishing-how-to-fish-rod-controls-spots/).

## How to get more Focus

How long you can stay in Focus depends on your Focus training level ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151394/focus-mode-unlock-gta6)). Ways players have found to boost it:

- **Take a shower** at a safehouse ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)).
- **Sleep in a safehouse bed** for a short Focus buff ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-skip-time-and-sleep-in-gta-6-day-length-and-best-time-for-crimes/)). [How sleep works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/)
- **Feed Dolores,** Jason's pet snake, for an early boost ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/gaming/gta-6-6-gameplay-tricks-that-can-make-your-time-in-leonida-easier.html)).
- **Eat or drink items with the Focus icon** ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151394/focus-mode-unlock-gta6)). Beebom spotted a purple eye icon on the Blitz Smoothie in Jason's fridge, but hasn't confirmed what it does.

Exact meter lengths and refill rates aren't published by Rockstar yet.
