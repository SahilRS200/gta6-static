Treasure diving is one of the easiest early side activities in GTA 6: a boat, scuba gear and a metal detector, then shipwrecks full of doubloons you can sell for cash. Here is how to unlock it and how to clear a wreck without wasting a trip. Buttons are PS5 first, Xbox in brackets. No story spoilers beyond mission names.

## How to unlock it

- **Play Chapter 1 up to Island Dreams.** Jason meets Kenton, who runs a diving shop in Key Lento. The first job is taking a bachelor party out to a dive site, as [VGC's walkthrough](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-island-dreams-walkthrough/) describes.
- **After the mission, Kenton lends you his boat and gear.** He also sends the scuba gear, the Arras Bullhead speargun and the metal detector to a boat outside Jason's house, so changing boats doesn't cost you your kit ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-underwater-discoveries-treasure-sharks-guide/)).
- **Gear only stays on near water.** Spend too long on land and the scuba gear and metal detector unequip themselves (VGC).

## How to find treasure

- **Put the gear on at the back of the boat**, then dive with R1 (RB); click L3 (left stick) to swim faster.
- **Pick the metal detector** from the bottom-right slot of the weapon wheel and hold L2 (LT) to sweep the seabed. A **green flash** points towards treasure, and the pings get faster as you get closer.
- **Press Triangle (Y) at the "Search" prompt** to grab it. Sweep slowly and follow the strongest signal.
- **Watch the oxygen meter.** Diving is timed by your air supply ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/side-missions/gta-6/scuba-diving)).

## Six early wrecks around Key Lento

games.gg lists a starter route: Nuestra Señora del Ocio (10 doubloons), de la Noche (7 treasures), de la Ética Laxa (5), Urca de Cinco Torres (6), Nuestra Señora Lujuriosa (6) and Urca de Santa Sierra (6). Purple chest icons mark dive sites on the map, and Kenton's later jobs add more.

## Tips

- **Sharks are real.** Keep the Arras Bullhead for them: aim with L2 (LT) and fire. It has limited ammo, so don't waste it.
- **Sell to Kenton.** He pays cash for what you bring back, plus a bonus for a full set. Bank it, because [pocket cash is lost when you die](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/).
- **You can go back.** Every wreck can be revisited, so a missed item isn't lost (VGC).
- **It counts for 100%.** 100% completion needs all 33 underwater discoveries and five of Kenton's seven treasure sets (games.gg); it isn't time-limited (GTABase).
