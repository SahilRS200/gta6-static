GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight local time** in every region. That means the first players on Earth are in **New Zealand**, and they get in at **4:30 PM IST on Wednesday, 18 November** — a full **7 hours 30 minutes** before India's 12:00 AM IST unlock.

So yes, "early" is real. But most of the tricks doing the rounds on Instagram and YouTube don't work. Here's the honest breakdown.

## ❌ Myth: change your PS5 time zone to New Zealand

This is the one everyone shares, and it **does not work**. The PlayStation Store unlocks a game based on your **PSN account's region**, not your console's clock. Changing the time zone only changes the clock on your home screen.

Want to see how it's done anyway (and why it fails)? [Our PS5 time zone guide](/guides/ps5-time-zone/) walks through it.

## ❌ Myth: switch your Indian PSN account to New Zealand

PlayStation doesn't let you change the country or region of an existing PSN account. There's no setting for it.

## ⚠️ What some players actually do: a separate New Zealand PSN account

Launch reporting describes players creating a **second PSN account set to New Zealand** and buying GTA 6 there. It works because that account follows the NZ unlock time. Before you even think about it:

- You **buy the game again** on an account that isn't your main one. Your Indian purchase doesn't carry over.
- You need **New Zealand PSN store credit**, and grey-market sellers of "NZ wallet codes" are a common scam.
- Saves, add-ons and future GTA Online purchases live on **that** account.
- Giving false account details can break PlayStation's terms. It's your account on the line.

We don't recommend it, and we won't link sellers.

## 🎮 On Xbox?

Reports say Xbox lets you change the console's location (**Settings → System → Language & location**). Store and payment rules still apply, so read Xbox Support before you try it.

## ✅ The move that actually works

**Pre-load now** so the game is fully downloaded, then be on the couch at **11:59 PM IST on Wednesday**. And mute "GTA 6 ending" and "GTA 6 spoilers" on X and Instagram, because New Zealand gets a 7.5-hour head start.
