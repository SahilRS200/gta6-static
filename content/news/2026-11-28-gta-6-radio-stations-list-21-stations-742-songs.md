Long drives across Leonida get a lot better once you find your station. GTA 6 has **21 music radio stations with 742 songs between them**, going by Rockstar's in-game credits, plus **two talk stations** (The Wipe and WKTT) and **three bonus DJ shows** ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-radio-songs-list/)). **No story spoilers.**

That is a lot of music. VGTimes works it out at roughly **43 to 50 hours** of songs at an average song length, before the DJ shows. That's its own estimate, not a Rockstar number ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175880-gta-6-has-nearly-two-days-of-radio-with-742-tracks-across-21-stations.html)).

## Which station for which mood

Track counts and artists are from Vice's list of the credits ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-radio-songs-list/)):

- **Flash FM (80 songs):** the biggest station, pop across the decades: Daft Punk, Coldplay, Madonna, Dua Lipa, Frank Ocean.
- **Vice City FM (71):** '80s hits: Lionel Richie, Tina Turner, Simple Minds, Donna Summer, The Cure.
- **V-Rock (70):** rock: Tom Petty, Queen, Van Halen, AC/DC, Def Leppard.
- **Honey FM (52):** R&B and soul: Mariah Carey, Brandy, TLC, Barry White. It also carries the **Liquid City** show, with Craig Robinson as host and eight original songs.
- **Worldwide FM (41):** D'Angelo, Jamiroquai, Roberta Flack.
- **Dirty South Classics (39):** Southern rap: Lil Wayne, Outkast, Gucci Mane.
- **Leonida Jazz Radio (36):** Miles Davis, Grover Washington Jr.
- **Emotion 98.3 (35):** soft rock: Phil Collins, Genesis, Roxette.
- **The Chamber 106.6 (22):** metal: Metallica, Slayer, Black Sabbath, Megadeth.
- **Symphony FM (16):** classical.
- **The Vault Radio (24):** house and techno. This one is **hidden**; [unlock it with one poster scan →](/news/gta-6-secret-radio-station-the-vault-waink-poster/)

The rest: AfroBank FM (31), Stockyard FM (37), Cocoteo FM (30), CircoLoco Records Radio (27), Radio La Ola (25), Vice City Rap Radio (25), Back Country Radio (24), Gloriana Blues Radio (23), Kaleidoscope FM (18) and Radio On-U (16) ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-radio-songs-list/)).
