**Short answer: there is no GTA 6 PC release date.** GTA 6 launched on 19 November (12:00 AM IST in India) on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S only, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date. If you game on a PC, as a huge share of Indian players do, you are still waiting.

## What the PC crowd is doing meanwhile: playing GTA 5

- **GTA 5 on Steam went up, not down.** It hit a peak of about **87,000 players at once** in the day after GTA 6 launched, almost 10% higher than in October, PC Gamer reports citing SteamDB. The 13-year-old game usually averages about 40,000 to 50,000 players at a time ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-5s-steam-concurrents-have-gone-up-rather-than-down-now-that-gta-6-is-out-suggesting-a-lot-of-pc-players-are-waiting/)).
- PC Gamer reads that as a lot of PC players waiting for Rockstar rather than buying a console.

## The early-2027 report (not confirmed)

- **GamesBeat reported in October** that Rockstar plans to bring GTA 6 to PC in the **first quarter of 2027** (January to March), citing four industry sources who did not want to be named. Rockstar did not respond, and has not confirmed it ([GTA BOOM on the GamesBeat report](https://www.gtaboom.com/gamesbeat-reports-gta-6-will-hit-pc-in-early-2027)).
- **Treat it as reported, not announced.** PC Gamer notes Rockstar has in the past held PC versions back by at least seven months, so early 2027 would be unusually quick ([PC Gamer](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/gta-5s-steam-concurrents-gone-220043170.html)).

## What to do if you're on PC in India

- **That viral "GTA 6 running on PC" clip is fake.** A video on X claimed a fan-made PC port reached the title screen; XDA Developers says it stitched GTA 6's title screen onto older footage of GTA 5 on PC, and the account then asked for donations ([XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/that-viral-gta-6-pc-port-video-hoax-made-from-recycled-gta-5-footage/)).
- **Don't fall for "GTA 6 PC download" links.** Any PC download, crack or "port" today is fake and can carry malware ([how the scams work](/news/gta-6-free-download-apk-scam-warning/)).
- **Want to play now without a console?** Gaming cafes charge from about ₹250 an hour ([cafes and creators](/news/gta-6-india-gaming-cafes-booked-carryminati-mythpat-views/)), and the cheapest console route is an Xbox Series S ([compare costs](/news/gta-6-cheapest-console-india-xbox-series-s-vs-ps5-cost/)).
