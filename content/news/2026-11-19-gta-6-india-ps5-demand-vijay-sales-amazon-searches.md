GTA 6 has been playable in India since **12:00 AM IST today**, and Indian retailers say the game has been pulling people into buying a PS5 for weeks. The numbers come from retailers speaking to the media trade site BestMediaInfo ([BestMediaInfo](https://bestmediainfo.com/mediainfo/mediainfo-digital/gta-6-fever-grips-india-as-console-demand-and-creator-activity-surge-12819630)).

## What retailers are saying

- **Vijay Sales:** incoming PlayStation stock was **selling out within three to four days**. As supply improved, waiting periods came down to about **15–20 days**.
- **Amazon India:** PlayStation-related searches on the site **more than doubled over two weeks** in the run-up to launch.
- **Worldwide:** Sony says PS5 shipments passed **100 million** as of 10 November 2026.

TalkEsport repeats the same retailer figures and notes that the launch has run on Rockstar's global ads, with no India-specific campaign and no Hindi in the game ([TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/editorials/gta-6-india-marketing-launch-editorial/)). More on that: [GTA 6 in Hindi? →](/news/gta-6-hindi-language-support/)

- **Check stock before you go.** These are retailer comments, not Sony India sales data, and availability varies by city. A 15–20 day wait is possible if a store is out.
