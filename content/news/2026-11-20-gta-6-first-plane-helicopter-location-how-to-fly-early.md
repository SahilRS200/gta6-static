You don't have to wait for the story to hand you wings in GTA 6. Players have found a seaplane and helicopters within a short drive of the starting area, and you can simply take them. Here's where to go. No story spoilers.

## Southwind: a seaplane and a Maverick in one spot

- **Where:** Southwind, about **2.5 miles south-west of Jason's house in Key Lento**. Stay on the Sunbelt Highway (Route 1) the whole way ([Dork](https://readdork.com/features/where-steal-first-plane-helicopter-gta-6)).
- **The plane:** **Mammoth Dodo seaplanes** are usually parked in and around a small hangar by the **Sherwin Boatworks** car park, sometimes moored off the dock. A Dodo can take off from water or land and needs little room, so you can even drive it into the water and lift off from there.
- **The helicopter:** from the car park entrance, look east for an open gate to a dock with a **Buckingham Maverick**. Ignore the no-trespassing sign.
- **Heat:** nobody tends to hang around, so taking one there shouldn't draw attention ([Dork](https://readdork.com/features/where-steal-first-plane-helicopter-gta-6)).

## Option two: the guarded helipad near Hamlet

There's a private helipad **east of Hamlet, close to Key Lento**, behind a security checkpoint. Climb the chain-link fence at the side rather than walking past the guards, then move carefully to the helicopter. It isn't locked, and Game Rant's writer flew it away without picking up a wanted level ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-helicopter-location/)).

**Mile Zero Air Station** also has aircraft, but they are harder to get to ([Dork](https://readdork.com/features/where-steal-first-plane-helicopter-gta-6)).

## Get a parachute first (update, 21 November)

- **Buy one for $250** at any Ammu-Nation (the pink gun icon on the map): it's on the display near the body armour, or ask the clerk under **Equipment** ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-how-to-get-a-parachute/)).
- **Or grab one free.** Leonida has **25 Base Jump spots**, mostly rooftop helipads marked **H** in Vice-Dale County, and many have a parachute lying around. Doing the jump uses it up, so take the stairs down if you only want the item. Each base jump also counts toward the stunt total: see [GTA 6 stunt jumps and the Cunning Stunts trophy](/news/gta-6-stunt-jumps-locations-cunning-stunts-trophy/). There's another free one on the highest peak in **Mount Kalaga National Park** ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-how-to-get-a-parachute/)).
- **Equip it before you jump:** hold **L1 / LB** for the item wheel, switch with **R1 / RB**, then cycle the bottom-left slot with **R2 / RT** from body armour to the parachute. In freefall, press **Cross / A** to open it ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-how-to-get-a-parachute/)).

## Use it to clear the map fog (update, 21 November)

Most of Leonida's map starts hidden under fog. Nothing is locked: you can go anywhere once free roam opens in the first chapter, but the map only fills in where you've been. **Flying is the fastest way to clear it**, so a Southwind seaplane or helicopter doubles as a map-reveal tool. Skip the aircraft at **Mile Zero Air Station** if you want a quiet flight: taking one from the military base brings a much higher wanted level ([BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2322660/how-to-unlock-full-gta-6-map-guide/)).
