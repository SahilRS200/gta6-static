Seen a gorgeous GTA 6 PS5 Pro in a streamer's video and want one? You can't buy it. Rockstar's custom **GTA 6 PS5 Pro** was made in a run of about **100 units**, and they are being handed out, not sold, [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/100-limited-edition-GTA-6-PS5-Pro-consoles-released-by-Rockstar-but-retail-bundle-unlikely.1459750.0.html) and [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114892/rockstar-is-handing-out-100-of-these-gorgeous-limited-edition-gta-6-ps5-consoles-and-you-cant-buy-one/index.html) report.

## Who got one

- **Miami Heat players first.** Davion Mitchell showed his on Instagram on 18 November, a day before launch, and teammates Nikola Jović, Myron Gardner and Keshad Johnson posted theirs too ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-theme-exclusive-ps5-pro-revealed/)).
- **Then streamers and influencers.** A second batch is now reaching creators. It isn't clear whether these come from the same 100 or are extra units ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/100-limited-edition-GTA-6-PS5-Pro-consoles-released-by-Rockstar-but-retail-bundle-unlikely.1459750.0.html)).

## What's in the box

- A **dark grey PS5 Pro** with a palm-tree pattern, the GTA VI logo, neon stripes and a **serial number** on the front.
- **Two limited-edition DualSense controllers** and a copy of GTA 6, packed in a custom carrying case ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-theme-exclusive-ps5-pro-revealed/), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/tech-and-hardware/175761-rockstars-custom-gta-vi-ps5-pro-is-limited-to-just-100-units.html)).
- Nothing changes inside: it's a normal PS5 Pro, so GTA 6 runs exactly as it does on any PS5 Pro. [PS5 vs PS5 Pro performance →](/news/gta-6-ps5-vs-ps5-pro-xbox-performance-digital-foundry/)

Neither Sony nor Rockstar has announced a retail version, a matching console cover or a bundle, even as fans ask for one ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/100-limited-edition-GTA-6-PS5-Pro-consoles-released-by-Rockstar-but-retail-bundle-unlikely.1459750.0.html)). Treat any "GTA 6 PS5 Pro for sale" listing as a resale of a gifted unit at best, and a scam at worst.

## What you can buy in India: the GTA 6 DualSense

Sony's two **limited-edition GTA 6 DualSense controllers** (black and white) are a real retail product, launched with the game on 19 November at $84.99 in the US ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Sony-unveils-two-limited-edition-GTA-6-DualSense-controllers.1387582.0.html)).

- In India, **Vijay Sales lists the white edition at ₹7,490** (MRP, no discount). When we checked on 28 November, the page offered pre-booking rather than immediate purchase, so stock may be thin ([Vijay Sales](https://www.vijaysales.com/p/P261197/261197/sony-dualsenser-wireless-controller-grand-theft-auto-vi-white-limited-edition)).
