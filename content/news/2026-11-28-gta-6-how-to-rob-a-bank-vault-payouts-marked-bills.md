Store robberies pay pocket money. Banks are where GTA 6's big cash is, and the way you pay out is different: most of it comes as **marked bills** you have to sell. Here's when banks open up, which ones pay most and how to get out without a fight. All of it comes from [Screen Rant's bank guide](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-rob-banks-most-money-not-get-caught/), published 28 November. **No story spoilers.**

## When you can rob banks

- **Small banks:** after the Chapter 3 mission *Credit Check*.
- **Big vaults:** near the end of Chapter 4, through Yasmani's optional side mission *That's Where The Money Is…*.
- **Play the story first.** Early bank money comes as marked bills, so the payoff is better once you have the tools and a fence to sell to.

## Pick the right bank

Turn on the **Robberies filter** on the map: it shows each bank's **security level and likely payout**. Screen Rant counts **22 banks**: 14 small ones and 8 big ones with vaults you can get into.

Payouts, all in marked bills:

- **Lombank, Downtown** and **Lombank, Ocean Beach:** $135,000–$205,000 each, the biggest.
- **Fleeca, VCIA** and **Fleeca, Downtown:** $100,000–$150,000.
- **Shorefront, Ocean Beach:** $95,000–$140,000.
- **Small banks:** about $5,000–$45,000.

Take **both Jason and Lucia** on a big job: you carry more and it's easier to keep the staff and customers under control.

## The tools that make it work

- **Snap Gun** picks ordinary locked doors **without setting off the alarm**. It comes from one of Yasmani's jewellery jobs in Chapter 3.
- **Dye Pack Jammer** stops dye packs ruining the cash. It comes with *Credit Check*.
- **Focus** (L3 + R3 on PS5) highlights security gear, things you can use and clues to the safe code ([how Focus works](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/)).
- **Use the safe code, not explosives,** when you can: a blast destroys part of the money.

See when each tool unlocks in our [equipment list](/news/gta-6-equipment-list-all-12-tools-how-to-unlock/).

## Alarms, cameras and vaults

- Some banks let you **disarm security** and **destroy the camera footage**. That doesn't help if a staff member has already hit the alarm.
- **Forcing a keypad-locked door** in a higher-security bank sets off the alarm.
- **Vaults can't be opened with your normal kit.** You have to use the **security van** method from Yasmani's mission to reach an open vault.
- **Robbing a big vault always brings a four-star wanted level.** Plan the getaway before you go in.

## Getting away and cashing out

- **Want no police at all?** Stick to low-security banks. The big ones pay more but always bring a police response.
- **Watch the search circle on the minimap.** The police remember your car and your look, so change both: see our [wanted level guide](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/).
- **Killing people hurts your [Criminal Profile](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/).**
- **Marked bills can't be spent.** Sell them to a fence; **Yasmani in Little Cuba** gives the best rate.
