Parked your favourite car, got into a police chase and now it's gone? In GTA 6 your own cars aren't lost for good. How you get one back depends on how far into the story you are. No story spoilers below, only chapter numbers.

## Chapter 1: call Joey

Early in the game, your vehicle help comes from a mechanic, Joey, at **Voastal Auto Care** in the south of Key Lento. **Call Joey on your phone** and he delivers your vehicle to wherever you are, **repaired for free**. That works even if the car was completely wrecked ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-summon-personal-vehicles/)).

## Chapter 2 onward: the Scooter Bros app

Finishing a main mission in **Chapter 2** unlocks the **Scooter Bros** app on your phone ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-summon-personal-vehicles/)). It lets you:

- **Bring one of your personal vehicles to you** for a small fee. It turns up at your location straight away.
- **Repair** a vehicle you own ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)).
- **Get an impounded car back**, for a fee, without going to the police lot yourself. This needs the upgrade below.

Kotaku warns that the fees add up, and that **getting a car out of a police impound is expensive**.

## Is the $12,500 upgrade worth it?

The Scooter Bros upgrade costs **$12,500** in-game cash. It gives you **three free uses per in-game day** and adds impound recovery from the app ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-summon-personal-vehicles/), [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)). If you call your car several times a day, it pays for itself. Early on, when money is tight, spend on other things first. [How to make money fast →](/news/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast-early-game/)

## Save money on repairs and rides

- **Repair at a petrol pump.** Kotaku says it is often cheaper than paying Scooter Bros, and you can fill the tank in the same stop. [How refuelling works →](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/)
- **Need to cross the map fast?** The RydeMe app costs $100 a ride: call a driver, then focus on them in the car to skip the trip ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)).
- **Don't keep driving a car the cops know.** Switching cars helps you lose a wanted level, and Scooter Bros can bring yours to you once things calm down. [Wanted level explained →](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/)
