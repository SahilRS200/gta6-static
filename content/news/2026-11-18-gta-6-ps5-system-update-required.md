Playing on PS5 tonight? There's one more download, and this one isn't the game. **PlayStation Support has said a new PS5 system software update is required before you can play GTA 6**, [Insider Gaming reports](https://insider-gaming.com/playstation-confirms-latest-ps5-system-software-update-is-needed-to-play-gta-6/), and it asked players to install it before midnight on 19 November.

## The update

- **Version:** 26.07-14.23.00, released on 17 November.
- **Size:** about **1.3 GB**, [according to Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-launch-day-ps5-update/amp/).
- **Patch notes:** only "security fixes". Sony hasn't explained why GTA 6 needs it.
- **Pre-loaded already?** That doesn't cover you. The system update is separate from the game's own patches.

## How to install it on PS5 or PS5 Pro

1. Go to **Settings → System → System Software**.
2. Open **System Software Update and Settings**.
3. Select **Update System Software** and let the console restart.

It takes a few minutes on most Indian broadband connections, but do it **now**, not at 11:59 PM, when everyone else in India is hitting the same servers.

## Why the grumbling online

Some players are annoyed at a last-minute console update, and Xbox players got no equivalent, [autoevolution notes](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/sony-releases-mandatory-ps5-system-update-for-gta-6-players-triggers-public-backlash-278584.html). Many believe it is aimed at jailbroken consoles, but that is player speculation; Sony hasn't said so.
