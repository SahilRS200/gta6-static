Something you will run into in your first hours after the **12:00 AM IST** unlock: the fuel needle moves. Unlike earlier GTA games, **vehicles have a fuel tank that drains**, and if you ignore it, you stop. Here's how it works, without story spoilers.

## How long does a tank last?

Not long. Kotaku timed it and found vehicles go from full to empty in roughly **18 to 29 minutes** of driving, depending on the car and how you drive, reports [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2320980/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-cars-need-gas-tank-refuel/). For scale, Jalopnik says crossing all of Vice City takes about 15 minutes, so one tank covers a long trip but not a long session.

The game warns you twice: at **half a tank** and again when you're **nearly empty**. Nearby petrol pumps show up on your compass, and there are plenty of them ([Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2320980/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-cars-need-gas-tank-refuel/)).

## How to refuel

1. Pull up to a pump.
2. Press **R2 on PS5** (RT on Xbox).
3. Watch a short **10-second** fill-up scene. There's no pump mini-game.

**Electric cars** work the same way at charging points ([Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2320980/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-cars-need-gas-tank-refuel/), [The Autopian](https://www.theautopian.com/gta-6-finally-fixes-the-biggest-problem-the-cars-of-grand-theft-auto-games-have-always-had/)). Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson defended the system before launch: "Hey, that's pretty fast for a fill-up... You can't complain about that," as reported by [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/rockstar-says-gta-6s-controversial-gas-stops-take-just-10-seconds-you-cant-complain-3406246/).

## The catch: stolen cars and police chases

A car you steal keeps whatever fuel or charge it had. If it runs dry mid-chase, it **stalls** and you're on foot with cops behind you ([Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2320980/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-cars-need-gas-tank-refuel/)). Before a getaway, glance at the gauge. If you don't want to bother, the in-game **Scooter Bros** app can bring one of your own vehicles to you instead.
