Critics gave GTA 6 a **99 on Metacritic** ([our review roundup](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/)). A day after launch, players sound less united. No story spoilers below.

## New: the Metacritic user score opened at 5.1, now 4.9

By later on 21 November it had slipped to **4.9** from nearly **6,800** user ratings, while critics stay at **99** from 152 reviews ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175295-gta-6-user-score-falls-to-4.9-on-metacritic-as-critics-stay-positive.html)).

Metacritic has now switched on user ratings for GTA 6, and the PS5 user score sat at **5.1 out of 10** when TheGamer checked on 21 November (IST), with more than half of the reviews negative. Read it with care: TheGamer found many reviewers **admitting they haven't played the game**, plus politically charged rants and keyboard-mash reviews posted only to drag the number down. Mixed in are genuine complaints about console performance, the controls and missing series features. Critics, meanwhile, still sit at **99** on OpenCritic's top-critic average ([TheGamer](https://thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta-review-bombing-user-score)).

## The number: roughly half and half

A **PeakMetrics** analysis of launch-day posts on X, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Bluesky found **54.5% of opinionated posts favourable and 45.5% unfavourable**, Geeks + Gamers reports. That counts loud posters, not a survey of buyers, so read it as "the internet is split", not "half of players dislike it" ([Geeks + Gamers](https://geeksandgamers.com/gta-vi-players-roast-rockstar-as-glowing-critic-reviews-clash-with-fan-backlash/)).

## What players complain about

- **30fps on every console.** There is no 60fps mode, even on PS5 Pro. Players report drops into the low 20s in big firefights on PS5 ([Geeks + Gamers](https://geeksandgamers.com/gta-vi-players-roast-rockstar-as-glowing-critic-reviews-clash-with-fan-backlash/)). Kotaku's reviewer also noticed dips on crowded Vice City streets ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-the-kotaku-review-2000757842)). Our [stutter fix](/news/gta-6-stutter-frame-drops-ps5-fix-hdcp-dead-zone/) has the settings players say help.
- **Slow missions.** Long drives, long conversations and everyday chores (grilling, painting, mini-golf) have drawn jokey comparisons with *The Sims*.
- **Bugs.** Clipping, crashes and physics glitches; see our [launch bugs tracker](/news/gta-6-bugs-glitches-launch-falling-through-map/).

## The loudest review: 2/10

One X user who said he played early gave it **2/10** and called it the "biggest disappointment in gaming history", while praising the graphics. Many replies pushed back that a few hours is too little to judge a story [of about 50 hours](/news/gta-6-how-long-to-beat-story-hours/) ([IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/gta-6-early-review-backlash-fans-1833257)). IBTimes says it could not verify his account.

## What the fans like

Kotaku's reviewer found the companion hints natural rather than hand-holding, and many players defend the trade: a slower frame rate for a world this dense ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-the-kotaku-review-2000757842)). The Statesman's roundup notes that even reviews calling it a masterpiece repeat a few complaints, 30fps among them ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/gta-6-reviews-critics-call-it-a-masterpiece-but-here-are-the-complaints-almost-every-review-keeps-repeating-1503664147.html/amp)).

**Rockstar hasn't responded** to the performance or mission complaints.

In India, three S8UL creators are firmly on the positive side so far ([their first impressions](/news/gta-6-first-impressions-indian-creators-s8ul/)).
