Sam Houser almost never speaks in public. As GTA 6 rolls out around the world, the Rockstar co-founder and president has put out a **rare launch statement** welcoming players to Leonida, reported by [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/as-gta-6-finally-releases-rockstar-boss-sam-houser-issues-rare-statement-we-are-incredibly-grateful-and-look-forward-to-many-more-years-of-sharing-these-experiences-with-you/), [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/rockstar-co-founder-sam-houser-releases-statement-on-gta-vi/) and [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/news/gta-6-launch-rare-statement-from-rockstar-co-founder-sam-houser/).

## What he said

- **Why Rockstar exists.** Houser goes back to the studio's founding idea: that games can earn the same respect as other kinds of storytelling.
- **The promise.** He says the team tried to make something that will **"exceed your expectations"** and show what games as a medium can do.
- **Thanks, and the long view.** He thanks players and says Rockstar is **"incredibly grateful"** and looks forward to "many more years" of sharing these games ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/as-gta-6-finally-releases-rockstar-boss-sam-houser-issues-rare-statement-we-are-incredibly-grateful-and-look-forward-to-many-more-years-of-sharing-these-experiences-with-you/)).

## Why it's notable

Houser has given only a handful of interviews in Rockstar's history. Before launch, [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/rockstar-co-founder-sam-houser-pulls-out-of-gta-6-interview-ahead-of-launch/) reported that he pulled out of a planned GTA 6 magazine interview for personal reasons. So a written note at launch is the most fans have heard from him in years.

## What it does not confirm

The "many more years" line is **not an announcement** of GTA 6 Online, DLC or a PC version. Rockstar has not said anything new about those in the statement. If you see posts claiming Houser "confirmed" an online mode, treat them as speculation.

## For players in India

Nothing changes for us: GTA 6 unlocks at **12:00 AM IST tonight** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Critics agree with Houser's pitch so far: the game is at [99 on Metacritic](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/).
