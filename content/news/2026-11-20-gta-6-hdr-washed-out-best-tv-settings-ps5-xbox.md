Leonida is meant to look like a sunburnt postcard. If yours looks flat, grey or foggy, the culprit is almost always **HDR set up wrong somewhere between the console, the TV and the game.** Here's the order to fix it in. None of this needs a patch.

## 1. Set up the console first

- **PS5:** Settings → Screen and Video → Video Output. Set **HDR to "On When Supported"**, leave Resolution, 120 Hz Output, VRR and ALLM on Automatic, then run **Adjust HDR** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-best-tv-settings/)).
- **Xbox Series X/S:** Settings → General → TV & Display Options. Set 4K UHD, tick **Allow 4K and Allow HDR10**, turn on VRR and Auto Low Latency Mode if your TV supports them, keep Color Space on Standard, then run **Calibrate HDR for Games** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-best-tv-settings/)).

## 2. Then the TV

- Switch on **Game Mode** (LG calls it Game Optimizer). It cuts input lag and switches off picture processing that can wash out the image ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-best-tv-settings/)).
- **Turn off automatic or dynamic tone mapping,** especially on high-end sets such as LG OLEDs; Engadget's team found it was making HDR look washed out ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)).
- Turn off energy-saving and auto-brightness modes, and try a Warm colour temperature ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-best-tv-settings/)).

## 3. Last, the game's own HDR sliders

Beebom's starting point: **Max Brightness** at your TV's peak brightness, **Black Levels 0**, **Paper White 25** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-best-tv-settings/)). Check a sunny street and a night scene, and nudge from there.

## Your screenshots and clips look grey?

That's HDR too: the console captures an HDR image that looks washed out on a normal phone or laptop screen. Players fixed it by **turning HDR off** (Video Output on PS5; HDR10 and Auto HDR on Xbox Series S, then restart) before capturing, Kotaku reports ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/live-updates-on-gta-6-the-biggest-launch-in-gaming-history-2000758030)). Turn it back on to play.
