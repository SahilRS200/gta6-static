GTA 6 hides a **portal back to the original Vice City from 2002**. Step through it as Jason and you land in a low-poly, PS2-era version of the old Malibu Club, then come back with **Tommy Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt**. YouTuber **Memeio** found it after about **three days** of digging ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-easter-egg-takes-you-back-to-Vice-City-in-2002.1458005.0.html), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/23/a-gta-6-player-finds-tommy-vercettis-shirt-portal-to-original-vice-city/)). It's a side secret, not a story mission. **No story spoilers.**

## Before you start

- **You may need Chapter 3,** when the whole map opens up; VICE says "may", so it isn't confirmed ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tommy-vercetti-shirt-vice-city-easter-egg/)).
- **Don't fast travel** during the substation run: VICE says it can reset your progress.
- **Make a manual save first:** [how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/).

## Step by step

1. **Radio tower.** Climb the Badger R&D Radio Tower in Key Lento, near Jason's house. Flip the power switch to On, then press the button in the room. A Malibu Club logo comes up on the TV, and an LPL (Leonida Power and Light) poster is your clue ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tommy-vercetti-shirt-vice-city-easter-egg/), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/tommy-vercetti-gta-6-easter-egg/)).
2. **12 LPL substations, in order.** Each has blue letters painted on the wall; together they spell the old Vice City cheat **"Life Is Passing Me By"**. At each one, press the button or pull the lever marked with the right symbol. Insider Gaming has the full table: circle (B on Xbox) at stations 1, 2 and 11; L1 (LB) at 3, 5 and 9; square (X) at 4, 6, 7 and 8; triangle (Y) at 10 and 12 ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/tommy-vercetti-gta-6-easter-egg/)).
3. **Check your phone.** A text from an unknown number reads **"U-235 REACTOR2 19-06"**.
4. **Go to Reactor 2** at the LPL Turtle Head Power Station, east of Hamlet, **between 7 PM and 6 AM** in-game. A purple portal opens. Need to pass time? [Sleep in a safehouse](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/).
5. **Step through as Jason,** look around the 2002 Malibu Club, then walk back out through the portal ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tommy-vercetti-shirt-vice-city-easter-egg/)).

## What you get

- **Tommy Vercetti's blue Hawaiian shirt.** Every guide agrees on this one. Wear it from your wardrobe: [how to change clothes](/news/gta-6-how-to-change-clothes-outfits-hair-barber/).
- **His motorbike, reportedly.** Insider Gaming and Notebookcheck say you also keep Tommy's bike; VICE doesn't mention it, so treat it as likely rather than certain ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/tommy-vercetti-gta-6-easter-egg/), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-easter-egg-takes-you-back-to-Vice-City-in-2002.1458005.0.html)).

## Bonus: the portal wipes a six-star wanted level

A streamer, **Haigure Tamao**, reached six stars near the power station, then dived through the portal: entering old Vice City cleared his wanted level, and he came back to Leonida with **zero stars** ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/japanese-vtuber-actually-escapes-gta-6s-impossible-6-star-wanted-level-3431071/)). It may not be intended, so it could be patched. More on the cops: [wanted level explained](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/).
