**Update, 5:50 PM IST:** reviews are out. The embargo lifted today, 18 November, as the rumour said, and GTA 6 is at **99 on Metacritic**. [Read the scores and the complaints →](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/)

Everyone wanted to know the scores before midnight. Here's how it played out.

## The rumour

Insider NateTheHate said in late October that review codes had gone out and that the **embargo lifts on 18 November**.

## What's confirmed

**It was right.** Rockstar never announced a date, but critic reviews went live on 18 November, and the game is tied for the highest Metacritic score ever ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/18/the-gta-6-metacritic-review-score-is-in-and-ties-an-all-time-record/)). [Full breakdown →](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/)

## Ignore the early "scores" going viral

Two sets of numbers are doing the rounds on X and Reddit. **Neither is a review score.**

- **Metacritic 6.5:** a user score appeared on the GTA 6 page from a handful of ratings with no written reviews, before anyone could have played the game. Metacritic has since **removed those fake user reviews and blocked new user ratings** for now ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/metacritic-has-already-had-to-remove-fake-gta-6-user-reviews/), [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/metacritic-removes-fake-gta-6-reviews-after-early-ratings-reportedly-dragged-down-its-score/)).
- **PlayStation Store stars:** star ratings were spotted on the PS Store before launch, from a small number of accounts and with no text ([tbreak](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-playstation-store-ratings-before-launch/)). Dataminer figures of around 4.7 to 4.9 out of 5 are reported, but they come from tiny samples and aren't critic reviews.

The real critic score is now published, so check Metacritic itself rather than trusting a screenshot.

## Now that reviews are out

- Scores are showing up everywhere at once: YouTube, X, Instagram, Google Discover.
- Reviews usually avoid the ending, but **headlines and thumbnails can still spoil** missions, characters and map areas.
- New Zealand players have been live since **4:30 PM IST**, so raw gameplay clips are out too.

## Stay spoiler-free until midnight

Mute these on X and Instagram: `GTA 6 ending`, `GTA 6 spoilers`, `GTA VI ending`, `GTA 6 final mission`. The floating **FAQ** button and the **Quick help** slide on our homepage have a copy button for the list.
