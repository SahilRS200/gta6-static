Only in India: you can now order **GTA 6 with your groceries**. Quick-commerce apps **Blinkit and Zepto** have listed the game, with delivery estimates **as low as 10 minutes** in select areas.

## Where it's available

Reports list select areas of **Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Noida, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, Kolkata and Ahmedabad**. Blinkit is reported to be adding more cities over the next two days. Check the app for your pin code.

## The catch: there's no disc

The "physical edition" is a **box with a download code inside**. So:

- You still have to **download up to 212 GB**. A 10-minute delivery does not mean a 10-minute start.
- It **unlocks at 12:00 AM IST** like every other copy. No early access.
- Beebom reports only the **PS5 code-in-box edition** on both apps. Smartprix mentions Xbox pricing too, so check the listing before you pay.

## Price

PlayStation Store India lists the Standard Edition at **₹5,999** and the Ultimate at **₹7,499**. Compare the app price with the store before you order. [Full India price breakdown →](/gta-6-price-in-india/)

## Should you order?

- **Yes**, if you want the box as a collectible or prefer paying by UPI or cash on delivery.
- **No**, if you want to play at midnight and haven't started downloading. Buy digitally and [start the pre-load now](/news/gta-6-preload-live-india/).
