Only in India: you can now order **GTA 6 with your groceries**. Quick-commerce apps **Blinkit and Zepto** have listed the game, with delivery estimates **as low as 10 minutes** in select areas.

**Update, 19 Nov evening: Zepto says it has never sold out this fast.** Zepto CEO Aadit Palicha said **over 1 lakh users** searched the app for GTA 6, PS5s and controllers this week, and that Zepto had never sold out this fast before. Blinkit CEO Albinder Dhindsa joked about "a suspicious number of sick leaves" in the coming days ([PTI via Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/gta-vi-mania-grips-india-blinkit-and-zepto-cash-in-on-gaming-craze-3581659/)). Neither company has shared sales numbers. If your pin code shows it out of stock, buying digitally on the PlayStation Store is instant.

## Where it's available

Blinkit lists select areas of **11 cities**: **Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Noida, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, Kolkata and Ahmedabad**. Check the app for your pin code.

**Update, 19 Nov:** users in **Mumbai and Bengaluru** reported the game **out of stock** on Blinkit, with some Bengaluru areas selling out quickly ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/gta-6-is-available-on-blinkit-in-india-ahead-of-midnight-launch-already-out-of-stock-in-mumbai-bangalore)). Blinkit hasn't confirmed numbers. If your code won't redeem, PSN may be the problem, not the code: [PSN sign-in errors →](/news/gta-6-psn-sign-in-error-services-busy-reddit-down/)

## The catch: there's no disc

The "physical edition" is a **box with a download code inside**. So:

- You still have to **download up to 212 GB**. A 10-minute delivery does not mean a 10-minute start.
- It works the same as every other copy: the game unlocked in India at **12:00 AM IST on 19 November**.
- Beebom reports only the **PS5 code-in-box edition** on both apps. Smartprix mentions Xbox pricing too, so check the listing before you pay.

## Price

PlayStation Store India lists the Standard Edition at **₹5,999** and the Ultimate at **₹7,499**. Compare the app price with the store before you order. [Full India price breakdown →](/gta-6-price-in-india/)

## Should you order?

- **Yes**, if you want the box as a collectible or prefer paying by UPI or cash on delivery.
- **No**, if you want to play tonight. Buy digitally and start the download straight away; a code still means the full download.
