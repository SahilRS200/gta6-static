Yes, GTA 6 hides a **tank you can drive away for free**, plus two **Legendary weapons**, at the end of a short conspiracy side quest about a president's secret bunkers. The steps below follow [Beebom's guide](https://beebom.com/gta-6-free-tank-secret-bunker-location/) and [Khel Now's](https://khelnow.com/gaming/gta-6-secret-bunker-free-tank-guide-202611), which agree on all three codes. It's a side secret: no story spoilers.

## Before you go

- **Daytime only** for the first step: the man who starts it isn't there at night (Beebom, Khel Now).
- **Jason or Lucia** both work, Beebom says.
- **Save by hand first**, in case a step doesn't trigger. [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)

## Step by step

1. **Go to the Friends of Freedom landmark** in eastern Downtown Vice City in the day. Talk to the stranger behind it, pick **"What really happened?"** and then **"Russo?"** He is **Jose Bermudez**, and he points you to his apartment.
2. **Search his apartment in Little Cuba** (NW 4th Avenue). Upstairs, play the **tape recorder** and look at the **"Sharktooth Island" photo** by the fridge.
3. **Bunker 1, Sharktooth Island** (north of Waning Sands): enter **010317** on the keypad, pull the lever to restore power and head for the President's room.
4. **Open the safe.** Sit in the chair and search the drawer, answer the phone, play the tape in the other room, then read the classified document. Put the radio numbers in the order Protocol Two gives and the safe opens with **2975**. Inside: **Russo's Legendary pistol** and a photo of the next bunker.
5. **Bunker 2, northwest of Grassrivers near Big Pine:** the keypad is on a **tree trunk**; the code is **085355**. Restore power at the switch box and play the tape in the side room.
6. **Find the secret room:** go through the door ahead, turn right and crouch under the shutter. Press the **big red button** and the hangar opens: the tank is yours.
7. **Before you drive off,** grab the **Legendary Cold War 556 assault rifle** at the back of the hangar. It's easy to miss (Beebom, Khel Now).

## Can you keep the tank?

Sort of. Beebom says it's fast, has a mounted turret, and **comes back to the bunker if you lose it**, because the door stays open. But you **can't register it** as your own vehicle, so you can't store it in a garage. Expect a heavy police response if you take it into Vice City.
