GTA 6 didn't just sell, it emptied every other game's lobby. According to market-research firm **Circana**, **61% of US PS5 players and 56% of Xbox players worldwide who switched on their console on 19 November played GTA 6** that day. Circana's games analyst Mat Piscatella shared the figures from the firm's Player Engagement Tracker ([VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/)).

## The numbers

- **61% of active US PS5 players** played GTA 6 on launch day ([The Fly via TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/61-of-u-s-ps5-players-engaged-with-gta-vi-on-november-19-says-circana-thefly-news)).
- **56% of active Xbox players worldwide** did the same ([VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/)).
- **513 minutes on PS5, 494 on Xbox:** that's the average time those players spent in the game on 19 November, roughly **8.5 and 8.2 hours** ([VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/)).
- **Fortnite came second** with just **10%** of active players across both consoles ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/gta-6-over-half-of-console-owners-played-it/)).

## Read it with care

- **It's a share of people who played that day**, not of everyone who owns a PS5 or Xbox. Some headlines say "61% of PS5 owners"; that's not what Circana measured ([VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/)).
- **The PS5 figure is US only.** There's no India figure, and Circana didn't share one for India's players.
- **It's one day.** It tells us how big launch day was, not how many people will still be playing in a month.
