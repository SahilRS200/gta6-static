Looking for a mansion to buy in GTA 6? There isn't one. Garages are the only property for sale, and they store cars rather than earn money. Here's what to know, with no story spoilers.

## Houses: not for sale

- **Safehouses come with the story.** New ones unlock as you play; you can't buy a house or apartment ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/properties/)).

## How to buy a garage

- **Finish the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver.** It adds the **Vice City Real Estate** app to your phone, and you buy every garage there ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/properties/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-garage-locations/)).
- **8 garages are for sale, from $4,000 to $40,000.** All eight cost **$160,000** in total.
- **Cheapest:** Mitchell Auto Center, Kelly County, $4,000. **Priciest:** Atlantic Court Garage, Ocean Beach, $40,000. In between: Persistence Road ($7,600), 7th Avenue ($12,000), Store n' Bolt ($16,800), Wyatt Motors ($21,600), Vela Rota Driver ($24,000) and Bonita Court ($34,000), per GTABase.

## What a garage gets you

- **Space for cars, not income.** Each parking spot registers one vehicle. The base game's 12 garages (8 bought, 4 free from the story) give 16 spots, GTABase counts.
- **A trophy.** Owning all eight bought garages unlocks the **Little Boxes** trophy or achievement; the free story garages don't count.
- **Ultimate Edition** and pre-order bonuses add extra garages, Beebom says ([what Ultimate unlocks when](/news/gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses-unlock-chapters/)).

## Tips

- **Start cheap.** A $4,000 garage gives you one more spot for a car you want to keep ([how to steal cars](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/)).
- **Bank your cash first.** You can lose money when you die or get busted ([hide your cash](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)), and see [how to make money fast](/news/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast-early-game/).
