One of GTA 6's strangest running jokes is that being a furry is **against the law in Leonida**. In-game TV ads from an "Anti-Furry Association" tell people to report furry activity to the police ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-outlaws-furries-and-lets-you-help-them-escape-the-cops-3429724/), [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/11/19/gta-6-furry-law/)). The joke turns into a side mission: rescue a man in a fox suit from the police, and his friends pay you with a gun you can't get anywhere else. No story spoilers.

## Where and when it starts

- **Place:** Miramar Drive in **Crosstown, Vice City**. If you're in Chapter 3, it's right across from your apartment ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-furry-location-fury-dw9-gun/), [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-furry-smg-choice-guide/)).
- **Time:** at night only, **between 9 PM and 4 AM** in-game. The encounter is random, so check back after missions if it isn't there ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-furry-location-fury-dw9-gun/)).
- **What to look for:** a panicking man in a fursuit in the road, surrounded by police.

## How to do it

1. **Drive up and choose "Offer Ride".** On PS5 that's holding Up on the D-pad, then Square. **Don't skip this:** Game Rant says if you don't offer the ride, he's killed and the mission is gone for good ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-furry-location-fury-dw9-gun/)). Make a [manual save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/) before you go.
2. **Lose the police on the way to the marked parking garage.** Driving into the garage clears your wanted level ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-furry-location-fury-dw9-gun/)). Struggling? [How to lose the cops →](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/)
3. **Switch to the van** and drive the furries to their safehouse. Another furry drives off in your car as a decoy; Screen Rant says you get it back undamaged at the end ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-furry-smg-choice-guide/)).
4. **Pick your reward.** At the safehouse, Chase the Fox offers a gun or **$12,500** in cash ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-furry-smg-choice-guide/), [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-furry-location-fury-dw9-gun/)).

## Gun or cash?

- **Take the gun**, both guides say. It's a **DW-9 SMG with a furry art decal**, often called the Fury DW-9. It's a little less accurate than the standard DW-9 but handles a little better, and you can't get it by robbing stores ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-furry-smg-choice-guide/)).
- **Money is easy to make** elsewhere: [fast money early](/news/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast-early-game/) or [how to rob a store](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-store-safe-code-cameras-escape/). Only take the cash if you really don't want the gun.
