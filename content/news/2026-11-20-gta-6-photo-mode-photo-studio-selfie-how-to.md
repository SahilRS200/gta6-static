GTA 6 has a proper photo mode. Rockstar calls it **Photo Studio**, and it works from the very first mission. Here is how to use it, plus the in-game phone camera for selfies. No story spoilers.

## How to open Photo Studio

1. **Pause the game:** Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox Series X/S.
2. **Pick Photo Studio** from the pause menu. Insider Gaming says Square on PS5 or X on Xbox also jumps straight to it from the pause screen ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/does-gta-6-have-photomode-photo-studio-how-to/)).

It is available from **Lucia's prologue**; you don't need to unlock anything ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-photo-mode/)).

## What you can change

- **Camera angle** and **depth of field** (blur the background).
- **Filters**, colour palette and vignette.
- **Up to five light sources**, each with its own colour and brightness. Lights work well with the game's shadows at night.
- **Hide your character** to shoot just the scenery.

**What you can't do:** change the **time of day or the weather** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-photo-mode/), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/does-gta-6-have-photomode-photo-studio-how-to/)). Want golden hour? Sleep in a safehouse bed and wake up in the evening ([how to sleep and skip time](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/)). Photo Studio also works in cutscenes, but some options show "Currently Unavailable" there.

## Saving your shot

Select **Save Photo**, or hide the menu and use your console's own screenshot button (Create on PS5, Share on Xbox). Saved shots show up in the phone's **Gallery** app ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-photo-mode/)).

Is your screenshot coming out grey or washed out? That's an HDR capture issue: [the fix](/news/gta-6-hdr-washed-out-best-tv-settings-ps5-xbox/).

## Selfies with the phone camera

The phone camera unlocks a little later, once you take control of Jason.

- Press **D-pad Up** for the phone and open **Camera** (third icon, top row).
- Flip to the **front camera** for a selfie. Jason has **six poses and seven facial expressions**, and you can change your partner's pose when you're in the shot together.
- The rear camera has up to **5x zoom**, grid lines and **17 filters** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-photo-mode/)).

**Snapmatic**, the in-game social app, is for browsing other people's posts, liking photos and marking places on your map.
