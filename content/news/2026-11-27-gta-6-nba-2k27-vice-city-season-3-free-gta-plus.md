GTA 6's Vice City is taking over another Take-Two game. **NBA 2K27's Season 3, "Vice City Takeover", starts today, 27 November, and runs until 15 January 2027**, [2K announced](https://newsroom.2k.com/news/vice-city-comes-to-nbar-2k27-in-season-3). The part that matters to GTA 6 players: if you have **GTA+**, you can play NBA 2K27 for free during the season.

## The GTA+ perk

- **Who:** GTA+ members on **PS5 and Xbox Series X/S**, [per GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/nba-2k27-vice-city-season-3-gta-plus/).
- **What:** the **NBA 2K27 Standard Edition, Season 3 included, at no extra cost**, from 27 November to 15 January. Members who'd rather buy it get 20% off.
- **It's a membership benefit, not a free copy to keep**, and online play still needs PS Plus or an eligible Game Pass plan, [MIXED News notes](https://mixed-news.com/en/gta-6-nba-2k27-vice-city-season-3-gta-plus/).
- **A GTA+ bonus pack** (Blue Boat Hat, oversized T-shirt, 30 Skill Boosts, 15 Gatorade Boosts) follows from 17 December to 31 March.

**Got a free GTA+ month from your digital GTA 6 purchase?** Claiming it now covers part of this window. It auto-renews at full price afterwards, so set a reminder to cancel. [How to claim it →](/news/gta-6-free-gta-plus-month-offer-deadline/)

## What's in the Vice City season

- **A Vice City park** in The City, with beachfront courts and neon-lit pastel hotels, and the **Sahara Arena** replacing the NBA Arena for the season.
- **Limited-time events:** Conquer Leonida, Vice Run, and **Neon vs Noir**, a 5v5 pitting Vice City against Los Santos.
- **Rewards** include a Los Santos jersey, a Vice City varsity jacket and a Burger Shot employee outfit.

## The Vice City Edition

A digital-only **NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition** with Miami Heat centre Bam Adebayo on the cover costs **$39.99** (an introductory console price) on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2 and PC. It bundles the Season 3 Pro Pass, a Vice City T-shirt and boosts, [per GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/nba-2k27-vice-city-season-3-gta-plus/). Check the rupee price on PS Store India or the Xbox Store before you buy; we haven't seen it listed yet.

Nothing changes inside GTA 6 itself. More for players in India: [GTA 6 India hub](/gta-6-india/).
