GTA 6 has only just started unlocking, and it may already be the most pre-ordered game ever. These are **estimates from analytics firm Sensor Tower, not official numbers**. Rockstar and Take-Two have not published pre-order totals.

## Official, 20 Nov: $3.79 billion on day one

Take-Two has now given its own number: **$3.79 billion in retail sales by the end of launch day and 32.5 million copies sold before release**, which it calls the biggest entertainment launch in history. That is well above the tracker estimates below. [The official numbers →](/news/gta-6-official-sales-3-79-billion-day-one-record/)

## Update, 20 Nov: about 24.6 million and $2.36 billion on day one

- **About 24.58 million copies, worth roughly $2.36 billion**, by Thursday evening, 19 November (launch day), Sensor Tower estimates, as reported by Reuters via [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-take-two-grand-theft-auto-vi-sales/). That's up about 5.5 million copies from the launch-morning estimate.
- **Nearly three times GTA V's first day**, which made roughly $800 million in 2013, by the same report. [Make Tech Easier](https://maketecheasier.com/gta-6-first-day-sales-estimate/) says about three in four copies are still on PS5.
- **Still not official.** Take-Two has only called demand "unprecedented" and hasn't published a sales figure; [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/) reports it is expected to share official numbers soon. Analysts quoted by Reuters say GTA Online's timing matters more to the stock than these unit sales.

## 19 Nov: about 19 million and $1.84 billion

- **About 19.05 million digital copies, worth roughly $1.84 billion**, by Sensor Tower's launch-day estimate as reported by [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/) and [Hromadske](https://hromadske.ua/en/lifestyle/274650-gta-6-launches-with-near-perfect-reviews-estimated-19-million-copies-sold). That's up about 2 million in a day.
- **About 14.23 million on PS5 and 4.82 million on Xbox Series X|S**, still roughly three in four on PlayStation.
- That is **more than double the roughly $800 million** GTA V made on its first day in 2013, by the same coverage. It is still a tracker estimate built mostly from pre-orders: **Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced a sales figure.**

## 18 Nov evening: over 17 million

- **About 17.07 million digital pre-purchases, worth roughly $1.65 billion**, per Sensor Tower's tracker as reported by [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/) on the eve of launch.
- **About 12.73 million on PS5 and 4.35 million on Xbox Series X|S.**
- Sensor Tower projects it could reach **up to 25 million** by launch. Still estimates, still digital only, still not confirmed by Rockstar.
- It's also being watched like nothing before: [GTA 6 just broke the all-time streaming launch record →](/news/gta-6-streaming-record-twitch-5-million-viewers/)

## The numbers (16 November)

- **About 14.94 million digital pre-orders** across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S as of 16 November, according to Sensor Tower estimates reported by [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-many-people-pre-ordered-gta-6/) and [Rockstar Intel](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-vi-could-make-2-billion-this-week-15-million-pre-orders-already/).
- **Roughly $1.45 billion** in pre-order revenue, more than ₹12,000 crore ([The Loadout](https://theloadout.com/gta-6-has-already-made-1-5-billion-and-nobody-has-played-it-yet)).
- **About three in four are on PlayStation**: roughly 11.1 million on PS5 and 3.8 million on Xbox ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-many-people-pre-ordered-gta-6/)).
- The estimate **roughly doubled in two weeks**, from about 7.7 million in early November.

## Read it with care

What *is* official: on 10 November, **Sony and Microsoft both confirmed GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in the history of their stores**, without giving a number ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-is-officially-the-most-pre-ordered-game-in-playstation-and-xbox-store-history-sony-and-microsoft-confirm/)).

Sensor Tower counts **digital pre-orders only**, so physical copies and retail codes are not included. Tracker figures can also over- or under-count. There is no India-specific breakdown.
