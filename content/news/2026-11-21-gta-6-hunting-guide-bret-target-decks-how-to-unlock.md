Hunting is back in GTA 6, and it works more like Red Dead Redemption 2 than GTA 5: a side questline, specific animals to track down, and a payout that depends on how cleanly you kill them. Here is how to start it and how to avoid wasted trips, with PS5 and Xbox buttons. No story spoilers beyond mission names.

## How to unlock hunting

- **It's a Jason side quest.** After the Chapter 2 mission **Coffee Break**, Jason gets a text from his old army friend **Bret Vogel**. That opens the Strangers & Freaks mission **Veteran Hunted**, followed by **Bret's Hunting Decks** ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-animals-wildlife/), [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/strangers/brets-hunting-decks)).
- **You get a rifle.** Bret hands you an **SM-69 hunting rifle** to start with (OutputLag).
- **It counts for 100%.** GTABase lists Bret's Hunting Decks as required for 100% completion, and a bronze trophy, **Fair Game**, is tied to hunting a unique animal for Bret ([Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/psn-trophies/fair-game)). Our [trophy guide](/news/gta-6-trophy-list-platinum-missable-trophy-guide/) has the full list.

## How the target decks work

- **15 decks in all.** Each deck is a short shopping list of animals. Bret texts you the next one when you finish, often with a clue, such as an animal being nocturnal ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-brets-hunting-target-deck-locations)).
- **The first three**, per Push Square: whitetail buck and doe (Mount Kalaga National Park or the Abandoned Concrete Plant); a white wild turkey, an eastern spotted skunk and a wild boar (Adler Creek, Bonanza or Mount Kalaga); then a cane toad, a nutria and a Nile monitor (the Buckskin swamps or Pelican Valley).
- **Only clean kills count.** Bret accepts animals in **Pristine** or **Good** condition only. Use Focus (**L3 + R3**) to see an animal's vitals, then aim for the head or heart with the right gun. A clean shot also pays more when you sell (Push Square, OutputLag). New to Focus? [How Focus works →](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/)
- **Bring a truck.** Bret collects big animals like boars and bears himself; smaller ones you carry in a vehicle. Push Square used a pickup.

## Tips that save time

- **Match the gun to the animal.** Push Square used the SM-69 for deer and boar and the smaller **Wortham W-22** for the turkey and skunk. Scopes help, because many animals bolt when you walk up; [Ammu-Nation](/news/gta-6-weapons-guide-ammu-nation-buy-store-recover-guns/) sells attachments.
- **Hunt at the right hour.** Some animals appear only at certain times. The skunk showed up around 8 PM in-game for Push Square. [Sleep to skip time →](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/)
- **Boars fight back.** Find them in shaded forest by day and shoot from range.
- **You don't have to kill to log an animal.** For the wildlife journal, hold **L1 (LB)**, pick Binoculars, zoom in until "Study" appears and hold **R1 (RB)**. The journal (Pause → Progress → Animals) has 255 entries across 8 tabs, 61 of them hunting animals (OutputLag). [Birdwatching guide →](/news/gta-6-birdwatching-62-bird-species-wildlife-logbook/)
- **Hunting is "clean" in your profile.** Killing animals normally pushes your [Criminal Profile](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/) toward Psycho, but hunting doesn't count.
