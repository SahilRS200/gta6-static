GTA 6 has **hundreds of rooms, houses and flats you can walk into** ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)), but the game never tells you which doors open. The map does. Here's how to read it. **No story spoilers.**

## How many can you enter? A fan counted 1,563

A Reddit user, Aileos, counted **1,563 enterable buildings out of 5,588 in Leonida, about 28%**, covering shops, banks and other buildings. That's more than double the "700 interiors" figure fans argued about before launch. It's a fan count, not a Rockstar number ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175720-gta-6-has-1563-enterable-buildings-nearly-28-of-leonidas-total.html), citing Eurogamer).

## The world map: two shades of blue

- **Dark blue building = there's an entrance or an interior.** Most buildings on the world map are shaded in one of two blues ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-beginners-guide-6-tips-you-need-to-know-before-starting-gta-6/)).
- **Light blue building = scenery.** You can't go in ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
- Dark blue means *something* is accessible, not that every floor or every door is.

## The minimap: find the actual door

- **Walk up to a dark blue building and watch the minimap.** The entrance is usually marked with a **white line** ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-beginners-guide-6-tips-you-need-to-know-before-starting-gta-6/)).
- **Inside a shop, head for the back.** As you get close to back doors, the minimap shows whether there's more to find: a hidden room, a staircase, or a door that will never open ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-beginners-guide-6-tips-you-need-to-know-before-starting-gta-6/), [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).

## Focus: white door or red door

- **Use Focus (L3 + R3 on PS5) and look at a door.** **White** means you can go in; **red** means it's alarmed ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-beginners-guide-6-tips-you-need-to-know-before-starting-gta-6/)). Walk through a red one and expect the police; here's [how the wanted level works](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/).
- **Many places have a back door,** and the snap gun gets you in without setting off the alarm. Valuables usually sit in a safe in a locked back room; some safes can be picked with the right tool, others need sticky bombs ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
