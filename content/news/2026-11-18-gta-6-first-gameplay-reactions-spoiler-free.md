The first players are in. Since New Zealand unlocked at 4:30 PM IST, clips and hot takes have been pouring onto X and Twitch. Here's what people are saying about **how GTA 6 plays**, with **no story details**. (Note: these are player reactions, not verified reviews. For the critics' verdict, see our [Metacritic 99 roundup](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/).)

## What players love

- **The driving.** It's the most-shared clip type so far. One widely shared post called a first drive proof that Rockstar had "truly outdone themselves", and early reactions single out a "next-level driving experience" ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/gaming/gta-6-early-reviews-set-internet-alight-first-driving-clips-99-metacritic-claim-fuel-unprecedented-hype-1.500752451)).
- **The graphics.** "The graphics are so good," one streamer said on stream, as screenshots spread widely ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-gameplay-first-look-surfaces-online-watch-videos-of-how-the-game-starts-and-graphics-look.html)).
- **Combat that feels like Red Dead 2.** Digit describes the shooting as feeling close to Red Dead Redemption 2, and early clips show a healing mechanic in fights.

## The mixed bits

- **Not everyone is sold.** Digit reports at least one streamer's chat was unhappy with the gameplay shown.
- **It's 30fps on every console**, even PS5 Pro, which is a common complaint in clip comments. Rockstar says it cut a full frame of input lag to make it feel more responsive ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-pro-is-not-powerful-enough-to-run-gta-6-at-60fps-rockstar-explains-frame-rate-decision)). [Why there's no 60fps →](/news/gta-6-30fps-ps5-pro-rockstar-explains/)

## Update, 19 Nov morning: what players said after night one

India unlocked at **12:00 AM IST**, and the verdict from people actually playing is mostly glowing:

- **India: "absolute cinema".** Indian players are flooding X with clips, praising the graphics, NPCs and wildlife, and one creator said streams "don't do justice" to how it looks ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-india-launch-internet-erupts-with-video-clips-of-jason-and-lucia-committing-crimes-in-leonida-check-first-look.html); the article describes the opening scene).
- **Australia: 9.5/10 and bedtime at 4 AM.** A player with 5,000 hours in GTA V rated it 9.5 after a 6–7 hour first session, while a critic at ScreenHub gave it a cooler 4 out of 5 ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/gta-vi-smashes-expectations-after-overnight-launch/107401924)). Launch night also set Australia's internet traffic record. [The full story →](/news/gta-6-launch-australia-internet-record-players-up-till-4am/)
- **The gripe hasn't changed:** 30fps on every console.
