**Short answer: there's no menu teleport in GTA 6.** You fast travel by booking a ride on your phone and paying to skip it, or by catching public transport. RydeMe is the main way, and it only opens after the BuckMe mission ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-fast-travel-explained-how-to-fast-travel-in-gta-6/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-fast-travel-explained/)). Spoiler-free: only mission names that tell you when a feature opens.

## RydeMe — the main fast travel

- **When it unlocks:** during the BuckMe mission. RydeMe needs a bank account, which that mission sets up, and the app then appears on your phone by itself ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-fast-travel-explained-how-to-fast-travel-in-gta-6/)).
- **How to book:** press up on the D-pad to open the phone, open RydeMe and pick your safehouse, your map marker, or "New Destination" to choose a spot on the map ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-fast-travel-explained-how-to-fast-travel-in-gta-6/)).
- **The fare shows before you confirm.** Once you're in, ride in real time, speed it up ($5 a go, Beebom says), or **skip the whole trip for $100** on top of the fare ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-fast-travel-explained-how-to-fast-travel-in-gta-6/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-fast-travel-explained/)). Kotaku's tip: once you're in the car, focus on the driver to bring up the skip ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)).
- **Can't see the car?** Check the minimap. If a driver can't reach you, the ride is cancelled, so walk to an open road and book again ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-fast-travel-explained-how-to-fast-travel-in-gta-6/)).

## Paradise Taxi — the phone-contact option

- **Call it from your Contacts.** The same $100 skip (or $5 speed-ups) applies, but the cab may stop for other passengers, so riding it out is slower ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-fast-travel-explained/)).

## Buses, trains and ferries — the cheap way

- **Fares depend on distance and skipping the ride is free,** Beebom says. The catch: you have to be at the stop, station or dock when it arrives, and routes are fixed, so you may end up near your target rather than on it ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-fast-travel-explained/)).
