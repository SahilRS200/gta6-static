GTA 6 players are climbing one radio tower on purpose to meet its strangest resident: **a man who can't be killed with a sniper rifle and comes after you when you try**. Clips from Reddit and X show him walking through the shots, then appearing behind Jason and killing him in one hit ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-strange-demonic-npc-encounter/), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-has-an-unkillable-NPC-that-hunts-down-players-but-there-might-be-way-to-kill-him.1458771.0.html)). It's a free-roam secret, not a story mission. **No story spoilers here.** Rockstar hasn't said anything about it.

## Where it is

- **The radio tower near Jason's house in Key Lento.** A free **Shrewsbury SM-69 sniper rifle** sits on top; grab it on the way up ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-strange-demonic-npc-encounter/)).
- **From the top, scope the pedestrians below.** Notebookcheck says the man to look for wears a **light blue T-shirt and a baseball cap** ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-has-an-unkillable-NPC-that-hunts-down-players-but-there-might-be-way-to-kill-him.1458771.0.html)). Game Rant says it's not clear exactly which NPC it is, and other players have filmed different pedestrians doing the same thing, so it may be a chance encounter ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-strange-demonic-npc-encounter/)).

## What happens

- **The shots don't hurt him.** He looks straight up at you and makes a throat-slitting gesture, and Jason reacts to the bullets doing nothing ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-strange-demonic-npc-encounter/)).
- **He comes for you.** Some players say he runs to the tower and climbs it, with dramatic music playing ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-has-an-unkillable-NPC-that-hunts-down-players-but-there-might-be-way-to-kill-him.1458771.0.html)). In the first viral clip he teleports behind Jason and kills him instantly: "Wasted" ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-strange-demonic-npc-encounter/)).

## Can you kill him?

- **One reported counter, unconfirmed:** a reader comment on Notebookcheck says to **parachute down the tower while he climbs it**, and that he can be hurt once the music stops. Notebookcheck didn't test it ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-has-an-unkillable-NPC-that-hunts-down-players-but-there-might-be-way-to-kill-him.1458771.0.html)). Need a parachute? See [how to get one early](/news/gta-6-first-plane-helicopter-location-how-to-fly-early/).
- **Save first.** You'll probably die, and dying costs you the cash you carry: [use a manual save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/) and [stash your money](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/).
- **The theories:** fans guess he's an alien (the tower's walls carry UFO references) or another NPC who knows he's in a game ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-has-an-unkillable-NPC-that-hunts-down-players-but-there-might-be-way-to-kill-him.1458771.0.html)). Nothing is confirmed.
