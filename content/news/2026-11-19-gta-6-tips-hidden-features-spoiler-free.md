GTA 6 unlocked in India at **12:00 AM IST**, and the game throws a lot of systems at you without explaining them. Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen put together a long list of **spoiler-free** tips from his time with the game ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)). These are the ones we think help most in your first night. **No story spoilers.**

## Money and getting around

1. **Dive shipwrecks early.** Underwater wreck exploration opens up very early and pays out easy cash. More ways to earn: [GTA 6 money guide](/news/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast-early-game/).
2. **Hold down on the D-pad** to slow time, see nearby points of interest and mark them for your GPS.
3. **Fix your car at a petrol pump.** It's often cheaper than calling the in-game vehicle app, which charges for repairs and spawns. While you're there, fill up: cars now run out of fuel. [How refuelling works →](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/)
4. **Need a quick ride?** The in-game RydeMe taxi lets you fast travel for $100 (in-game cash). Lost your own car? [Here's how to get it back →](/news/gta-6-car-impounded-get-it-back-scooter-bros/)

## Shaking the police

5. **Keep a spare outfit in your car's trunk.** Changing clothes helps you lose police attention. You can also store guns there.
6. **Calm a cop without a fight.** At one star, with no gun drawn, hold the left trigger on an officer to try to defuse it.
7. **Repaint at Pay 'n' Spray.** GameSpot notes an early mission introduces the auto shop, where a repaint makes the cops ease off ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/grand-theft-auto-6-review/)).
8. **Watch for red tracker icons** on cars. Leave those alone until you can deal with the tracker later.

## Small things that pay off

9. **Eat on the move.** You can eat and drink while walking, shooting or even driving. Eating before a job boosts health, stamina and focus.
10. **Pet the dogs and cats** (hold the left trigger). It earns focus and behaviour points, and so does throwing your rubbish in the bin.
11. **Use your indicators.** Other drivers react to turn signals.
12. **Shop as a pair.** Buying clothes for your partner earns romance points. [How the relationship meter works →](/news/gta-6-relationship-meter-jason-lucia-levels-how-to-raise/)
13. **Flip an overturned car yourself.** Stand beside it, click the left stick to get into position, then hold Square (PS5) or X (Xbox) to push it back onto its wheels. It fails if the car is too heavy, you're out of stamina or it's on a steep slope. The same push rolls a car that's out of petrol or shoves a boat or jet ski off the sand ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/two-cool-hidden-features-gta-6-doesnt-explain-flipping-and-sitting-2000757835)). Out of petrol? [How to refuel →](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/)
14. **Sit down and take it in.** Hold Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox) on foot: your character sits on grass or sand, leans against a wall, or rests on one foot elsewhere (Kotaku).

## Bonus: take a shower

Engadget found a tip in the game's help menu: **a shower raises your focus meter**, helps Jason and Lucia's relationship and **washes off blood stains** so you draw less police suspicion ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/)). Worth a stop at home after a messy job. Also from Engadget's live blog: aiming can feel "floaty", so try the **aim assist options** in settings.

Kotaku's last tip is the best one: **stay off "full story" videos** for now. Clips from players who started hours earlier are everywhere.
