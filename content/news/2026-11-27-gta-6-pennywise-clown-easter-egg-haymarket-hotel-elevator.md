GTA 6 hides a nod to **Pennywise**, the clown from Stephen King's *IT*, in a hotel lift in Vice Beach. It's a short jump scare, not a mission, and it gives you **no reward** — but it's a good one to show a friend. Game Rant found it and wrote up the steps ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-it-the-clown-easter-egg-secret-location-find)). **No story spoilers.**

## When you can do it

- **Any time after the prologue,** once you have a car to drive to Vice Beach, Game Rant says. Later chapters put you closer, so the trip is shorter.
- **Go after midnight in-game.** Game Rant says nothing happened at about 8 PM, but the clown was there at around 4 AM. Need to pass time? [Sleep in a safehouse](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/).

## Step by step

1. **Find the Haymarket Hotel** in Vice Beach, west of Ocean Beach, at the far left of the area past the shops ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-it-the-clown-easter-egg-secret-location-find)).
2. **Walk in and turn left** to the elevators. The left one says "Sorry, Out of Order", with a red balloon nearby.
3. **Take the right-hand elevator,** the one full of balloons, and press the button to go up.
4. **Wait for the lights.** They flicker and go out; when they come back, a bloodied clown is there and swings a machete, then vanishes.
5. **Ride back down** and you're done.
