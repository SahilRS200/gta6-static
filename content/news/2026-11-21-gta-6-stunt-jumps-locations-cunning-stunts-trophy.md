Stunt jumps are back in GTA 6, but they are only one part of a much bigger stunt list, and the trophy doesn't need all of them. Here is what counts, where the game tracks it and the fastest way to the trophy. No story spoilers.

## How many stunts there are

- **397 stunts in all**, in four types: **130 stunt jumps**, **202 under-the-bridge flights**, **40 knife flights** and **25 base jumps** ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-stunt-jumps-hoops/)).
- **Cunning Stunts (Silver):** complete any **250** stunts, of any type ([Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/achievements/20817)). You don't need every jump.
- **100% completion** asks for **300** of the 397 stunts, plus **90 of the 100 basketball hoops** (OutputLag, [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist/)). OutputLag notes it isn't settled yet whether a plain landing counts toward the 300 or only a finished challenge.

## Where to find them

- **Stunt jumps:** Map > Exploration > Stunt Spots. Clearing map fog reveals icons you would otherwise miss.
- **Your totals by region:** Progress > Activities > Stunts.
- **Basketball hoops:** Map > Exploration > Points of Interest > Basketball Hoops (all per OutputLag).

## How to finish them faster

- **Read the challenge first.** Each stunt jump has its own condition, such as a backflip, clearing a vehicle, a set speed in a set car or bringing your partner. A jump is only marked Completed once that is done, so check the map card before you drive over (OutputLag).
- **Fly for quick numbers.** A helicopter is the fastest way through bridges and knife flights; OutputLag suggests the Balsero and Bayside canals for a quick run of bridges. Need a helicopter? [Where to find one early →](/news/gta-6-first-plane-helicopter-location-how-to-fly-early/)
- **Base jumps:** start between the two yellow cones and open the parachute early enough to steer.
- **Fast cars for long gaps.** [The fastest cars and where they are →](/news/gta-6-fastest-cars-where-to-find-ruiner-nitrous-early/)
- **Don't use cheats on this save.** Cheats disable trophies (OutputLag). Our [trophy guide](/news/gta-6-trophy-list-platinum-missable-trophy-guide/) has the full list.
