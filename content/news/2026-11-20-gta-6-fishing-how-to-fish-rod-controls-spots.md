Fishing is one of the calmest things to do in GTA 6, and you can start early in the Keys. Here is how it works, with PS5 and Xbox buttons. No story spoilers.

## Get a rod

- **You don't buy one.** Rods lie around docks and small boats across the map. The easiest is on the little dock behind **Jason's house in Key Lento**, which opens up early in the story ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-fish-in-gta-6/), [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-fishing-guide/)).
- **Your first rod comes with a Small Saltwater Lure,** games.gg reports — fine for small fish.

## The controls

- **Find fish:** press **L3 + R3** together for Focus on PS5 (games.gg lists **LT** on Xbox); it highlights the spots where fish are likely to be. [How Focus works →](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/)
- **Cast:** hold **L2 / LT** to grip the rod and keep holding it, then hold **R2 / RT** and release. Hold longer to cast farther.
- **Hook:** reel with the **right stick** (turn it clockwise) until the controller vibrates hard, then press **R2 / RT**.
- **Tire the fish:** move the **left stick** left and right while it fights.
- **Land it:** when the vibration stops, turn the right stick clockwise again to reel it in.
- **Missed?** Press **Circle / B** to recast. On Xbox, **X** cuts the line.

## Tips

- **Different water, different fish.** Take a boat further out to sea for bigger fish and new spots, Beebom says.
- **Keep or throw back.** The result screen shows the species and weight, flags a "NEW RECORD", and lets you release the fish. A counter tracks how many species you've caught ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-fishing-guide/)).
- **Need cash instead?** See [how to make money fast](/news/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast-early-game/).
- **Save first.** GTA 6 keeps one autosave, so make a manual save before a long session ([how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)).
