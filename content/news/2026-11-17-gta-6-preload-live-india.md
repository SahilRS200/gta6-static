Rockstar opened **GTA 6 pre-loading on 10 November 2026**. Pre-orders have been open for much longer, but the pre-load is what actually matters on launch night: it's the download itself.

**Pre-loading puts the game on your console early. It stays locked until 12:00 AM IST on 19 November.** It does not let you play before that.

## Why you should do it tonight

Launch-night servers get hammered and Indian broadband varies a lot. If the download isn't finished by midnight, you'll be watching a progress bar while everyone else is in Leonida.

## PS5: check your download

1. Open **Game Library** on your PS5.
2. Find **Grand Theft Auto VI** under your purchased games.
3. Select it and choose **Download** if it isn't already queued.
4. Leave the console in **Rest Mode** with downloads allowed so it keeps going overnight.

## Xbox Series X|S

Open **My games & apps → Full library → Owned games**, find GTA VI and install it.

## Before midnight

- Keep the console **online** so the license unlocks on time.
- Leave space for a **day-one update**. Big launches almost always ship one.
- Mute spoiler keywords — New Zealand plays [7.5 hours before India](/news/play-gta-6-early-india-new-zealand-trick/).
