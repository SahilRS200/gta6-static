Two days in, players are hunting GTA 6's Easter eggs as hard as its story. Here are four you can go and find tonight on PS5 or Xbox. **None of them touch the story**, so this page is spoiler-free.

## 1. The Fast and Furious car (Paul Walker tribute)

- **What it is:** a green **Mitsubishi Eclipse-style car** like the one Brian O'Conner (Paul Walker) drove in *The Fast and the Furious* (2001), [BGR reports](https://www.bgr.com/2322463/gta-6-hidden-fast-and-furious-paul-walker-car/).
- **Where:** a garage attached to a **white house near the middle of Vespucci Island**.
- **How to get it:** break into that garage. The car is inside and you can drive it away.
- **Tip:** keep it. If it gets towed or wrecked later, you can get it back: [car impounded? →](/news/gta-6-car-impounded-get-it-back-scooter-bros/)

## 2. The egg at the top of the tower (a Vice City callback)

- **What it is:** a nest of large, colourful eggs at the top of one of Leonida's tallest communications towers. Throw one at the road below and a **short cutscene** plays as it hits a passing car, [GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-secret-cutscene-seven-minute-tower-easter-egg-795530-20261119) and [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/gta-6-player-discovers-literal-easter-egg-leonida-1833342) report. X user xGarbett shared the first clip.
- **Why it's funny:** *GTA: Vice City* (2002) hid a literal Easter egg too. Kotaku's writer also found the nest ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/18-coolest-details-players-have-spotted-in-gta-6-so-far-2000758435)).
- **Before you climb:** the ladder takes **about seven minutes**. Neither outlet gives the exact tower, and the throw may take a few tries. **Save first** in case you fall: [how to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
- IBTimes UK says you can also talk to an influencer at the top.

## 3. The RX Men comic (an X-Men parody)

- **Where:** on the magazine rack inside the **247 store in Key Lento**, Mariana County. PowerPyx lists it as a collectible ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=374112)).
- **Bonus:** Key Lento is also where the free home gym and the talking-parrot cash are. [Parrot cash →](/news/gta-6-talking-parrot-hidden-cash-three-sheets-liquor/)

## 4. Michael's boat from GTA 5 (new)

- **What it is:** a **Marquis sailboat**, the same model Michael De Santa owned in *GTA 5*. Look at its GPS screen: it shows the map of **Los Santos**, GTA 5's city, not Leonida, [Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta-5-easter-egg-michael-boat/).
- **Where:** parked **outside Jason's safehouse** from the start of the game, so you can check it in your first hour.
- **Is it really his?** Players think so, but Rockstar hasn't said. Game Rant calls it "presumably" Michael's boat.

## 5. Mount Chiliad chatter (a GTA 5 mystery callback, new)

- **What it is:** two passers-by near a bar you drive past during an **early mission** chat about the **Mount Chiliad mystery** from *GTA 5*, joking about a hidden bunker with jetpacks and eggs under the mountain, [GamingBible reports](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-gta-5-mount-chiliad-mystery-easter-egg-802897-20261120). Reddit user Tr1angleChoke spotted it first.
- **Will you hear it?** It's ambient street talk, so it may not trigger for everyone. Turn subtitles on and slow down near the bar.
- **Does it mean anything?** Fans are already arguing over it, but Rockstar hasn't said; it may just be a wink at long-time players.
