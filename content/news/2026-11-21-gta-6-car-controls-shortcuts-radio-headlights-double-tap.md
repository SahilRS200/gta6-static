GTA 6 hides a few driving shortcuts behind double-taps, and some of them aren't in the in-game help menu at all. Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen, who has put more than 150 hours into the game, rounded them up after spotting several in Reddit posts ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/here-are-some-super-useful-control-shortcuts-gta-6-doesnt-tell-you-about-2000759100)). Buttons below are PS5 / Xbox. No story spoilers.

## The five shortcuts

- **Radio on or off: double-tap D-pad left.** No need to open the vehicle menu to kill the music. This one isn't in the help menu.
- **Headlights: double-tap Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox).** Each double-tap cycles off, high beams and low beams. Also missing from the help menu.
- **Hop out with the engine still running: double-tap Triangle (PS5) or Y (Xbox).** The car keeps its engine and radio on. Do it at speed and you roll out while the car carries on, which is handy for crashing a car into something.
- **Walk around a boat while it moves: double-tap Triangle / Y at the wheel.** You get up and walk the deck while the boat keeps going.
- **Road rage: click the right stick.** Your character yells at the drivers around you. The help menu calls it the road rage button.
