Two of GTA 6's strangest side quests are easy to miss: a cryptid hunt with a man called Kirk, and a strawberry farmer called Kyle whose crops are not normal. LADbible lists both among the most unsettling secrets players have found ([LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-secret-missions-5-best-056826-20261124)). Neither touches the main story. **No story spoilers, and we don't give away how either one ends.**

## The Skunk Ape (that "Bigfoot" clip)

- **It isn't Bigfoot.** Locals call it the **Skunk Ape**, VGC says ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-bigfoot-explained-how-to-find-bigfoot-in-gta-6/)).
- **How to start it:** read Kirk's Skunk Ape flyers. You'll see them around Key Lento early on, and LADbible says there's a notice at the Rusty Anchor. Or spot the creature yourself; it most often shows up on the drive to the Kingfishers camp by the water south of Hamlet ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-bigfoot-explained-how-to-find-bigfoot-in-gta-6/), [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-secret-missions-5-best-056826-20261124)).
- **Wait for Kirk.** A few days later he contacts you. VGC says the chain starts **only as Jason, and only at night**. [How to switch characters →](/news/gta-6-how-to-switch-between-jason-and-lucia/) · [How to skip to night →](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/)
- **Three quests:** Search for the Skunk Ape, Search for Tree Effigies, then **Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove**, which Kirk texts you about from Chapter 2 onward ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-help-kirk-find-mr-mangrove/)).
- **The finale is Lucia-only, at night,** near Cottonmouth Lake. Kirk hands you a unique tranquilliser rifle, the **Mister Mangrove Laird 350**, and one ending choice pays **$10,000**; the others pay nothing extra ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-help-kirk-find-mr-mangrove/)). Save before the choice.

## Kyle and the McKittrick strawberry farm

- **How to start it:** early in Chapter 2, as Lucia, follow the **Check Out McKittrick Farm** marker to the farm's hangar. Kyle has a broken arm and needs a crop-duster pilot ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-check-out-mckittrick-farm-mission/)).
- **Crop-dusting tips:** fly low, come in from behind Kyle's truck in the direction it's driving rather than across it, hold the dust button through each pass, and turn wide if you overshoot. Land gently; he complains about damage ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-check-out-mckittrick-farm-mission/)). New to flying? [Where to find a plane early →](/news/gta-6-first-plane-helicopter-location-how-to-fly-early/)
- **More jobs follow:** Collect Chemicals for Kyle, then a check-in on Kyle later in the chapter ([OutputLag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-check-out-mckittrick-farm-mission/)).
- **Eat the strawberries.** Afterwards, the mutated strawberries on his farm raise your **maximum stamina by a few percent** ([GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-mutated-strawberries)). More stamina: [gym guide →](/news/gta-6-gym-workouts-strength-stamina-guide/)
- **The ending is a choice.** LADbible says one option pays **$5,000** and another pays more in a different way ([LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-secret-missions-5-best-056826-20261124)). Make a manual save first: [how to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)

Both chains count as Strangers & Freaks side quests, which matter for [100% completion](/news/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist-requirements/).
