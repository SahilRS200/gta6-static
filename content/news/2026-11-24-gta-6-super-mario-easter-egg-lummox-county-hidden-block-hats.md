GTA 6 hides a nod to Nintendo's plumbers: **an invisible block above some ruins in Lummox County that drops a red "J" cap for Jason and a green "L" cap for Lucia**, with a familiar little tune when you hit it ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-includes-a-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-2000759536), [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114843/gta-6-has-a-hidden-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-that-lets-jason-and-lucia-dress-like-nintendos-iconic-plumbers/index.html)). Player xGarbett found it and shared it on X; Kotaku went there and confirmed it. It's a free-roam secret. **No story spoilers here.**

## Where it is

- **Northern Leonida, in Lummox County, past Mount Kalaga.** Follow a twisty road that seems to lead nowhere to some brick and stone ruins ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114843/gta-6-has-a-hidden-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-that-lets-jason-and-lucia-dress-like-nintendos-iconic-plumbers/index.html), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175580-gta-6-easter-egg-lets-jason-and-lucia-wear-mario-and-luigi-style-caps.html)).

## How to make the block appear

1. **Use the bong on the purple tarp** in the ruins.
2. **Climb to the top of the ruins.**
3. **Stand in the smoke rising from one of the columns.** A near-invisible block appears just above your head ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114843/gta-6-has-a-hidden-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-that-lets-jason-and-lucia-dress-like-nintendos-iconic-plumbers/index.html), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175580-gta-6-easter-egg-lets-jason-and-lucia-wear-mario-and-luigi-style-caps.html)).

## How to break it

- **Jump into it.** Nintendo Life says a jump makes it burst ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/random-gta-6-hides-an-adorable-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-in-its-massive-world)).
- **Jump didn't work? Shoot it.** xGarbett couldn't break it by jumping as Jason, so he shot it, and the caps still came out ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175580-gta-6-easter-egg-lets-jason-and-lucia-wear-mario-and-luigi-style-caps.html)).

## Wearing the caps

- **One cap each:** red "J" for Jason, green "L" for Lucia. Kotaku's reporter picked them up, called Lucia over, then went to a clothing shop to put them on ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114843/gta-6-has-a-hidden-mario-and-luigi-easter-egg-that-lets-jason-and-lucia-dress-like-nintendos-iconic-plumbers/index.html)).
