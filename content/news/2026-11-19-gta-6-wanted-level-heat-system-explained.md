Getting chased already? GTA 6's police work differently from GTA V. In the old game, cops forgot you the moment the stars faded. In GTA 6 they **remember what they saw**: your clothes, your car, and whether Jason and Lucia were together. Here's how it works, without story spoilers.

## Six stars, four states

GTA 6 has **six wanted stars**, one more than GTA V and the first six-star system since Chinatown Wars in 2009 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-wanted-system-explained/)). You only get stars if someone sees the crime and can identify you, or an alarm goes off ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/wanted-system-explained/)). The way the stars look tells you what the police are doing:

- **Hollow:** a crime was reported but police don't know it was you. Leave before they arrive.
- **Solid white:** they've identified you and can see you.
- **Flashing white:** you broke line of sight; they're searching your last known spot.
- **Red:** you escaped, but the area stays a **"Hot Area"**. Go back while matching the description and the chase can restart. At six stars, the whole map is hot.

## How do you get six stars?

Only by going on a full rampage. Reaching **six stars takes killing a lot of civilians or police**, VGTimes reports, citing a Shazoo guide; stealing a cargo jet at Vice City International gets you to four stars without hurting anyone, and attacking people and police on top of that pushes it to five. Police, NOOSE units, boats and aircraft then chase you, and **at six stars they don't stop until you die** ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175224-gta-6s-six-star-wanted-level-takes-extreme-violence-to-trigger.html)). The post-story wanted-level cheat only goes up to **five** stars; the sixth has to be earned. Crimes like these also push your [Criminal Profile](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/) towards Irredeemable.

## What the red icons mean

Icons under the stars show what police know: **a weapon**, **your physical description**, **your clothing**, **a duo** (they're looking for Jason and Lucia together), **CCTV footage**, and **your vehicle** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-wanted-system-explained/)).

## How to lose the heat

1. **Change clothes.** Keep a spare outfit in your car's trunk (save it there from the safehouse menu first). [How to change clothes →](/news/gta-6-how-to-change-clothes-outfits-hair-barber/)
2. **Change your look.** A hat, glasses or a bandana changes your description.
3. **Ditch the car.** Swap vehicles to clear the vehicle icon; newer cars may have GPS trackers.
4. **Split up.** Separating Jason and Lucia clears the duo icon.
5. **Don't escalate.** More violence during the chase raises your stars ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/wanted-system-explained/)).
6. **Repaint.** A Pay 'n' Spray respray helps the cops ease off ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/grand-theft-auto-6-review/)). Planning a job? See [how to rob a store and get away](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-store-safe-code-cameras-escape/).

Rockstar hasn't published exactly which crimes trigger each star level, so treat the thresholds as something players are still mapping out.
