GTA 6 has a proper ghost story: a haunted lighthouse, two ghostly keepers and a wreck with a **Legendary melee weapon**, the Cursed Knife, at the bottom of the sea. Here's the route, based on [VICE's guide](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-cursed-knife-ghost-ship-easter-egg/) and [GameSpot's](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-ghost-ship-haunted-lighthouse/). It's a side secret: no story spoilers.

## Before you go

- **Where:** St. Lucia Lighthouse on **Little Hardin Island**, just north of Vinewood Beach. Swim or boat across (VICE).
- **When:** only between **10 PM and 4 AM in-game**. Arrive earlier and nothing happens (VICE).
- **Save first.** VICE says the boat trigger can be finicky, so a manual save saves you a long night. [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)

## Step by step

1. **Climb to the top of the lighthouse** after 10 PM and watch two ghosts fight until both fall off the edge.
2. **Climb down and pick up the photo** of the two sailors, then flip it over.
3. **Go to the abandoned house** just west of the lighthouse, climb the stairs and break the door down.
4. **Take the sailors' diary** from the fireplace (turn right inside), read it and close it.
5. **Take the speedboat** parked across from the island.
6. **Head straight east** of the lighthouse. You're in the right spot when fog rolls in and the engine cuts out.
7. **Dive straight down** in front of the boat to the sunken wreck and pick up the glowing knife.

That's it: the **Cursed Knife** goes into your inventory as a Legendary melee weapon (VICE).

## Tips

- **Engine won't cut out?** Circle the area slowly, or reload your save and try the next night.
- **Want to see the ship itself?** Go back up the lighthouse and look out with binoculars (VICE).
