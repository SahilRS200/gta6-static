If you're buying GTA 6 on the PlayStation Store in India tonight, there's a small bonus with a deadline. **PlayStation's offer terms say a digital purchase of GTA 6 made by 19 November 2026 comes with one free month of GTA+**, Rockstar's subscription. [YourStory also flagged the offer on PlayStation India](https://yourstory.com/2026/11/gta-6-is-here-everything-indian-players-need-to-know).

## The offer, in short

- **What you get:** one month of GTA+ at no extra cost, [per PlayStation's terms page](https://www.playstation.com/en-ae/support/games/gta-vi-offer-terms/).
- **Which copies count:** the **digital Standard or Ultimate Edition** bought on the PlayStation Store. An Ultimate Edition upgrade doesn't count. Boxed copies from Blinkit, Zepto or Amazon are outside these terms.
- **Buy by:** 19 November 2026.
- **Claim by:** 31 March 2027, at a time you choose.
- **Where:** all PlayStation country stores except a short list (Bahrain, China, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar and Russia). India is not on that list.

## How to claim it

1. Look for the confirmation email from your purchase. It has the redemption steps.
2. Open the **GTA+ page on the PlayStation Store** and follow the on-screen steps. The claim itself costs nothing.
3. One claim per account, and it can't be moved to another account.

## The catch: it auto-renews

After the free month, GTA+ turns into a **paid monthly subscription at the regular price**. The claim screen shows when billing starts. If you don't want it, cancel in **Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Payment and Subscriptions → Subscriptions** before that date.

**Tip:** there's no rush to claim. GTA 6 has no online mode at launch ([what we know →](/news/gta-6-online-multiplayer-not-at-launch/)), and you have until 31 March 2027, so you can start the free month whenever it suits you. Only the purchase has to happen by 19 November.
