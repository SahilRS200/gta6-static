Driving past one of Leonida's huge casinos and wondering how to get in? **You can't.** GTA 6 has at least two large casinos that can't be entered, and they don't come up in the story or in any side mission, Kotaku reports ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6s-map-contains-areas-that-could-be-used-in-the-future-says-rockstar-2000756338)). No spoilers below.

## Why there's no gambling

Rockstar's vice president of design, Stephen Donkin, told Kotaku that Jason and Lucia's story "didn't feel like the right place" to bring in gambling, especially given how carefully the game's economy is balanced. That fits what players are finding: money matters more in GTA 6 than in earlier games, with car repairs and fuel costing cash ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/gta-6-is-here-mandy-a-jet-skiing-dog-and-8-more-things-players-are-talking-about-1.500752990)). A casino you could reload your way through would break that.

## The other places you can't use yet

Kotaku lists more spots that are built but unused for now: a very detailed **zoo**, a large **graveyard** and a small town in the north. Donkin said the map "has been built for the long term" and that a few areas give Rockstar "options in the future" if it has a gameplay or story need for them.

That is **not** an announcement. Rockstar has not confirmed any DLC, any casino update or a date for GTA 6 Online. Kotaku's suggestion that these places could be used in expansions or an online mode is its own guess. We track what's actually confirmed on [What's next](/whats-next/).
