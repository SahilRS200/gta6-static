Bored of the regular dial? GTA 6 has **a secret radio station you have to unlock yourself**: **The Vault**, an electronic and rave station tied to a Vice City nightclub. It takes a couple of minutes and one phone scan, and it counts towards 100% completion. **No story spoilers.**

## How to unlock The Vault station

1. **Go to The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City.** PowerPyx lists it under Downtown; Destructoid places it west of the NINE1NINE building. Use the map marker if the two descriptions don't match what you see ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=373924), [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-unlock-the-secret-vault-radio-station-in-gta-6/)).
2. **Walk in through the front entrance** and look for **a red poster with a mask** and "The Vault" written on it, just inside ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-unlock-the-secret-vault-radio-station-in-gta-6/), [Rankedboost](https://rankedboost.com/gta/radio-stations/)).
3. **Open the Waink app on your phone and scan the poster.** It's the same app you use to [scan cars before stealing them](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/). One poster is all this station needs ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=373924)).
4. **Tune in:** pick **Radio → The Vault** in any car, or listen on headphones ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=373924)).

## Bonus: The Sniper of La Perle playlist

A second hidden playlist needs **4 posters**, and the game doesn't show which ones you've already scanned, so do them in one trip ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=373924)):

- **La Perle:** by the cashier in **Aunt Zola's Soul Food**.
- **La Perle:** on the table next to the entrance in **TWA Ponya Tattoo**.
- **La Perle:** on the counter beside the clerk in **Drip Drops**.
- **Crosstown:** in **Jack of Hearts**, second floor, eastern recording booth, on a crate.

Then play it from **On Demand → The Sniper of La Perle**. Check progress under **Progress → Collections → Exploration → Unique Playlists**; both playlists count towards the 100% checklist ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=373924)).
