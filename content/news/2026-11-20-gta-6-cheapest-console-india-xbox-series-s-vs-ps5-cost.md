Don't own a console yet and want in on GTA 6? Two Indian outlets have added up what it actually costs to start playing. The short answer: **an Xbox Series S is the cheapest way in by a long way, but it's also the weakest version of the game.**

## What each setup costs

- **Xbox Series S:** from ₹31,990 (Gadgets 360 tracker), or ₹38,000–40,000 at Amazon, Flipkart and Croma. With the ₹5,999 game: **about ₹38,000–46,000**.
- **PS5 Slim Digital:** ₹64,990. With the game: **about ₹71,000**.
- **PS5 Slim Disc:** about ₹69,990. With the game: about ₹76,000.
- **PS5 Slim Disc, game and a power backup:** about **₹91,983**, WION's day-one estimate.

Figures from [The Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/technology/gta-vi-is-finally-here-but-can-india-afford-to-play-it) and [WION](https://www.wionews.com/photos/want-to-play-gta-6-here-s-the-estimated-gaming-setup-cost-in-india-1795039591888). Console prices move often, so check before you pay. Remember the GTA 6 box in India holds a download code, not a disc, so a Disc Edition PS5 gains you nothing for this game.

## The catch with the Series S

The Series S saves you more than ₹30,000, but Digital Foundry found it runs GTA 6 at **720p internally** with **serious frame drops**, down to about 20fps in the heaviest fights ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-xbox-series-s-performance-analysis-admires-the-effort-but-says-significant-frame-drops-are-a-problem/)). It plays, but a PS5 holds 30fps far better. Compare all four versions in [PS5 vs PS5 Pro vs Xbox](/news/gta-6-ps5-vs-ps5-pro-xbox-performance-digital-foundry/). The PS5 Pro isn't officially sold in India; WION lists grey-market imports at ₹80,000 and up, so buy only from a seller you trust.

## Why it matters in India

- The Daily Pioneer cites industry estimates (via ET BrandEquity) of about **7.5 lakh PS5s** and about **50,000 Xboxes** in India, roughly 1 lakh of those PS5s grey imports. Those are unofficial numbers.
- India's whole games market earned **$1.04 billion in 2025 across 51.1 crore players**, according to the GDAI GameDev Outlook 2026, about $2 per player per year.
- ₹5,999 works out to roughly $62.60, which the paper reports is among the lowest GTA 6 prices worldwide. That comparison is reported, not verified by Sony.
