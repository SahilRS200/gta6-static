## Update, 2:37 AM IST: the peak is now about 7.9 million

The record kept climbing as GTA 6 unlocked across Asia. Streams Charts now puts the preliminary peak at **about 7.89 million concurrent viewers** across platforms, reached after the **Japan and South Korea** unlock. That's roughly 2.5 million more than the first count at the New Zealand launch ([Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/gta-vi-breaks-streaming-launch-records-within-minutes-its-release), [Streams Charts on X](https://x.com/StreamsCharts/status/2123114732332720248)).

- **By platform:** Twitch about **3.29 million**, YouTube about **3.19 million**, Kick about **1.5 million**. Streams Charts says the numbers are still preliminary ([Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/gta-vi-breaks-streaming-launch-records-within-minutes-its-release)).
- **More than 200 creators** set new personal peak-viewer records, Streams Charts says, and several dozen saw ten times their usual peak.
- **India's Mythpat** (Mithilesh Patankar) reached a peak of **125,360 viewers**, about **1.25 lakh**, Win.gg reports. For comparison, the biggest single-channel peaks it lists are RRaenee (273,899) and Windah Basudara (266,991) ([Win.gg](https://win.gg/gta-6-smashes-streaming-records/)).
- **Europe, Brazil and the US hadn't unlocked yet** when the 7.8 million mark was posted, so the final number is likely to go higher.

## First count, at the New Zealand unlock

GTA 6 had only unlocked in one small country, and it had already become **the most-watched game launch in streaming history**. When New Zealand went live at **4:30 PM IST**, millions of people around the world tuned in to watch others play.

## The numbers

- **About 5.3 million peak viewers across livestreaming platforms**, according to preliminary data from analytics firm [Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/gta-vi-breaks-streaming-launch-records-within-minutes-its-release).
- **Over 2.5 million on Twitch alone, within about 30 minutes** of the unlock ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/there-are-already-25-million-people-watching-gta-6-on-twitch/), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/174996-gta-6-draws-more-than-2.5-million-twitch-viewers-within-30-minutes.html)).
- **The old launch record** belonged to Valorant's closed beta, at about 1.74 million concurrent viewers in April 2020. Streams Charts says GTA 6 has passed it.
- **Biggest single-player game on Twitch.** Hogwarts Legacy's 2023 peak was about 1.3 million. Sources differ slightly on its exact figure.

These were **early, preliminary figures**, and they rose as the launch reached Japan and South Korea (see the update above). League of Legends' all-time Twitch peak of about 3.1 million had not clearly been passed in the first reports.

## What it means for India

At the time, most of those viewers couldn't play yet, and many were in India. Our unlock came at **12:00 AM IST** ([exact time](/gta-6-release-time/india/)). If you can't play yet, YouTube's 24-hour stream with Total Gaming, Payal Gaming, Sharkshe and Mythpat runs from 10:30 AM IST on 19 November.
