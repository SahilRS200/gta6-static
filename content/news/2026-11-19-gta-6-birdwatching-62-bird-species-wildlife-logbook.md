Need a break from the cops? GTA 6 has a quiet side activity that has the internet's birdwatchers excited: you can spot birds through binoculars and log them. Spoiler-free, here's what is confirmed.

## What's in the game

- **62 bird species.** Rockstar North's Rob Nelson gave the number to The Guardian during a studio visit, as reported by Fox News and Gfinity ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/outkick-culture/reddits-birding-community-goes-wild-unique-grand-theft-auto-6-feature-comes-light), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-will-feature-62-bird-species-and-a-trackable-wildlife-logbook)).
- **Binoculars plus a wildlife logbook.** In the preview, Jason looks at a gull on a beach post through binoculars, and the sighting is recorded in a **wildlife logbook** you fill in over the course of the game.
- **Birds are only part of it.** Gfinity, citing earlier reporting, puts Leonida's wildlife at **over 170 animal species**, including about 30 land mammals plus fish and reptiles.
- **Short of real Florida.** PC Gamer points out that Florida has more than 500 native bird species, so 62 is a curated slice ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/twitchers-rejoice-gta-6-will-have-62-different-species-of-birds-though-that-falls-a-bit-short-of-the-over-500-native-species-found-in-florida/)).

## What isn't confirmed yet

Rockstar hasn't published the full list of 62 birds, where to find them, or any reward for completing the logbook. Gfinity says the logbook looks similar to Red Dead Redemption 2's animal compendium, but that's a comparison, not a confirmed rule. Treat online "all bird locations" lists with care until they're tested.

## Why players care

A post on Reddit's r/birding asking who was looking forward to it passed **1,700 upvotes**, Dexerto reports, with several people saying Red Dead Redemption 2 first got them into real birding ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/birdwatchers-are-buying-gta-6-just-to-look-at-birds-3428480/)).
