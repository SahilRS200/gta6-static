Going for the Platinum, or just curious what GTA 6 tracks? Here is the trophy list in short, with the one thing to do early so you don't need a second playthrough. No story spoilers.

## The list in numbers

- **55 trophies:** 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox the same 55 achievements are worth 1,000 Gamerscore ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series)).
- **The Platinum is "Vice Got a Price!"** You get it for earning every other trophy ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/)).
- **No online trophies.** Everything is single-player, and none of it needs DLC ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/)).
- **About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10,** by PowerPyx's estimate: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 for the 100% checklist and around 10 for cleanup. It's long rather than hard. Story-only times are in [how long GTA 6 is](/news/gta-6-how-long-to-beat-story-hours/).

## Do this early: the possible missable

- **Positive Mental Attitude** is the only trophy flagged as possibly missable. Early in Chapter 1, on a morning at Jason's house, find Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony and **accept her optional offer to meditate**. PowerPyx and GTABase say it isn't confirmed the trophy needs it, but it costs you a few minutes, so do it ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series)).
- Everything else can be done after the story, both guides say.

## The hard ones

- **A Long Road (Gold):** 100% completion. It covers every robbery, underwater discovery, Shooting Range gold and drone race, plus part of other activities. PowerPyx says the bar is closer to 60% of everything in the game. [All 35 requirements →](/news/gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist-requirements/)
- **The Shootist (Silver):** a gold medal in all 42 Shooting Range challenges across 12 ranges. Ranges also raise your Focus level ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series)).
- **Cunning Stunts (Silver):** any 250 of the 397 stunts; flying under bridges is the quickest way. The 100% checklist wants 300. [Stunt guide →](/news/gta-6-stunt-jumps-locations-cunning-stunts-trophy/)
- **You WAiNKer! (Silver):** scan 100 spots with the WAiNK app; landmarks, curiosities and sculptures all count. The same app scans cars to steal: [how that works](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/).

## Don't wreck your run

- **Cheats switch off trophies and saving** on PS5 (on Xbox, achievements for that session), and they only unlock after the story anyway: [GTA 6 cheats explained](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/).
- **Keep manual saves.** There's no mission replay menu, so a fresh slot before each chapter helps: [saves and replays](/news/gta-6-replay-missions-no-replay-menu-save-slots/).
- **Wildlife counts toward 100%.** Start the bird logbook early: [birdwatching guide](/news/gta-6-birdwatching-62-bird-species-wildlife-logbook/).
- **Hidden trophies exist.** "The Truth Is Out There" comes from a night-time UFO event near Port Gellhorn: [how to trigger it](/news/gta-6-ufo-location-truth-is-out-there-trophy/). "Capital Offense" means driving out through a car showroom's glass: [the steps](/news/gta-6-capital-offense-trophy-showroom-banshee-key-cloner/).
- **How rare is the Platinum?** One week in, a single PSNProfiles player had it, and under 1% had the final-mission trophy: [the trophy data →](/news/gta-6-how-many-players-finished-story-trophy-data-under-1-percent/)
