Loved a mission and want to play it again? In GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 you could pick it from a replay list. **GTA 6 doesn't have one.** IGN's reviewer, who finished the game, found no option to replay completed story missions from a menu, [ComicBook reports](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/gta-6-removes-a-major-gta-5-feature-but-the-reasoning-makes-sense/). No story spoilers below.

## Why it's missing

- **Rockstar hasn't said.** ComicBook's guess is the story itself: choices you make can change how later missions play out, so dropping you into one mission out of order could clash with your own playthrough. Treat that as a theory, not Rockstar's word.
- **It could change later.** Nothing is announced, so don't wait for a patch.

## How to replay a mission anyway

- **Save manually before missions you care about.** GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave, [KeenGamer explains](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-save-in-gta-6-manual-saves-autosave-and-safehouses/). Use a fresh slot just before a big mission and name it in your head ("before the heist").
- **Reload that slot later** to play the mission again. Anything you did after that save is not lost, as long as you also keep a newer save in another slot.
- **Don't overwrite your main save.** Keep one slot as your "real" progress and use the others as checkpoints.
