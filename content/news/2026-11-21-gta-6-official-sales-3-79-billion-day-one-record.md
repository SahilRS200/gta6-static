It's official now. On Friday, 20 November, **Rockstar and Take-Two said GTA 6's retail sales reached $3.79 billion by the end of launch day**, 19 November. That's more than ₹31,000 crore at today's exchange rates, and they call it **"the biggest entertainment launch in history"** across any medium, games, films or music ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-earns-3-79-billion-in-one-day-as-the-biggest-entertainment-launch-in-history/)).

## The numbers

- **$3.79 billion** in worldwide retail sales through the end of 19 November, a company estimate ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-3-79b-day-one-sales-biggest-launch)).
- **32.5 million copies sold before release**, Take-Two says, a record across all platforms. Reports differ on whether that is pre-orders alone or pre-orders plus launch day ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114806/gta-6-makes-dollars3-79-billion-with-32-5-million-copies-sold/index.html)).
- **Nearly five times GTA V**, which made about $800 million in its first 24 hours in 2013. For scale, *Avengers: Endgame* took about $1.2 billion over its opening weekend ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-3-79b-day-one-sales-biggest-launch)).
- **Take-Two shares rose about 2.7%** on Friday ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-3-79b-day-one-sales-biggest-launch), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4675294-take-two-says-gta-vi-posts-record-379b-in-day-one-sales)).

Rockstar founder Sam Houser said the studio is "incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world" ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-earns-3-79-billion-in-one-day-as-the-biggest-entertainment-launch-in-history/)).

## Read it with care

- **It's sales, not Take-Two's profit.** Sony, Microsoft and shops take their cut of every copy, and Take-Two didn't say exactly how it counted ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-3-79b-day-one-sales-biggest-launch)).
- **It's higher than the tracker estimates.** Sensor Tower had put day one at about 24.6 million digital copies and $2.36 billion; the official count includes physical code-in-box copies too. [The earlier estimates →](/news/gta-6-pre-orders-15-million-record/)
- **There's no India figure.** Neither Rockstar nor Sony has shared India sales. What we do know: Blinkit and Zepto sold out fast and PS5 stock went in days. [GTA 6 in India →](/gta-6-india/)
