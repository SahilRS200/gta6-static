If your PS5 controller is flashing low battery halfway through a GTA 6 evening, it isn't broken. **A fully charged DualSense lasts about 4 hours 30 minutes in GTA 6**, Game Informer found in its own test with the controller that came with a PS5 Pro. **The DualSense Edge does worse, about 3 hours 40 minutes**, going by player reports ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/psa/2026/11/26/is-your-dualsense-running-low-playing-gta-vi), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175999-gta-6-leaves-dualsense-edge-with-less-than-four-hours-of-battery.html)).

## Why it drains so fast

- **GTA 6 uses everything the DualSense has:** the adaptive triggers, haptic vibration and the built-in speaker, nearly all the time ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/psa/2026/11/26/is-your-dualsense-running-low-playing-gta-vi)).
- **It's about half of what you'd get elsewhere.** In a game like Phantom Blade 0, the same controller lasts roughly twice as long ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/psa/2026/11/26/is-your-dualsense-running-low-playing-gta-vi)).
- **Older controllers drop faster.** Battery age, a replaced battery and what you're doing in the open world all change the number.

## How to make a charge last longer

1. **Turn down vibration and triggers.** On PS5 go to **Settings > Accessories > Controllers (General)** and lower **Vibration Intensity** and **Trigger Effect Intensity**. DualSense Edge owners find these under **Settings > Accessories > DualSense Edge Wireless Controller** ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/hardware/adjust-general-controller-settings/)).
2. **Mute the controller speaker.** Game Informer says switching off triggers, haptics and speaker should stretch the battery, though it didn't measure by how much.
3. **Keep a USB-C cable nearby.** Plug in when the warning shows and keep playing; a long cable from the console is the simplest fix for marathon sessions.

You lose some feel with the triggers off: weapons and cars give less feedback. Try a lower intensity first rather than switching them off.
