**Playing GTA 6 on Xbox Series X/S? Turn off automatic capture uploads before your next session.** Players are reporting **two-day Xbox suspensions** after their GTA 6 clips and screenshots were uploaded to Xbox's servers on their own and flagged under its content rules, Pure Xbox reports ([Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-players-warned-to-turn-off-auto-uploads-or-risk-getting-banned-on-xbox)). PS5 players aren't affected by this.

## What players are reporting

- **A beach clip was enough.** The first warning came from a Reddit user who said their capture showed "standard" Vice City beach gameplay with NPCs in swimsuits and no nudity, yet it brought a Terms of Service notice and a two-day ban ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175310-gta-6-players-warned-to-disable-xbox-auto-uploads-after-suspension-reports.html)).
- **Why it happens:** Xbox uploads every screenshot and clip automatically by default, and uploaded captures are checked against rules on nudity, sexual content, drugs and extreme violence. GTA 6 is an 18+ game with plenty of all of these ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175310-gta-6-players-warned-to-disable-xbox-auto-uploads-after-suspension-reports.html)).
- **No appeal, so far.** The rulings appear automated and can't be appealed, and it's not clear what exactly triggered them ([Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-players-warned-to-turn-off-auto-uploads-or-risk-getting-banned-on-xbox)).
- **No word from Microsoft or Rockstar yet.** These are player reports; neither company has commented ([Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-players-warned-to-turn-off-auto-uploads-or-risk-getting-banned-on-xbox)).

## The fix: stop automatic uploads (1 minute)

1. Press the **Xbox button** and open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Preferences > Capture & share**.
3. Set **Automatically upload** to **Don't upload** ([Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-players-warned-to-turn-off-auto-uploads-or-risk-getting-banned-on-xbox)).

Your captures still save on the console, and you can still upload a single clip by hand when you choose ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175310-gta-6-players-warned-to-disable-xbox-auto-uploads-after-suspension-reports.html)). Check what's in a shot before you share it, especially from [Photo Studio](/news/gta-6-photo-mode-photo-studio-selfie-how-to/).

## This has happened before

In 2024 Xbox banned Baldur's Gate 3 players whose console auto-uploaded clips of that game's nude scenes. Microsoft confirmed it then and said it would lift first-offence suspensions ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/xbox-confirms-its-banning-baldurs-gate-3-players-who-auto-upload-sex-clips)). Auto-upload is still on by default. We'll update this page if Microsoft responds on GTA 6.
