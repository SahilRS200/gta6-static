Stuck on a shootout, or handing the controller to a younger sibling or a parent? **GTA 6 has no difficulty levels** — no Easy, Normal or Hard. Instead, Rockstar tucked a long list of assists into the settings menu, spread across several tabs ([Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2026/11/18/early-looks-at-gta-vi-accessibility-features/)). These are the ones worth knowing.

## Make it easier

- **Lower enemy health, your damage taken and vehicle damage.** Each is its own toggle, so you can soften only the shootouts and leave the rest alone ([Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2026/11/18/early-looks-at-gta-vi-accessibility-features/)).
- **Auto-drive.** The game can drive for you, and there are separate assists for drive-by and general shooting.
- **Simplified minigames,** plus a **quick-time-event tap assist** with five levels.
- **Holds become taps.** Many hold-the-button actions can be switched to toggles, and a hold-delay setting stops accidental presses.
- **Aim assist** can be tuned; there are three controller presets and adjustable stick and trigger sensitivity.
- **Lost the thread?** You can review mission objectives and recent dialogue.

One thing Can I Play That? could **not** confirm is full button remapping.

## See and hear it better

- **Subtitles** can be made bigger, with speaker names and background captions optional.
- **Text size, UI colours, objective-marker and map-icon size** are all adjustable; the minimap can stay on permanently, with a compass on top.
- **High contrast mode** paints characters, enemies and items you can use in contrasting colours. Kotaku notes you can't change those colours.
- **Colourblind mode** boosts the colour of focus trails.
- **Feel sick or tired?** Turn down motion blur, head bob and screen effects, and soften flashbangs.
- **Tinnitus reduction** cuts the ringing after explosions, and there's a mono audio toggle.
- **Gore** can be reduced, and dismemberment turned off.

## Menu narration and audio description

GTA 6 reads out most menus and UI (English only), and cutscenes have audio descriptions on their own toggle ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/live-updates-on-gta-6-the-biggest-launch-in-gaming-history-2000758030), [Can I Play That?](https://caniplaythat.com/2026/11/18/early-looks-at-gta-vi-accessibility-features/)). Accessibility site Double Tap says this still isn't enough for a totally blind player to roam the open world or finish missions alone: there's no navigation help or audio cue guiding you to a destination ([Double Tap](https://doubletaponair.com/gta-vi-brings-audio-description-and-narration-but-totally-blind-players-will-not-be-able-to-play-it-alone/)).

These early write-ups were put together from reviews, guides and streams, not Rockstar's own list, so a setting may sit under a slightly different name in your menu.
