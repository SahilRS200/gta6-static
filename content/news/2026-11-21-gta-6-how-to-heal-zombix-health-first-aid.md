Health in GTA 6 only creeps back slowly on its own, so a big shootout can leave Jason or Lucia weak going into the next one. Here are all the ways to get health back, fastest first, with PS5 and Xbox buttons. No story spoilers.

## 1. Zombix: the quickest full heal

- **What it is.** Zombix is the game's healing drink and the best way to fully heal in one go ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)).
- **Where to get it.** Buy it in shops, such as grocery stores, or pick it up around Leonida ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)). Stock up before a mission rather than mid-fight.
- **How to use it.** Consumables sit on the item wheel: hold **L1** (Xbox: **LB**) and press **Square / X** or **Triangle / Y** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/)).

## 2. Adrenaline: a short boost in a fight

Adrenaline products don't heal you for good. They raise your maximum health and cut the damage you take for a short time ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)), or briefly make you near-invulnerable ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/)). Save them for the moment a fight turns bad.

## 3. Food and drink

Food and drinks from shops, restaurants or a safehouse kitchen restore some health ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/)). Only items with the **health icon** do it, and some briefly extend your health bar too ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)).

## 4. First aid kits

You'll find first aid kits in safehouses and other buildings. They work the moment you pick them up and can't be carried, so remember where they are ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/)).

## 5. Sleep or just stay out of trouble

- **Sleep in a safehouse bed** to refill health ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)). [How sleeping works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/)
- **Avoid damage for a while** and health slowly comes back by itself. It is slow, so use it only when nothing else is to hand ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-heal-in-gta-6/), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-how-to-heal)).
