Which console you play GTA 6 on at 12 AM IST matters more than many expected. **Digital Foundry has published its technical analysis**, and while every version targets 30fps, they don't all hold it equally. Here's the short version, with no story spoilers.

## PS5 Pro: the best way to play

- **A near-flawless 30fps.** Digital Foundry's John Linneman says he played for over 50 hours without a single dip outside deliberate stress tests, [VGC reports](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-base-ps5-performance-analysis-shows-struggle-to-maintain-30-fps-at-all-times/). The worst point in the hardest stress test was 24fps, and it recovered quickly.
- **Sharp image.** PSSR upscaling "convincingly mimics native 4K", with perfect frame pacing, [Push Square writes](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-base-ps5-performance-suffers-minor-and-sustained-frame-rate-drops-but-its-unwavering-on-ps5-pro).

## Base PS5: fine most of the time, dips in heavy action

- **Normal play mostly holds 30fps**, but there are small, noticeable dips when driving fast through the city.
- **Big firefights with explosions** can pull it into the **mid-20s**, and those drops last until you leave the area.
- **Softer image** than the Pro, especially in small details. There are no graphics or performance options to change.

## Xbox Series X and Series S

- **Series X** renders at 1440p internally and mostly holds 30fps, with occasional dips in busy combat and chases, [per VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-xbox-series-s-performance-analysis-admires-the-effort-but-says-significant-frame-drops-are-a-problem/).
- **Series X tip: set the console to 120Hz.** With a VRR-capable TV, Digital Foundry found the Series X version "slightly smoother" than PS5 when the Xbox is set to 120Hz, [Pure Xbox reports](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/three-big-takeaways-from-digital-foundrys-gta-6-analysis-for-xbox-series-xs). The game still targets 30fps; VRR just smooths the dips. You'll find both in the Xbox's TV and display options.
- **Series S** renders at **720p** internally and "has serious frame rate problems," Digital Foundry's Oliver Mackenzie says. Driving often sits in the mid-to-high 20s, typical combat stays in the mid-20s, and the toughest fights can drop to **about 20fps**. Walking and cutscenes are fine.
- Digital Foundry hopes a patch improves things, but Rockstar hasn't announced one.

## What it means for you in India

- **On a PS5 Pro:** nothing to worry about.
- **On a base PS5 or Series X:** expect a solid game with the odd stutter in chaotic moments. Turn on TV Game Mode to keep input lag down, and on Series X with a VRR TV, set 120Hz (above).
- **On a Series S:** it is playable, but this is the weakest version. If you have a choice of consoles at home, Digital Foundry advises playing elsewhere for now.
