Missed the GTA 6 pre-order window? The **Vintage Vice City Pack**, which came free with pre-orders and digital purchases made by **19 November**, is now on the **PlayStation Store and Xbox Store as a $4.99 add-on**. The store listings went up on 20 November, the day after the offer closed ([GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-just-turned-a-preorder-bonus-into-a-499-add-on-040b), [Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/23/gta-6s-vintage-vice-city-pre-order-bonus-returns-as-paid-dlc/), [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/gta-6-vintage-vice-city-pack-deadline)). Rockstar hasn't commented on the change. **No story spoilers.**

## What's in the pack

- **Shore Court Garage near Ocean Beach,** with a **'55 Vapid Stanier** inside, a weapons locker and a spot to keep stolen goods until a fence collects them ([Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/23/gta-6s-vintage-vice-city-pre-order-bonus-returns-as-paid-dlc/)).
- **Retro outfits and hairstyles:** a pastel linen suit for Jason, a red sequin dress with curls for Lucia.
- **A palm-tree weapon pattern** based on Tommy Vercetti's shirt from 2002's Vice City.
- It needs the base game, on **PS5 or Xbox Series X/S** ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175479-gta-vis-vintage-vice-city-pre-order-pack-is-now-a-4.99-dlc.html)).

## What it costs in India

The outlets report the **US price of $4.99**; we haven't seen a confirmed **₹ price** yet. Check the GTA 6 add-ons page on the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store on your console before you buy, and pay only through the official store. **Never buy "pre-order codes" from resellers.**

## Already have it?

- **Pre-ordered or bought digitally by 19 November?** You should already own the pack: look for the garage on your map. Don't buy it twice.
- **The free GTA+ month isn't part of the $4.99 pack,** Power Up Gaming says; that was a separate perk for digital buyers ([our GTA+ explainer](/news/gta-6-free-gta-plus-month-offer-deadline/)).
- **Ultimate Edition** bonuses are a different set that unlock chapter by chapter ([what unlocks when](/news/gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses-unlock-chapters/)).

## Is it worth $4.99?

It's cosmetic plus a garage, so it doesn't change the game. Want Tommy's shirt for free? Our [Vice City portal guide](/news/gta-6-vice-city-portal-tommy-vercetti-shirt-easter-egg/) shows how to earn it in-game. More on prices in India: [GTA 6 India hub](/gta-6-india/).
