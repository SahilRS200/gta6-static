"Capital Offense" is one of GTA 6's hidden trophies, and for days most trophy lists only had its one-line clue: **"Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style"**, a Bronze on PS5 ([Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/achievements/20850)). GTA Culture's mystery tracker still listed it as having no published method on 24 November ([GTA Culture](https://gtaculture.com/guides/gta-6-secrets-and-mysteries)). Into Indie Games has now published a route that worked for them. No story spoilers.

## What you need first

- **Reach Chapter 3** and finish Randy's side mission **"Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs"** as Lucia. Randy has you download the **Key Cloner** app, steal a car and deliver it; after that the app is yours for good ([Into Indie Games](https://intoindiegames.com/walkthroughs/tips-tricks/grand-theft-auto-6-gta6-unlock-the-capital-offense-trophy-achievement-key-cloner-vehicle-showroom/)).
- Why it matters: showroom cars are keyless, and keyless cars can't be hotwired. Our [car-stealing guide](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/) explains the security tiers.

## How to get it

1. Go to **Sunrise Premium Autos** on El Joven Blvd in northern Southside. It's easy to spot by its all-glass front.
2. Inside, find the **blue Bravado Banshee with a white stripe**.
3. Get in and start it with the **Key Cloner** when the prompt appears.
4. **Drive straight out through the showroom window.** The trophy (or Xbox achievement) should pop a few seconds after you leave ([Into Indie Games](https://intoindiegames.com/walkthroughs/tips-tricks/grand-theft-auto-6-gta6-unlock-the-capital-offense-trophy-achievement-key-cloner-vehicle-showroom/)).

## Good to know

- The guide's author isn't sure the Banshee is required, or that it must be this showroom; other cars and dealerships may work. The steps above are the ones confirmed to work, so use them.
- Smashing out of a showroom in a stolen car will draw the police: [how to lose them](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/).
- Make a manual save before you try, so a messy getaway doesn't cost you progress.
