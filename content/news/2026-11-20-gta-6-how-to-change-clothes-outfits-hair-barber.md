Bought a new jacket and can't find it? Picked a hat off someone and it vanished? GTA 6 handles clothes a little differently from GTA 5. Here's how it works, spoiler-free, based on [VGC's guides](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-clothes-in-gta-6/).

## Changing clothes

- **Your safehouse is your wardrobe.** Everything Jason and Lucia buy or earn from missions is stored there, and that's where you change ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-clothes-in-gta-6/)).
- **Clothes you grab in the world aren't kept yet.** A hat taken from a passer-by or an enemy is only saved once you get back to the safehouse.
- **Save outfits to your car, by hand.** You can keep several outfits in the trunk of Jason's and Lucia's cars, but the game doesn't do it for you, even after a shopping trip: save each outfit to the car from the safehouse menu.

## Why it matters: losing the cops

Police in GTA 6 remember what you were wearing, and VGC calls a change of outfit **the quickest way to lower your wanted level**. So stash a spare look in the trunk before you start trouble. ## Hair, beard and makeup

- **Barbers and stylists** across Leonida do haircuts and hair dye. For Jason they also change facial hair ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/how-to-change-hair-in-gta-6)).
- **Lucia's makeup and nails** are done at beauty parlours, marked with a lip icon on the map.
- Both have **signature hairstyles** plus standard ones, and some styles are sold only at certain shops.
- **Pre-order bonus:** if you got the Vice City Style pack, its haircuts, facial hair and makeup are applied at the same shops.
