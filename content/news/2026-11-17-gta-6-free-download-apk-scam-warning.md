## Update, 20 November: Kaspersky counts 27,416 GTA 6 malware hits

Security firm **Kaspersky says its software recorded 27,416 detections of GTA 6-related malware and unwanted apps** worldwide between June and November 2026 ([Kaspersky via Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/kaspersky-detects-over-27000-attacks-on-gamers-awaiting-gta-vi-release-2397235)). The bait: fake PC installers, "early pre-loads", activation key generators and cheat tools, plus fake pre-order and download sites that ask for payment or personal details. Kaspersky also found sellers calling themselves "CyberLeek" offering a supposed full build for $1,000 and a 24-hour "demo" for $100 ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/27000-reasons-not-to-download-gta-vi-for-pc/)).

Kaspersky's advice matches ours: buy only from the official console stores or known retailers, and never download a "pre-load" or "PC version" from a website, torrent or Discord link. The only real pre-load came through PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store. There is still **no PC release date** ([what we know](/news/gta-6-pc-release-date-gta-5-steam-players-rise/)).

## Update, 19 November: the "GTA 6 on PC" videos are fake too

Now that the game is out, a new wave of "GTA 6 running on PC" clips is going round. **No PS5 emulator can run GTA 6, and there is no unofficial PC port**, DSOGaming reports; it warns readers off PC torrent links ([DSOGaming](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/you-cannot-play-gta-6-with-a-ps5-emulator-and-there-isnt-an-unofficial-pc-port/)). One viral "port" claim from a days-old X account fell apart when users showed its footage was GTA 5 ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175107-gta-6-pc-port-claim-falls-apart-after-video-is-exposed-as-fake.html)). The PC clips that are real use **PS5 Remote Play**: the console runs the game and the PC only shows a video stream. That needs your own PS5 and your own copy. More on that in our [60fps frame-generation explainer](/news/gta-6-60fps-ps5-smoovplay-frame-generation-explained/).

Search "GTA 6 download" in India right now and you'll find APKs, "PC early access" links and Telegram channels promising the game for free. **All of it is fake.**

## The facts

- GTA 6 launches **only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S** (Rockstar).
- There is **no PC version** at launch and no PC date has been announced.
- There is **no mobile version.** Any "GTA 6 APK" is not GTA 6.

## The scams doing the rounds

- **"GTA 6 APK / mobile download"** — usually malware that steals your contacts, OTPs or UPI details.
- **"GTA 6 PC crack / early access"** — fake installers that bundle password stealers.
- **"Free PSN codes for GTA 6"** — survey scams that harvest your phone number and email.
- **Telegram / WhatsApp "leaked build" groups** — ask you to pay via UPI for a "link" that never works.
- **Cheap "NZ wallet codes"** for the New Zealand early-unlock trick — a classic grey-market trap.

## How to stay safe

1. Buy **only** from PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or a known retailer.
2. Never install an APK or `.exe` that claims to be GTA 6.
3. Never share an OTP or your PSN password for a "free code".
4. Turn on **two-step verification** on your PSN / Microsoft account.
5. Skip any "GTA 6 Online early access" offer: there is no online mode at launch. [Why →](/news/gta-6-online-multiplayer-not-at-launch/)
