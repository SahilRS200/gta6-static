The most painful lesson of GTA 6's first hours: **the cash in your pocket is not safe.** Until you open your first bank account, every dollar you carry is gone if you **die or get busted** by the police, and dying also costs you any stash bags you're holding ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)). Here's how to keep it. **No story spoilers.**

## Where to keep your money

- **Jason's bedroom floorboards.** Inside Jason's house, interact with the **loose floorboards** in his bedroom. It's a hidden stash where you can store cash. Drop money off whenever you pass by ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide), [Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/gaming/gta-6-6-gameplay-tricks-that-can-make-your-time-in-leonida-easier.html)).
- **Your bank account comes early.** An early story mission, roughly **one to two hours in**, gives you your first bank account. After that, banked money is safe ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
- **Don't rob banks or loan stores yet.** Their cash often carries **dye packs**, and the jammer you need to beat them only comes later in the story ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).

## More small things that save you trouble

- **Scan a car before you steal it.** The **WAiNK** phone app (hold Up on the D-pad) shows a car's security, what it sells for and what registering it costs ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
- **Read doors in Focus Mode.** A **white** marker means you can walk in; **red** means the door is alarmed ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide), [Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/gaming/gta-6-6-gameplay-tricks-that-can-make-your-time-in-leonida-easier.html)).
- **Feed Dolores.** Jason's pet snake gives you an early **Focus boost** when you feed her a mouse at home ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/gaming/gta-6-6-gameplay-tricks-that-can-make-your-time-in-leonida-easier.html)).
- **Watch the clock.** Some shops close at night and some missions only run at certain times. **Sleep at Jason's house** to skip ahead ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
- **Finish the Leonida Keys side jobs early.** Diving, fishing and your neighbour's requests are easiest while you're there; the drive back later takes **around 10 minutes** ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).
- **Call a mechanic from anywhere.** After an early mission unlocks repairs, you can order one almost anywhere on the map, for a fee ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/gaming/gta-6-6-gameplay-tricks-that-can-make-your-time-in-leonida-easier.html)).
