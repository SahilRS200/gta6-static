Bored of the rusty pickup? GTA 6 has quick cars hiding in plain sight, and one of the best is parked a minute from Jason's house. One thing first: **Rockstar hasn't published top speeds**, so every "fastest car" list is players and outlets judging by feel, not official numbers ([HotCars](https://www.hotcars.com/how-to-get-fastest-car-early-in-gta-6/), [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6-guide-the-10-fastest-cars-and-where-to-find-them)). No story spoilers below.

## The early pick: a nitrous Imponte Ruiner

- **What:** an **Imponte Ruiner** (the game's Trans Am) with a **supercharger through the bonnet and nitrous already fitted** — use it and blue flames shoot out the back ([HotCars](https://www.hotcars.com/how-to-get-fastest-car-early-in-gta-6/)).
- **Where:** a driveway near the **Cove Condominiums** sign in **Key Lento**, parked among pickups, about a minute's drive from Jason's house.
- **How to keep it:** steal it, take it to **Coastal Auto Care** (the mod shop near Jason's home) for a respray, park in Jason's usual spot at home, go in and **sleep**. When he wakes up, the car is saved for good ([HotCars](https://www.hotcars.com/how-to-get-fastest-car-early-in-gta-6/)). New to sleeping? See [how to sleep and skip time](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/).

## Supercars: head to Capri, Mariana County

GamingBolt's list of the currently known fastest cars puts most of the supercars in **mansion driveways in Capri**, the north-western peninsula of Mariana County ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6-guide-the-10-fastest-cars-and-where-to-find-them)):

- **Granforma Zorrusso** — a driveway with pillars on the western edge of the peninsula. A rare spawn.
- **Pegassi Vitello** and **Pfister Comet '88** — the pale green mansion on the western edge. Expect tougher security on the Vitello.
- **Grotti Furia** — a driveway on the southern edge.
- **Pegassi Tempesta** (often a police-livery version) — tends to spawn at the entrance of the **Eden Grove Mariana Hotels on Vice Beach**.
- **Grotti Cheetah '95** — **Ultimate Edition only**, and only some time after a Chapter 5 mission. See [when each Ultimate bonus unlocks](/news/gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses-unlock-chapters/).

Expensive cars usually carry alarms and trackers, so **scan them with the Waink app first**. [How stealing works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/)

## The film-car Easter egg

The green Eclipse-style car from *The Fast and the Furious* sits in a villa garage on **Vespucci Island**. You need a **Slim Jim**, which unlocks in **Chapter 2**; cut the garage's wall alarm before leaving, and registering it at a Pay 'n' Spray costs **$6,750** ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-paul-walker-mitsubishi-eclipse-location/)).
