Four days after **PM Modi's "India is playing a whole new game!" post**, GTA 6 has pulled in another Indian politician. **Congress MP Shashi Tharoor** (Thiruvananthapuram) tried the game for the first time in a video posted to YouTube and his Instagram, [Business Today reports](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/grand-theft-without-auto-shashi-tharoor-turns-gta-6-review-into-a-demonetisation-dig-at-modi-govt-569037-2026-11-23).

## What he said

- **On the game:** Tharoor said he is not a gamer, and after a short go decided GTA 6 was **"not for me"**.
- **On the name:** He questioned whether "Grand Theft Auto" makes sense in English.
- **The punchline:** He then called the 2016 note ban, when ₹500 and ₹1,000 notes were withdrawn, **"kind of a grand theft without auto"**, drawing laughs from the people around him.

As of Business Today's report on 23 November, neither the BJP nor the Prime Minister's Office had replied ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/grand-theft-without-auto-shashi-tharoor-turns-gta-6-review-into-a-demonetisation-dig-at-modi-govt-569037-2026-11-23)).
