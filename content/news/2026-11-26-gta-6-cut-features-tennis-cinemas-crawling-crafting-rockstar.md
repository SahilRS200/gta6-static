Looking for a tennis court or a cinema in Leonida? Don't. Two Rockstar North developers, development manager **Rob Nelson** and design producer **Scott Mitchell**, have listed features that were tried for GTA 6 and cut before launch, in an interview with the German magazine PC Games reported by [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-devs-reveal-major-gameplay-features-that-didnt-make-the-final-cut-3431334/) on 25 November. No story spoilers here.

## What was cut, and why

- **Tennis.** Rockstar worked on bringing the GTA 5 minigame back, but dropped it because the result wasn't up to the team's standard (Dexerto).
- **Cinemas and theatres.** Watching a show, as in Red Dead Redemption 2, nearly made it in; it was cut for not being interactive enough (Dexerto).
- **Crawling, decorating Jason and Lucia's homes, and a full crafting system.** All considered and scrapped; Dexerto says some didn't fit the game's story or play.
- **Mini golf survived.** That one is in the final game (Dexerto).

## No chainsaw, no katana

Players hoping for the classic Vice City chainsaw won't find one, and katanas are missing too. Nelson told VGC it was a choice, **"not a resource thing"**: a chainsaw "would have been too much for this game at this resolution and these characters", adding, "Even at the most psycho, did Jason and Lucia really need that?" ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-skips-chainsaws-katanas-rockstar-explains-why/)). What you can use: our [weapons guide →](/news/gta-6-weapons-guide-ammu-nation-buy-store-recover-guns/)

Earlier, in an August preview, Nelson said that at one point in development you *had* to buy groceries. That was dropped too, so groceries are now optional ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-cut-feature-groceries/)).

## Will they come back?

Rockstar hasn't said. Nothing in the interview points to a patch or DLC adding them, so treat these as gone, not "coming soon". Anyone selling a "tennis mod" or "crafting unlock" for PS5 or Xbox is lying.
