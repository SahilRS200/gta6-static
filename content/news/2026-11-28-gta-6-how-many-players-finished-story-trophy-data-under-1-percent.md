Still stuck in the early chapters and feeling slow? You're in the majority. A week after launch, **fewer than 1 in 100 players have reached the end of GTA 6's story**, according to the public trophy and achievement trackers. No story spoilers below; we only use chapter numbers.

## The numbers (as of 25 November)

PSNProfiles tracks about **1.17 lakh PS5 players** (116,590). Of them, per [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/how-many-players-finished-gta-6-trophy-data/):

| Story trophy | PS5 players who have it |
|---|---|
| First story trophy | 99.17% |
| Finish Chapter 1 | 66.22% |
| Finish Chapter 2 | 40.55% |
| Finish Chapter 3 | 6.45% |
| Finish Chapter 4 | 1.26% |
| Final mission | **0.88%** (1,023 players) |

- **Xbox is a step behind.** TrueAchievements data puts Chapter 1 at about 56%, Chapter 2 at about 31%, Chapter 3 at 4% and the final mission below 1% ([Player.One](https://www.player.one/gta-6-story-progress-chapter-completion-numbers-show-players-are-taking-it-slow-164620)).
- **One Platinum.** A single PSNProfiles player had the Platinum, which needs 100% completion ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/how-many-players-finished-gta-6-trophy-data/)).
- **Fast-moving numbers.** Two days earlier only about 13% of tracked PS5 players had finished Chapter 2 ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175648-only-40-of-gta-6-players-have-cleared-chapter-2-achievement-data-shows.html)), so today's figures will be higher.

These trackers count only players who sign up to them, so treat the numbers as a sample, not an official Rockstar figure.

## Why so slow?

- **Chapter 3 is a wall of time, not difficulty.** Reaching it takes around 15–20 hours if you do side content, and the main story is about 50 hours ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175648-only-40-of-gta-6-players-have-cleared-chapter-2-achievement-data-shows.html), [Player.One](https://www.player.one/gta-6-story-progress-chapter-completion-numbers-show-players-are-taking-it-slow-164620)). Our [how long is GTA 6](/news/gta-6-how-long-to-beat-story-hours/) guide has the full breakdown.
- **Players are wandering off.** 44% of tracked PS5 players have already beaten Cal at fishing and about 40% have discovered 100 vehicles, more than have finished Chapter 2 ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/how-many-players-finished-gta-6-trophy-data/)).
