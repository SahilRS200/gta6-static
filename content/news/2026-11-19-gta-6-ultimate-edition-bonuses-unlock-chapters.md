Bought the **Ultimate Edition** (₹7,499 on PlayStation Store India, against ₹5,999 for Standard; see [our price guide](/gta-6-price-in-india/)) and wondering where your extras are? They aren't all handed over on day one. GTA 6 unlocks its 16 Ultimate bonuses **as you move through the story**, chapter by chapter ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-ultimate-edition-unlocks/)). No story spoilers below, only chapter numbers.

## What unlocks when

- **Chapter 1:** six shops (One-Eyed Willie's, Rideout Customs, PTT Youngin$ Compound, Stock 305 Clothing, Sara's Unisex Salon and Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor), Jason's personalised weapon variants, and three vehicles: the **Ganado Retro Build, Jason's Dinka Enduro and the Crest Kayak**. Those three can be used **only during Chapter 1**, so enjoy them while you can.
- **Chapter 2:** Lucia's weapon variants, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with a garage, and a classic car collection you track down for restoration.
- **Chapter 4:** the Vice City Style outfits and tattoos, and the Shitzu Squalo boat.
- **Chapter 5:** the **'95 Grotti Cheetah** sports car.

When a bonus becomes available, a **purple icon** can appear on your map showing where to collect it ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-ultimate-edition-unlocks/)).

## Why players are angry

A post shared by PlayStation LifeStyle claims reaching Chapter 2, where most extras arrive, "could take over 25 hours", and the outlet estimates about 60 hours to reach the Cheetah. Some fans called the $100 edition the "scam of the year" and compared it to a battle pass ([PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2026/11/18/gta-6-100-ultimate-edition-locked-content-backlash), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175061-gta-6-fans-call-100-ultimate-edition-a-scam-over-locked-bonuses.html)). **Rockstar hasn't confirmed those hour estimates**, and it hasn't commented on the backlash.

## Should you still buy Ultimate?

If you want the extras early, they won't come early: you get them by playing the story either way. On the Standard Edition? Nothing here changes the game itself, so there's no rush to upgrade.
