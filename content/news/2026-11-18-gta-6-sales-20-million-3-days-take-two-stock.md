Wall Street liked the reviews. Shares of **Take-Two Interactive**, Rockstar's parent company, rose about **6% in US premarket trading on Wednesday** after GTA 6 landed a 99 on Metacritic, according to [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taketwo-shares-climb-as-gta-vi-reviews-impress-ahead-of-blockbuster-launch-5035826). [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-owner-take-twos-stock-explodes-after-high-review-scores-but-its-still-down-massively-this-year/) notes the stock is still down sharply for the year, and the jump came after a roughly two-day sell-off before launch ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/11/63383348/take-two-shares-jump-as-gta-vi-draws-near-perfect-reviews-ahead-of-release)).

## Update, 20 Nov: shares dip 1% on launch day

Take-Two's stock **fell about 1% on Thursday**, launch day, after closing 6% higher the session before, [Reuters reports](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/20/take-two-set-to-shatter-sales-records-as-039gta-vi039-launches-to-massive-fanfare). Reuters says Take-Two is on course for nearly **$2 billion in first-day sales**, more than twice GTA V's roughly $800 million in 2013. TD Cowen analyst Doug Creutz told Reuters the timing and size of the next GTA Online matter more to the stock than GTA 6 unit sales ([GTA 6 Online: no date yet](/news/gta-6-online-multiplayer-not-at-launch/)).

## Update, 19 Nov: PlayStation takes 75%, Sony shares rise

On launch day, **Sony's shares rose nearly 3% in Tokyo** on Thursday while Japanese tech stocks broadly fell, reports [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/sony-shares-rise-as-playstation-dominates-gta-vi-sales-5037816). The reason: Sensor Tower's tracker puts GTA 6 at about **17.07 million copies**, with **PlayStation taking 75% of sales and Xbox 25%**. Sony also earns a cut of every digital copy sold on the PlayStation Store.

These are still **Sensor Tower estimates, not official figures**. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't announced sales yet.

## The 20 million forecast

Oppenheimer analyst **Martin Yang** forecasts roughly **20 million copies sold and more than $1.85 billion in gross revenue in the first three days**, per [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taketwo-shares-climb-as-gta-vi-reviews-impress-ahead-of-blockbuster-launch-5035826). That is an **analyst estimate, not a Rockstar figure**. For comparison, Sensor Tower's launch-day estimate is already about 19 million copies and $1.84 billion ([our sales-tracker story](/news/gta-6-pre-orders-15-million-record/)).

Yang also named **cyberattacks on console networks** as the main launch-day risk, which matters for anyone in India planning to play the moment it unlocks.

## When we'll get real numbers

Rockstar and Take-Two haven't published any sales or pre-order totals yet. Yang expects the **first official sales disclosure as early as 20 November**, with a first-weekend update possibly around **23 November** ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taketwo-shares-climb-as-gta-vi-reviews-impress-ahead-of-blockbuster-launch-5035826)). We'll update this when it lands.
