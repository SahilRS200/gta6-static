Yes, GTA 6 already has a money glitch, two days after launch. The best-documented one is a **save-and-reload loop at a red barn** that makes drug-stash loot respawn while the bags you already took stay in your car. Beebom tested it and got **about $11,280 of loot on the first run** and **about $8,640 on the next** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-unlimited-money-glitch/)). It only touches your own single-player save: GTA 6 Online isn't out. No story spoilers below.

## How the red barn glitch works

1. **Bring your own car.** You need your personal vehicle's boot to hold the loot.
2. **Go to the red barn** north of Key Lento in South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet.
3. **Take everything:** the stash on the table and in the safe in the back office, the cash bag in the open van in the middle of the barn, and the stash in the nearby vans with teddy-bear art. Fill both stash bags.
4. **Put both bags in your boot.**
5. **Make a manual save** (not an autosave), then **load it**. You wake at Jason's house with the bags still in the car.
6. **Drive back.** The office, safe and vans should be stocked again. Store, save, reload, repeat.

## Getting paid

- **Sell at Brian's lockup** in south-west Key Lento (keypad code **7700**): drop the goods inside. Brian fences them and the cash arrives **a few in-game days later in the mailbox** at your safehouse ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-unlimited-money-glitch/)).
- **Bank it.** Cash you carry is lost if you die or get busted. [Where to hide it →](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)

## The catches

- **It's flaky.** Beebom says loot doesn't always respawn; save and reload a few more times or wait a few in-game days. Some bags can't be picked up until they're full.
- **Back up your save.** Keep a separate manual save slot you don't overwrite while looping. [How saving works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
- **The first patch left it alone.** Update 01.002.002 (23 November) doesn't mention the glitch, and Notebookcheck reports it is untouched ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-GTA-6-update-leaves-infinite-money-glitch-untouched.1457986.0.html)). A later patch could still close it, so don't count on it lasting. [What the patch fixed →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

## Why many players are skipping it

Kotaku reports that plenty of players are refusing to use money glitches, saying it **takes away from the game** they waited 13 years for. It also notes the trick mostly **removes the cooldown on loot you could sell anyway**, there's no hard cap on honest earnings, and depending on where you are in the story you **may not be able to use your money yet**, so grinding early can be pointless ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-infinite-money-glitch-how-to-rockstar-games-ps5-2000758907)). Kotaku's honest alternative: treasure hunts early, then selling cars to mechanics in Chapter 2.
