**Update, 28 November:** a much faster glitch is spreading — **stash bag duplication**. You drop a full stash bag while crouched and pick it straight back up without moving, and the bag's value goes up each time. VICE turned it into one bag worth **$277,200** and says ten bags in a van come to **about $2.7 million** ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/new-gta-6-money-glitch-lets-you-duplicate-millions-in-minutes/)). Steps first, then the older red barn loop below. Both only touch your own single-player save.

## New: the stash bag duplication glitch

1. **Fill a stash bag** with valuables — drugs, jewellery or marked bills from a robbery, heist or raid. Higher-value bags pay more per cycle.
2. **Crouch** and stay crouched.
3. **Open the weapon wheel** (L1 on PS5, LB on Xbox), switch to **Items** (R1 / RB) and pick the stash bag.
4. **Hold Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox) to drop it.** Don't touch the left stick.
5. **Turn the camera only** (right stick) until the pick-up prompt shows, then **pick the bag up**. Its value goes up.
6. **Repeat.** Game Rant and Khel Now say each pickup adds the bag's original value again and it works **about 12 times per bag** before it stops growing ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-unlimited-money-glitch-stash-bag-duplication-grand-theft-auto-6/), [Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/gaming/gta-6-stash-bag-duplication-money-202611)). ComicBook.com saw bags stop at **$130,000** ([ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/ultra-fast-gta-6-stash-bag-money-glitch-lets-users-make-1-5-million-in-5-minutes/)).
7. **Sell it**, for example to Tyrone, or store it in a vehicle and fill another bag. VICE fills a 10-slot van this way and delivers to Tyrone in Vice City.

**If it doesn't work:** you almost certainly nudged the left stick between drop and pickup. Outlets disagree on whether each pickup doubles the bag or adds its starting value again, so expect results to vary.

**The catch:** your BuckMe account tops out at **$2 million** even late in the story, so VICE says bigger payouts can sit as "pending" until you spend some cash. [How the bank limit works →](/news/gta-6-buckme-bank-limit-cant-deposit-money-family-savings/) Rockstar hasn't commented, and a patch will probably close it.

## The older red barn glitch

GTA 6 had a money glitch two days after launch. The best-documented one is a **save-and-reload loop at a red barn** that makes drug-stash loot respawn while the bags you already took stay in your car. Beebom tested it and got **about $11,280 of loot on the first run** and **about $8,640 on the next** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-unlimited-money-glitch/)). GTA 6 Online isn't out. No story spoilers below.

## How the red barn glitch works

1. **Bring your own car.** You need your personal vehicle's boot to hold the loot.
2. **Go to the red barn** north of Key Lento in South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet.
3. **Take everything:** the stash on the table and in the safe in the back office, the cash bag in the open van in the middle of the barn, and the stash in the nearby vans with teddy-bear art. Fill both stash bags.
4. **Put both bags in your boot.**
5. **Make a manual save** (not an autosave), then **load it**. You wake at Jason's house with the bags still in the car.
6. **Drive back.** The office, safe and vans should be stocked again. Store, save, reload, repeat.

## Getting paid from the barn

- **Sell at Brian's lockup** in south-west Key Lento (keypad code **7700**): drop the goods inside. Brian fences them and the cash arrives **a few in-game days later in the mailbox** at your safehouse ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-unlimited-money-glitch/)).
- **Bank it.** Cash you carry is lost if you die or get busted. [Where to hide it →](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)

## Red barn catches

- **It's flaky.** Beebom says loot doesn't always respawn; save and reload a few more times or wait a few in-game days. Some bags can't be picked up until they're full.
- **Back up your save.** Keep a separate manual save slot you don't overwrite while looping. [How saving works →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
- **The first patch left it alone.** Update 01.002.002 (23 November) doesn't mention the glitch, and Notebookcheck reports it is untouched ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-GTA-6-update-leaves-infinite-money-glitch-untouched.1457986.0.html)). A later patch could still close it, so don't count on it lasting. [What the patch fixed →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

## Why many players are skipping it

Kotaku reports that plenty of players are refusing to use money glitches, saying it **takes away from the game** they waited 13 years for. It also notes the trick mostly **removes the cooldown on loot you could sell anyway**, there's no hard cap on honest earnings, and depending on where you are in the story you **may not be able to use your money yet**, so grinding early can be pointless ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-infinite-money-glitch-how-to-rockstar-games-ps5-2000758907)). Kotaku's honest alternative: treasure hunts early, then selling cars to mechanics in Chapter 2.
