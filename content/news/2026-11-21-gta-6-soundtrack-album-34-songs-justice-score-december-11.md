**Loved a song on the radio while driving through Vice City? You can now play it outside the game.** *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* is out on streaming services with **34 original songs**, and an original score by the French duo Justice follows on **11 December 2026** ([Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-34-songs-justice-score-december-11/)).

## New: the Deluxe Edition adds 23 songs

- **23 bonus tracks** landed on streaming services on 23 November as *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition)*, taking the album to **57 songs** ([Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-deluxe-edition-23-bonus-tracks-streaming/), [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/news/gta-6-the-album-deluxe-edition-out-now-23-bonus-tracks/)).
- **Who's on it:** Travis Scott, Anitta, Offset, 21 Savage, Latto, Denzel Curry, Slayyyter, The Dare, HEALTH, Power Trip and Real Dimez with Sexyy Red, and the electronic duo Overmono closes it with five tracks ([GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/rockstar-just-dropped-a-gta-6-deluxe-album-with-23-new-tracks-e16c)).
- **A streaming release, not a game update:** Rockstar describes the songs as coming from across GTA 6, and hasn't said any are new to the game ([Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-deluxe-edition-23-bonus-tracks-streaming/)).

## What's on it

- **34 new tracks**, released by Rockstar with Atlantic Records ([Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/dimassanfiorenzo/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-tracklist-features)).
- **Big names:** Travis Scott, Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen, Burna Boy, Gunna and Keith Richards, among others ([Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/dimassanfiorenzo/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-tracklist-features)). Noisy Pixel also lists JENNIE, Zara Larsson, Don Toliver and JHAYCO ([Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-34-songs-justice-score-december-11/)).
- **Track 1** is Justice with Michael McDonald, "Starting from Zero" ([Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/dimassanfiorenzo/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-tracklist-features)).
- **Vinyl and CD** versions are listed too, including a limited liquid-filled vinyl ([Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/dimassanfiorenzo/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-tracklist-features)). We haven't seen an India price for them.

## The Justice score

Justice's score for the game comes out separately on **11 December 2026** on streaming services, with **30 tracks** plus digital bonus material ([Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/dimassanfiorenzo/grand-theft-auto-vi-album-tracklist-features)).
