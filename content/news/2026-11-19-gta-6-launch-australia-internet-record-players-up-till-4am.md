GTA 6 didn't just break streaming records. In Australia it broke the **internet**. Traffic on the country's National Broadband Network peaked at **36.54 terabits per second** across the night of 18 November and early on 19 November, roughly **20% above the normal baseline**, an NBN spokesperson told [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/gta-vi-smashes-expectations-after-overnight-launch/107401924). That beat a record set only a week earlier, on 12 November.

The spike landed on GTA 6's launch night. A 212 GB game unlocking at the same minute for huge numbers of players, plus launch streams, is the obvious suspect.

## The UK broke its records too

When midnight reached the UK, the same thing happened. Virgin Media O2's evening peak (8–10 PM) came in **9.4% above its previous record**, which had been set only the week before by people preloading GTA 6. **BT and EE** both reported their **highest-ever home broadband peak** ahead of the midnight launch; BT said the game "has driven a level of demand we've never seen before" ([Radio News Hub via Rother Radio](https://www.rotherradio.co.uk/launch-of-gta-vi-breaks-broadband-records/)). Uswitch speed tests put the median download time for the 212 GB game at just over six and a half hours.

## What Australian players said after night one

ABC spoke to players who unlocked the game hours before India did. No story details here:

- **"Hell yeah."** That was former pro gamer Jamie Skella's answer when asked if the wait was worth it. He called the world "so rich, it's alive" and praised the voice acting.
- **6–7 hour first sessions.** One player with 5,000 hours in GTA V played through the night and rated GTA 6 a **9.5 out of 10**, singling out its role-playing elements. Another said he had to force himself to stop after 3 AM.
- **Four days off work.** One fan booked leave and even set his Xbox to New Zealand time to start early, then played until 4 AM.
- **Not everyone gives it 10/10.** ScreenHub Australia's critic gave it **4 out of 5**, calling it "a pretty good game" at its core but finding some sections boring and too limited. That's a rare cooler take next to the [99 on Metacritic](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/).
