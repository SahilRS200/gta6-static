A clip claiming **Sidhu Moosewala's music plays on GTA 6's radio** is doing the rounds on Instagram, with Punjabi fans calling it history: the first Punjabi artist in a GTA game. **There's no proof of that.** Rockstar has not confirmed the late singer in the game's radio lineup, and [DNA India](https://www.dnaindia.com/bollywood/report-sidhu-moosewala-creates-history-becomes-first-punjabi-artiste-to-feature-in-gta-vi-here-s-the-truth-behind-viral-clip-from-game-grand-theft-auto-vi-3224296) says the video **looks like a fan edit**.

## What the clip shows

- A player pauses the game, looks for a Punjabi station, picks one labelled "BBC", and a Sidhu Moosewala track starts playing while the player acts surprised.
- It was shared on Instagram by the account @sangtarr. DNA India doesn't say who made the edit.

## How fans reacted

Plenty of Indian players spotted it straight away. Comments DNA India picked out range from "Chill, fake hai guys" and "Unfortunately, there is no Punjabi song or station in GTA 6" to "Fake but interesting" and "If this is real then yeah, legends never die."

## What's actually in the game

- **No Indian-language station or song has been confirmed.** GTA 6 also has no Hindi or other Indian language for menus or voices: [GTA 6 in Hindi? →](/news/gta-6-hindi-language-support/)
- The radio does have real surprises: Weird Al co-hosts Vice City FM, which Rockstar confirmed: [what players are finding →](/news/gta-6-discoveries-bigfoot-pinkpantheress-radio/)
- Want real in-game finds instead? Try the [Easter eggs guide →](/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-fast-furious-car-tower-egg-cutscene/)
