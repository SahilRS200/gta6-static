GTA 6 tracks sleep. Jason and Lucia can only sleep in a **safehouse bed**, and a good night's rest is the quickest way to refill health and stamina. Here is how it works. No story spoilers.

## How to sleep

- **Go to a safehouse bed.** The first one is Jason's stilt house in Key Lento; the bed moves with you when the story takes you to a new safehouse. You can't sleep out in the open world ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-skip-time-and-sleep-in-gta-6-day-length-and-best-time-for-crimes/)).
- **Use the Rest prompt.** Press **Square on PS5** or **X on Xbox**, then pick when to wake up: morning, evening or night.
- **Then save manually.** Sleeping is not a save, so use a manual slot after you wake up ([how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)).

## What sleep does

- **Full health and stamina,** plus a short **focus** buff, KeenGamer reports.
- **The phone's health app** tells you if a character is well rested. Rockstar's Rob Nelson said going without rest slows how fast "certain attributes" recover; Rockstar hasn't said which ones ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/gta-6-health-app-ties-sleep-to-how-fast-jason-and-lucia-recover/), [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-is-a-bit-too-real-for-me-rockstar-will-punish-you-if-jason-and-lucia-dont-get-a-good-nights-sleep-we-track-lots-of-things/)).
- **It shows on their faces.** Long stretches without sleep make Jason and Lucia look worn out.
- **Train rested.** Workouts give weaker results when you're tired or hungry ([gym guide](/news/gta-6-gym-workouts-strength-stamina-guide/)).

## Other ways to move the clock

- **RydeMe rides** and some story missions also move time forward. [How fast travel works →](/news/gta-6-fast-travel-rydeme-taxi-bus-how-to/)
- **After the story,** collectible watches unlock two time cheats (Move Time Forward and Pause Time of Day). Cheats turn off trophies and saving ([how cheats unlock](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/)).

## How long is a day?

Players have measured a full in-game day at about **144 real minutes** (one in-game hour is about 6 real minutes), roughly three times GTA 5's 48-minute day. Rockstar hasn't published an official figure ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-day-and-night-cycle/), [KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-skip-time-and-sleep-in-gta-6-day-length-and-best-time-for-crimes/)).
