GTA 6 hides a survival game on a lonely island: you're drugged, collared and hunted by a man with night vision and a sniper rifle. GameSpot compares it to the films *Hard Target* and *Saw* ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-secret-mission-that-feels-like-hard-target-meets-saw/)). It's a side encounter with no mission marker, and it has nothing to do with the story. **No story spoilers.**

## How to start it

- **Go to Hyde Island,** one of the islands at the north end of Leonida ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/)). There's no road from the mainland, so take a boat ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/hyde-island-hunt-easter-egg-manhuntsaw/)). Or fly in: [where to find a seaplane early →](/news/gta-6-first-plane-helicopter-location-how-to-fly-early/)
- **Explore alone.** Sooner or later someone you can't see shoots you with a tranquilliser dart. Nothing needs to be accepted first ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/hyde-island-hunt-easter-egg-manhuntsaw/)). LADbible says helping a woman move a couch can also start it ([LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-secret-missions-5-best-056826-20261124)).
- **Make a manual save before you land.** Your guns are taken when it starts: [how to save](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/).

## What happens

You wake in a shipping container with your weapons gone and an **explosive collar** on your neck. A man on an old TV explains the game, a **10-second countdown** runs, and the door opens. Grab the **Nipper pistol from the table**. It has only **two bullets** ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-secret-mission-that-feels-like-hard-target-meets-saw/)). The hunter has **night vision, a high-powered sniper rifle, full armour and a tracking dog** ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/)).

## How to survive

- **Don't swim off or cross the fence.** The collar stays live until the hunt ends ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/hyde-island-hunt-easter-egg-manhuntsaw/)).
- **Stay hidden and move between lights.** The lit spots mark guns and ammo ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/)).
- **Find a shotgun.** Knock the hunter down with it, then finish him before he gets up. Keep away from the dog ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/hyde-island-hunt-easter-egg-manhuntsaw/), [The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/)). More on fights: [wanted level and combat guide →](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/)
- **If you die, go back and try again** ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/saw-style-killer-gta-6/)).

## The rewards

- **Island Poacher FF-50,** a unique camo heavy sniper rifle. Take it from the hunter's body.
- **Cash.** Dexerto says $20,000 to $30,000 when you leave through the **boathouse**, which is also where you get your gear back ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/hyde-island-hunt-easter-egg-manhuntsaw/)). Other guides don't give an amount, so yours may differ.
