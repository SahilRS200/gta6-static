Lost your favourite gun after a shootout in GTA 6? It's probably not gone. GTA 6 splits guns into ones you **own** and ones you just **picked up**, and only owned guns come back. Here's how buying, carrying and storing works on PS5 and Xbox. No story spoilers.

## Buy from Ammu-Nation, not off the floor

- **Ammu-Nation is where owned guns come from.** It also sells ammo, body armour and explosives. A gun you buy is shared by Jason and Lucia ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)).
- **The first store is in Key Lento**, the starting area, reports say ([gamer.org](https://www.gamer.org/gta-6-how-to-buy-store-recover-weapons/)).
- **Bigger stores stock more** guns and attachments, and spending more unlocks more stock through a loyalty system ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)).
- Ammo, armour and explosives you buy go straight to your owned car's trunk.

## How many you can carry

- **Four guns on you: two sidearms and two long guns.** Only one long gun fits on your back; the second has to stay in your hands ([gamer.org](https://www.gamer.org/gta-6-how-to-buy-store-recover-weapons/)).
- **Holster after a fight** with **L1 / LB**. Pistols are hard for people to notice, but walking around with a shotgun or rifle out can alarm people and bring the police ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)). Getting into a car holsters your gun automatically; you can switch that to manual in Settings → Gameplay → Combat.

## Store spares and get lost guns back

- **Your owned car's trunk is your armoury.** Spare guns, ammo, armour and explosives live there; how much fits depends on the vehicle ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)).
- **Lost an owned gun?** Dropped it or died: pick it up again from your car's trunk or from the **weapon locker inside any Ammu-Nation** ([gamer.org](https://www.gamer.org/gta-6-how-to-buy-store-recover-weapons/)).
- **Guns taken from enemies aren't yours.** They can't be recovered from a locker, and an unowned gun in your trunk is thrown away once it's out of ammo ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)).

## Upgrade what you own

Owned guns can take grips, magazines, sights, muzzle parts and other attachments at Ammu-Nation. Suppressors and flashlights come off anywhere; permanent parts need a store visit. Picked-up and unique guns can't be modified ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/gta-6-weapon-guide/)).
