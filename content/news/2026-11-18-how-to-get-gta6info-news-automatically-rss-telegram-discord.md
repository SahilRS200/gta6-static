We update GTA6Info.in **every hour** during launch week. You don't have to keep refreshing: every new story is published to our **RSS feed**, and almost any app can watch it for you.

**Our feed address:** `https://www.gta6info.in/news/feed.xml`

Copy that once. Everything below uses it.

## 1. A feed reader app (easiest)

A feed reader collects new posts from sites you follow in one list, like an inbox for news.

1. Install a free reader such as **Feedly** or **Inoreader** (Android, iPhone, web), or **NetNewsWire** on iPhone and Mac.
2. Tap **Add** / **Follow** / **+**.
3. Paste `https://www.gta6info.in/news/feed.xml` (or just `gta6info.in`, most readers find the feed by themselves).

New GTA 6 stories now appear in the app as soon as we publish them.

## 2. Telegram

Two ways, both free:

- **An RSS bot:** search Telegram for an RSS bot, start it, and send it our feed address. It messages you every new post. If you run a channel, add the bot to it as an admin and it posts there instead.
- **An automation:** on IFTTT or Zapier, create a flow from **RSS feed → new item** to **Telegram → send message**, using our feed address.

## 3. A Discord server

Great for your gaming group.

- **A Discord RSS bot** such as MonitoRSS: invite it to your server, pick a channel, and give it our feed address.
- **Or a webhook:** in Discord, open **Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook**, choose a channel and **copy the webhook URL**. Then use Zapier or IFTTT to send each new **RSS item** to that webhook.

**Keep the webhook URL private.** Anyone who has it can post in that channel.

## 4. Email

On IFTTT or Zapier, connect **RSS feed → new item** to **email**, and every new story lands in your inbox.

## 5. On your phone's home screen

GTA6Info.in works as an app. In Chrome on Android, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen**; on iPhone Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen, and the countdown even works offline.

## WhatsApp?

WhatsApp doesn't support RSS feeds or bots for regular users, so there's no clean way to auto-post there. The easiest option is to share our [India hub](/gta-6-india/) or any article link in your group.

## What you'll receive

Each update has the **headline, a one-line summary and the link**. Tap through for the full story, with sources listed at the top of every article.
