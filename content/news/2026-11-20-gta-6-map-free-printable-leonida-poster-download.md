Bought GTA 6 as a code-in-box from Blinkit, Zepto or a store and missed the paper map that used to come with every GTA? **Rockstar has put the official Leonida map online for free**, ready to print.

## Where to get it

- Go to Rockstar's GTA VI site, open **Media**, and find the card called **"Leonida Map & Cover Art Poster"**; it offers "print-ready formats of the official game map and cover art poster" ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media)).
- Both files are **free**, and went live on launch day, 19 November ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175128-rockstar-releases-official-gta-6-map-for-printing-download-it-now.html)).
- Get it **only from Rockstar's own site.** "GTA 6 map" downloads elsewhere can carry malware ([the scams doing the rounds](/news/gta-6-free-download-apk-scam-warning/)).

## The printed poster

Rockstar also plans a double-sided printed poster (map on one side, cover art on the other) on the **Rockstar Store for $0.99, or free with any store purchase**, "shortly after launch"; no date has been given ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-free-map/), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175128-rockstar-releases-official-gta-6-map-for-printing-download-it-now.html)). Check at checkout whether it ships to India before you order anything.

## Why it's handy in India

The India box is code-in-box: **no disc**; you redeem a code and download the game ([what's in the box](/news/gta-6-locked-not-starting-ps5-fix-india/)). Rockstar hasn't said a paper map comes in the box ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-free-map/)), so the free file is the easiest way to get one. A local print shop can print the file on A3 or bigger for a wall map.
