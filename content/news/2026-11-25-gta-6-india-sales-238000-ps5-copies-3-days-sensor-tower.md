We finally have a number for India. Market research firm **Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 sold about 2,38,000 copies (2.38 lakh) on PlayStation in India** in its first three days, from 19 to 21 November, XpertPick reports ([XpertPick](https://www.xpertpick.com/gaming/news/exclusive-we-have-some-estimated-figures-for-the-number-of-gta-6-copies-sold-in-india/)).

## What the number covers

- **PlayStation only.** Sensor Tower gave no Xbox figure and no all-platform total for India ([XpertPick](https://www.xpertpick.com/gaming/news/exclusive-we-have-some-estimated-figures-for-the-number-of-gta-6-copies-sold-in-india/)).
- **Discs and digital together,** without a split between them.
- **It's an estimate, not an official count.** Sensor Tower says it models sales from panels of millions of players it tracks worldwide. Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced any India sales figure ([Dainik Jagran English](https://english.dainikjagranmpcg.com/technology/gta-6-makes-379-billion-on-day-one-india-sales/article-45473)).

## Why it matters

At ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition, 2.38 lakh copies is roughly ₹140 crore of game sales in three days, before any Ultimate upgrades (our own rough maths, not Sensor Tower's). A PS5 Digital plus the game costs about ₹70,989, Outlook India notes, and many players went to cafes instead. Outlook India reports a Delhi cafe doubling its rate from ₹250 to ₹500 an hour during launch week ([Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/gta-6-fever-how-india-is-experiencing-the-worlds-biggest-gaming-phenomenon)).
