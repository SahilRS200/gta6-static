India unlocks at **12:00 AM IST**. Spend two minutes in the menus first, then try a few things Rockstar has shown off. **No story spoilers here**: everything below is about controls and systems.

## Settings to pick before you play

Game Rant suggests two aim setups ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-best-aim-settings-casual-realistic-gameplay/)):

- **Relaxed, GTA 5-style:** Targeting set to **Lock-On**, Aim Assist Strength **3**, Lock-On Time **5**, with Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist **on**.
- **Hardcore:** **Free Aim**, Aim Assist **0**, Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist **off**.

Treat these as a starting point: the guide was written before launch, so check the exact option names in your own menu. Also switch your TV to **Game Mode**. GTA 6 runs at 30fps on every console, and Rockstar says it cut input lag to make that feel snappier ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-pro-is-not-powerful-enough-to-run-gta-6-at-60fps-rockstar-explains-frame-rate-decision)). ## 5 things to try in your first hours

From Rockstar's preview interview with The Guardian, as summarised by [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-gameplay-details-guardian-interview/):

1. **Scout before a robbery.** You can case a place first: find a back entrance or a safe code, and disable security cameras or take the footage.
2. **Switch between Jason and Lucia.** You can swap freely most of the time; some moments lock you to one of them.
3. **Text, call and go on dates.** The pair can hang out at the gym or go for a drink, and how you treat the relationship matters.
4. **Be nice for once.** Rockstar added optional ways to show empathy, like asking a dog's owner if you can pet the dog.
5. **Go birdwatching.** There are 62 bird species to log with binoculars.

Expect a slow start: Rockstar North's Rob Nelson says Jason and Lucia begin small-time, with cheap cars and basic locks ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-gameplay-details-guardian-interview/), from [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/11/a-world-of-its-own-inside-the-making-of-the-most-detailed-grand-theft-auto-yet)'s studio visit). Watch the fuel gauge, too: a full tank lasts roughly 18 to 29 minutes of driving. [GTA 6 fuel system →](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/)
