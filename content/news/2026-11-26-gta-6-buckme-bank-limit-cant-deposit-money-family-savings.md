Sitting on a pile of robbery cash and the ATM won't take it? That's not a bug. GTA 6's bank app, **BuckMe**, caps how much your checking account can hold, and the cap rises as you **save money and move through the story**. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/5hSbxodEFeNbU1wARy1p4X/cannot-put-any-more-money-in-my-buckme-account-in-grand-theft-auto-vi) says the limits go up as the story progresses, so keep playing the main missions. No story spoilers below.

## How the limit works

- **Two accounts.** BuckMe has a **checking account** you can spend and withdraw from, and **Family Savings**, which you fill by moving money across from checking in the app ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=369740)).
- **Savings goals raise the cap.** Each Family Savings goal you hit lifts your checking limit, and some add a discount. New goals open as the story moves on ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=369740)).
- **When checking is full,** new money waits until you spend or save some of it ([eGamers](https://egamers.io/gta-6-buckme-guide-how-family-savings-checking-limits-and-perks-work/)).

## The tiers

| Save in Family Savings | Checking limit | Perk |
|---|---|---|
| $5,000 | $15,000 | none |
| $10,000 | $100,000 | 20% off RydeMe rides |
| $100,000 | $500,000 | 20% off garages |
| $500,000 (or reach Chapter 4) | $2,000,000 | 10% off liveries |

Source: [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=369740); [eGamers](https://egamers.io/gta-6-buckme-guide-how-family-savings-checking-limits-and-perks-work/) lists the same first three tiers. You're upgraded to **BuckMe Premium automatically when Chapter 4 begins**, whatever your savings. The $1 million and $2 million goals after that are optional and don't raise the limit.

## What to do

- **Feed Family Savings early.** Getting to $10,000 saved takes your limit from $15,000 to $100,000 and makes [fast travel](/news/gta-6-fast-travel-rydeme-taxi-bus-how-to/) cheaper.
- **Save $100,000 before buying a garage.** That tier knocks 20% off; see [garage prices](/news/gta-6-buy-property-garages-prices-real-estate-app/).
- **Don't carry the overflow around.** Pocket cash is lost if you die or get busted, so [stash it safely](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/) until you can bank it.
