Breezing through GTA 6's story and wondering if you're just very good? Partly, maybe, but mostly it's deliberate. Rockstar North co-studio head **Rob Nelson** told [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/gta-6-dev-interview-on-rails-story-easy/) that the story missions were tuned to be approachable. No story spoilers here.

## What Rockstar says

- **A middle ground on purpose.** Nelson said GTA 4 could be "a little too punishing" in places and GTA 5 was perhaps too easy for some players, so the team aimed for somewhere in between with GTA 6 ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6s-difficulty-was-designed-to-find-the-balance-between-4-being-too-hard-and-5-being-too-easy)).
- **Fewer instant fails.** Design producer **Scott Mitchell** said the team made "a conscious effort" to keep fail states appropriate and not overused, so you can still play a mission your way, stealth included, while keeping the big cinematic set pieces ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-difficulty-settings-easy-hard-rockstar-middle/)).
- **Designers start too hard, then tune down.** Because developers know every enemy position, first drafts tend to be too tough. Nelson described "unpicking" that so most players "enjoy, rather than endure" a mission ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6s-difficulty-was-designed-to-find-the-balance-between-4-being-too-hard-and-5-being-too-easy)).
- **The hard stuff is in the open world.** Nelson said open-world content such as **compounds and fronts** was made harder on purpose, for players who want something to sink their teeth into ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6s-difficulty-was-designed-to-find-the-balance-between-4-being-too-hard-and-5-being-too-easy)).

## Want more challenge? What to do

- **There's no Hard mode.** GTA 6 has no difficulty levels, only assists you can switch off. Turn off aim assist and other helpers in the settings: [the full list →](/news/gta-6-difficulty-accessibility-settings-auto-drive/)
- **Go after compounds.** Freddie's doomsday bunker is the best-known example, and it unlocks hurricanes: [how to break in →](/news/gta-6-freddie-gibbs-doomsday-compound-bunker-break-in/)
- **Chase a perfect result.** Game Rant suggests going for a perfect finish on heists as a self-imposed challenge ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-difficulty-settings-easy-hard-rockstar-middle/)).
- **Take the slow route.** Missions can't be replayed, so a run you rush is a run you don't get back: [keep a spare save →](/news/gta-6-replay-missions-no-replay-menu-save-slots/)
