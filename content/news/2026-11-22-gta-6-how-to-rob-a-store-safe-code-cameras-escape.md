Store robberies are GTA 6's quickest bit of street crime: a couple of hundred dollars from the till, more from the safe, and a short police chase if you plan it right. Buttons are PS5 first, Xbox in brackets. **No story spoilers.**

## Which stores you can rob

- **Most stores you can walk into**, including some with no map icon. Early examples near the start: the 247 in Key Lento, the Leonida Key Lento Mini Mart and the Globe Oil Food Center in Hamlet ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-store-robbery-guide/)).
- **Bigger jobs come later.** Jason and Lucia start small-time; higher-value robberies open as the story moves on ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-robberies-before-your-first-robbery/)).
- **Go as a couple if you can.** You can rob as Jason, Lucia or both, and doing it together counts towards their [relationship](/news/gta-6-relationship-meter-jason-lucia-levels-how-to-raise/).

## Before you go in

1. **Park the getaway car outside first**, and scan it with the phone's car scan for alarms and trackers — a tracked car can report you to the police by itself.
2. **Put a full spare outfit in the boot.** A hat or sunglasses alone won't change your look enough.
3. **Mask up before the door.** A mask, hat or glasses makes the CCTV less useful; shooting the cameras wipes the footage but costs you time.
4. **Use the front door.** At the Key Lento liquor store, forcing the back door alerts the owner.

## Inside: till and safe

- **Draw a weapon at the counter.** Some clerks empty the register; if not, open it yourself with **Triangle (Y)** and grab every stack. Customers can still try to stop you.
- **The safe pays more.** The code is either somewhere in the shop or known by a staff member — press **L3 + R3** for [Focus](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/) to spot who knows it. Hunting for it gives the police more time.
- **Explosives skip the code** but the blast destroys part of the cash.
- **Set yourself a time limit.** The till is fast and small; the safe is slow and bigger. Most registers hold a couple of hundred dollars.

## The beer bottle trick (no stars, no kills)

A clip that went viral on X on 27 November shows a quieter way to do a **jewellery store**, reported by [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-beer-bottle-trick-make-store-robberies-easy/):

1. **Walk in carrying a six-pack.** Staff don't treat a man with beer as a threat.
2. **Throw a bottle** to knock out the staff before anyone raises the alarm.
3. **A CCTV icon appears** once a camera sees you. Go to the back office, **destroy the recording** and the icon clears.
4. **Knock out the staff in the back** the same way, then empty the till and the front display cases.

Nobody is killed and the police are never called, so there's **no wanted level**, and it keeps the robber's [Criminal Profile](/news/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-irredeemable-explained/) clean ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-beer-bottle-trick-make-store-robberies-easy/)).

## Getting away

- **Expect 1–2 stars.** Police don't show on the minimap, and they remember your outfit and car — see our [wanted level guide](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/).
- **Change clothes from the boot**, then swap cars or repaint at a Pay 'n' Spray ($200).
- **Leave the red heat zone.** Losing the stars doesn't end the search, and robbing in an active heat zone brings the police faster.
- **Don't hit the same shop twice in a row** — it can close for a while.

## Bank the cash

You can carry up to **$20,000**, and carried cash is lost if you die or get busted. Deposit at an ATM or bank once the chase is over; phone deposits don't work ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-robberies-before-your-first-robbery/)).
