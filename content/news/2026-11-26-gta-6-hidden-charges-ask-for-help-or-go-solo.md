**Hidden Charges** is a main story mission in Chapter 3 of GTA 6, and partway through it the game asks you to choose: **ask for help** or **go solo**. Here's what each option changes, without any story spoilers ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-ask-for-help-go-solo-hidden-charges-grand-theft-auto-6/)).

## The short answer: ask for help

- **It saves you the climb down.** With help, you can switch between Jason and Lucia between towers, so you don't have to climb back down from each one (Game Rant).
- **Small relationship bonus.** Asking for help slightly improves Jason and Lucia's relationship (Game Rant).
- **Going solo** means climbing back down from each tower yourself. That's the only real difference (Game Rant).
- **The story doesn't change.** Neither choice affects the main story or how the mission ends (Game Rant).

## Two tips that save you time

1. **Don't take the helicopter** on the roof of the second tower. You get a wanted level almost straight after take-off, and you can't collect the last bag from the van until the heat dies down (Game Rant). [How to lose the cops →](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/)
2. **Use the parachutes.** Each rooftop has one, and it's the quickest way down. Jump from a spot with room to open it, and open it quickly (Game Rant).

There's no checkpoint restart if the helicopter goes wrong, and the game has [no mission replay menu](/news/gta-6-replay-missions-no-replay-menu-save-slots/), so make a manual save before you start. [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
