Is GTA 6 okay for kids? **No: it's rated 18+**, and police in the UK are telling parents who grew up on GTA not to judge the new game by their memories of the old ones ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-warning-police-warn-parents-about-games-impact-on-children-here-is-why.html)).

## What the police said

- **Guernsey Police's digital safety lead, Laura Simpson**, said the game has "a lot of blood and gore, but also things such as decapitations and unhealthy relationships", as reported by Digit, citing the BBC ([Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-warning-police-warn-parents-about-games-impact-on-children-here-is-why.html)).
- **Don't go by nostalgia.** Her point: how you felt playing an older GTA isn't how your child will feel playing this much bigger, more realistic one.
- **Streams count too.** Children can still see the content by watching online streams or watching adults play.
- Several other police forces put out GTA 6 messages around launch, some of them light-hearted ([UNILAD](https://www.unilad.com/news/world-news/police-warning-gta-six-release-523968-20261118)).

## What it means in India

The warning came from the UK, but the advice travels. GTA 6 set a [streaming record](/news/gta-6-streaming-record-twitch-5-million-viewers/), and India's biggest creators streamed it for hours ([our story](/news/gta-6-india-gaming-cafes-booked-carryminati-mythpat-views/)). If younger kids at home watch those streams, the same 18+ content is on screen. As the police advise, check what's in the game before deciding.
