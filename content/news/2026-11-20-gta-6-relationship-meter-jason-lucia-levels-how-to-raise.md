GTA 6 doesn't just track your wanted level and your cash: it also tracks how well **Jason and Lucia** are getting on. Here's how the relationship meter works, spoiler-free, and the easy habits that keep it high.

## The 4 levels

The meter has four steps, VGC reports ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-jason-lucia-relationship-levels-explained-how-to-improve-jason-and-lucias-relationship/)):

1. **Running on Empty** (1/4)
2. **Partners in Crime** (2/4)
3. **Crazy in Love** (3/4)
4. **Hot Together** (4/4)

**Where to check it:** press the touch pad (PS5) to open the menu and use the bumpers to reach the Jason and Lucia page, or hold right on the D-pad ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-jason-lucia-relationship-levels-explained-how-to-improve-jason-and-lucias-relationship/)).

## How to raise it

VGC's one-line rule: **do as much together as possible.**

- **Main missions together** count for a lot.
- **Small gestures:** hold hands while walking (R2 when prompted), kiss or show affection when the prompt appears, and sleep next to each other at the safe house.
- **Text.** Send a quick check-in (hold right on the D-pad) and reply to texts promptly when the two are apart.
- **Hang out:** working out, basketball or pool together all help. Our [gym guide](/news/gta-6-gym-workouts-strength-stamina-guide/) covers where to train.
- **Crime counts too:** a clean robbery or a successful escape from the cops lifts it ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-jason-lucia-relationship-levels-explained-how-to-improve-jason-and-lucias-relationship/)). Rockstar co-studio head Rob Nelson told Edge that beach walks, gym sessions and drives together all build closeness ([GTA Boom](https://www.gtaboom.com/edge-interview-breaks-down-how-the-gta-6-romance-works-f036)).

Quick extras from our [14 hidden features](/news/gta-6-tips-hidden-features-spoiler-free/): buying clothes for your partner and taking a shower also help.

## What neglect costs

- **Ignore the other character for too long and the meter drops.** The game warns you, but it never forces you to fix it ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-jason-lucia-relationship-levels-explained-how-to-improve-jason-and-lucias-relationship/)).
- **A strong relationship keeps things open:** VGC says it keeps some dialogue and mission options available and that it matters later in the story. At the bottom level the two grow distant.
- **It's never too late.** They can't fully break up, and a few small gestures bring the meter back up quickly. VGC also notes one scripted drop during the story that is easy to recover from.

## Players are split

Not everyone likes it. Some Reddit posters complained that the romance focus replaced older, cruder GTA features, while others enjoy the couple's dynamic ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/gta-6-players-complain-as-prostitution-dropped-from-series-12739759)). Either way, a few texts and a hand-hold cost you nothing.
