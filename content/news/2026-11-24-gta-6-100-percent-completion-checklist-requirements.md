Planning to finish everything in Leonida? GTA 6 tracks it with a **100% checklist of 35 tasks** in three groups, and finishing it earns the **"A Long Road"** trophy and achievement ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)). No story spoilers here.

## Three things to know first

- **The checklist appears only after the credits.** Open it from **Progress → Stats → 100% Completion**. Everything you do before that still counts ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)).
- **Nothing on it is missable.** It can all be done in free roam after the story. Kenton disappears for a while after Chapter 1, but his quests come back ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)).
- **You don't need everything.** Most tasks ask for a share, like 100 of 125 flamingos, so you can skip the hardest few.

## Story and crime (12)

- Finish the story, plus **100 optional tasks**.
- **All** 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 7 Payday Loan Jobs and 8 vaults; 34 of 35 Smash 'N' Grabs; 50 Stick Ups.
- **Fence** $100,000 of narcotics, $100,000 of jewellery, $250,000 of marked bills and $200,000 of vehicles ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-100-completion-checklist-guide/)). Our [store robbery guide](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-store-safe-code-cameras-escape/) covers the Stick Ups.
