Where did everyone go on launch weekend? Into Leonida, by the look of it. **Market research firm Alinea Analytics estimates daily active users of Call of Duty HQ fell 42% over GTA 6's launch weekend**, from an average of about **7.5 million to 4.4 million**, across PlayStation, Xbox and Steam ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Call-of-Duty-loses-42-players-thanks-to-GTA-6.1458798.0.html)). These are third-party estimates, not figures from Activision, Sony or Microsoft.

## Update, 26 November: Sensor Tower agrees

A second firm, Sensor Tower, sees the same pattern: **Call of Duty daily active users down 46% and Fortnite down 25%** over the launch weekend, with Crimson Desert and 007 First Light also losing players ([KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/gta-vi-launch-weekend-pulled-players-away-from-call-of-duty-fortnite-and-more/)). Sensor Tower also puts GTA 6 at [about 47.8 million copies by 22 November](/news/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-4-days-alinea-estimate/).

## The numbers

- **Call of Duty HQ** (home of Modern Warfare 4, Black Ops 7 and Warzone): down **42%**, and **44% on PlayStation**, its biggest platform. Four days after launch it was still **41% below** its usual level.
- **Fortnite** (PlayStation and Xbox): down **28%**, from 17.8 million to 12.8 million daily users, recovering to 19% below normal by the end of the weekend.
- **EA Sports FC 27:** down about **27%**, from 5.9 million to 4.3 million, now back within 20% of normal, helped partly by a 50% price cut.

All figures from Alinea via [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Call-of-Duty-loses-42-players-thanks-to-GTA-6.1458798.0.html).

## Take it with a pinch of salt

- **Modern Warfare 4 launched on 23 October**, 27 days before GTA 6, so some of the drop is the normal slide after a new Call of Duty.
- **PC players on Battle.net aren't counted**, and Alinea hasn't said what period its "normal" baseline covers.
- **Overlap:** 29% of GTA 6 players had also played Fortnite this month, against 16% for Call of Duty HQ, so the drop isn't only about shared players.

In October, Activision's marketing chief had argued GTA 6 might even bring lapsed players back to Call of Duty; these early numbers point the other way ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Call-of-Duty-loses-42-players-thanks-to-GTA-6.1458798.0.html)).
