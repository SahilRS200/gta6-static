The launch-week meme that has spread from TikTok to X, Threads and Reddit: **printed "GTA 6 contracts"** that give one partner **10 uninterrupted days** in Leonida, from **19 to 29 November** ([Unilad](https://www.unilad.com/technology/gta-6-couples-signing-contracts-for-uninterrupted-gameplay-183771-20261119)).

## What's in them

Going by the versions Unilad collected:

- **No hugs, chats or chores mid-mission.** They wait until the player finishes a mission or reaches a safehouse.
- **No standing in front of the TV** during a mission, cutscene or heist.
- **No backseat commentary** about how long you've been playing or how many times you failed a mission.
- **A $50 escape clause:** a partner who bought the game, or chipped in at least $50, can cancel one rule.

## Where it started, and the comeback

Singapore-based tech creator **Julian Tay** posted a video of his wife signing one, and it drew **roughly a million views** ([Unilad](https://www.unilad.com/technology/gta-6-couples-signing-contracts-for-uninterrupted-gameplay-183771-20261119)). Partners have hit back with **counter-contracts**: one X user who got the game as a gift from his girlfriend was handed four rules of his own, including checking in every few hours, pausing whenever she speaks, keeping gaming to half his free time and remembering that "Lucia is not your real girlfriend." None of it is legally binding, of course.
