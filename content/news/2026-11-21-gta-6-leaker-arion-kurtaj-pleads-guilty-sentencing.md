The man behind the biggest leak in GTA history has admitted it, the same week the finished game came out. **Arion Kurtaj**, now 21, pleaded guilty on **Monday, 16 November** to hacking Rockstar Games in September 2022 and trying to extort the studio ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/hacker-guilty), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/16/caught-gta-6-leaker-pleads-guilty-to-extorting-rockstar-games/)). **No story spoilers here.**

## Update, Monday 23 November: seven years, and free the same day

Kurtaj was **sentenced to seven years** at Woolwich Crown Court on Monday. Because of the time he had already spent locked up, he **left court a free man** the same day, [RTÉ](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1123/1600335-gta-hacker/) and [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/meridian/2026-11-23/hacker-who-leaked-clips-of-gta-6-jailed-for-seven-years) report. Mr Justice Turner called him "a very talented young man" and told him not to waste those talents going in and out of prison ([RTÉ](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1123/1600335-gta-hacker/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-hacker-released-from-prison-after-judge-warns-him-not-to-do-it-again-3430673/)). Besides the hacking and fraud, the charges he admitted included assaulting two emergency workers and having a phone in prison, RTÉ says.

## What happened

- **The leak:** in 2022 he posted **90 clips** of unfinished GTA 6 footage, close to an hour in all, under the name TeaPotUberHacker. That was more than a year before Rockstar's first trailer ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/hacker-guilty), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-leaker-pleads-guilty-to-Rockstar-hack-sentencing-set-for-November-23.1452228.0.html)).
- **The cost:** Rockstar estimated the breach cost it **$5 million** to recover from, plus thousands of hours of staff time.
- **The plea:** at Woolwich Crown Court in London. It also covers attacks on BT/EE, Revolut and Uber, which he carried out at 16 and 17, Notebookcheck reports. Because he pleaded guilty, there will be no trial.
- **Why now:** in 2023 he was found unfit to stand trial and given a hospital order. Doctors found him fit to stand trial in January 2026, Notebookcheck says.
