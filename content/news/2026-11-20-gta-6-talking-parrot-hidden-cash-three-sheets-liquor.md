Short on cash in your first hours? One of the easiest early stashes in GTA 6 is hidden behind a liquor store in **Key Lento**, and a **talking parrot** tells you where it is. Players found it on launch day. **No story spoilers**: this is a side errand near Jason's house.

## Step 1: Shirley's rum

- **Shirley** is Jason's neighbour on the same lane as his house; you meet her on the way home early on. She asks for a bottle of rum ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-complete-strange-noises-in-gta-6-shirley-house-liquor-store-location-guide/), [Screen Hype](https://www.screenhype.co.uk/where-to-find-the-secret-cash-in-gta-6-get-shirleys-rum-walkthrough/)).
- Get it at **Three Sheets Liquor** off the Sunbelt Highway, north of the Key Lento Pay 'n' Spray. It's free: she has a tab. Wait for the clerk to reach the counter, then pick the rum option ([Screen Hype](https://www.screenhype.co.uk/where-to-find-the-secret-cash-in-gta-6-get-shirleys-rum-walkthrough/)).

## Step 2: the parrot and the vent

- After you deliver the rum and play on a while, a **yellow mission icon** appears at the store ("Strange Noises"): the back of the shop is a front for exotic animals, with guards ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-complete-strange-noises-in-gta-6-shirley-house-liquor-store-location-guide/)).
- Deal with the guards: one in the back room, more who run in from outside, and a last one who tries to flee. Fists, stealth or guns all work ([Screen Hype](https://www.screenhype.co.uk/where-to-find-the-secret-cash-in-gta-6-get-shirleys-rum-walkthrough/), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-complete-strange-noises-in-gta-6-shirley-house-liquor-store-location-guide/)).
- In the outdoor area, **talk to the red-and-blue parrot** in the middle cage. He says the cash is hidden by the **generator**. Facing the parrot, look to the **back-left corner** and open the **vent** ([Screen Hype](https://www.screenhype.co.uk/where-to-find-the-secret-cash-in-gta-6-get-shirleys-rum-walkthrough/)).

## How much you get

Insider Gaming counted **$1,200** in the vent and about **$2,500** across the whole place, including a **safe** in the back and loose cash, plus adrenaline shots ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/how-to-complete-strange-noises-in-gta-6-shirley-house-liquor-store-location-guide/)). Other guides put the vent at about $1,300, so expect roughly that.
