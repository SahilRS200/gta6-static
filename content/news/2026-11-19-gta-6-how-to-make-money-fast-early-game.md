GTA 6 has been out in India since **12:00 AM IST**, and the first complaint after "my PS5 is loud" is "I have no money." Early on you usually hold only **a few thousand dollars** of in-game cash at a time ([ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/gta-6-how-to-make-money-early-in-the-game/)). Guides written in the first day agree on a handful of reliable earners. **No story spoilers below**, only the jobs and side activities the game opens up in its first hours.

## The best early earners

1. **Dive shipwrecks.** Treasure diving opens very early: a contact gives you a boat and a scanner, and you bring up valuables from sunken wrecks. Outputlag puts the first wreck at about **$1,400**, the first two at about **$2,400**, and later sites at up to about **$4,000** per dive, with very little risk ([Outputlag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-make-money-fast/)). Vice notes the payout can arrive a few in-game hours later, once the goods are sold ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast/)). How to unlock it and use the metal detector: [GTA 6 treasure diving guide](/news/gta-6-treasure-diving-scuba-kenton-shipwrecks/).
2. **Raid gang hideouts and illegal operations.** Vice calls stealing product from illegal operations and selling it on the **fastest early method**; ComicBook says raiding Kingfishers gang hideouts is a quick way to raise cash. Expect armed guards, so go in with full health.
3. **Sell stolen cars.** Early on you can sell cars at any Pay 'n' Spray, but sales are **capped per in-game day**. Later, a car buyer with **no daily limit** opens up and pays hundreds to a couple of thousand dollars per car; cars fitted with trackers are refused at first ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast/), [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/gta-6-how-to-make-money-early-in-the-game/)). New to stealing? [How car theft works now →](/news/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars-waink-app-hotwire-key-cloner/)
4. **Rob stores, once the story allows it.** Petrol stations, pawn shops and jewellery stores open up as you progress. Outputlag puts a small store at a few hundred dollars; grab the till and counter valuables and leave before the police arrive. Step by step: [how to rob a store](/news/gta-6-how-to-rob-a-store-safe-code-cameras-escape/).
5. **Watch the sea.** Outputlag reports floating packages from exploded boats worth about **$1,500–2,000** together. Sharks are the catch.

## Don't miss these

- **Ask the parrot.** Behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento, a caged parrot points you to a vent with about $1,200–1,300, after Shirley's rum errand. [Where →](/news/gta-6-talking-parrot-hidden-cash-three-sheets-liquor/)
- **Wait for the jet-ski dog.** Float alone in Gambit Bay for about 5 minutes and Cool Dog rides up, takes you to his island and digs up $1,200. [How →](/news/gta-6-cool-dog-lucky-dog-jet-ski-1200-cash/)
- **Check your mailbox.** Mission pay and fence money often lands in the **mailbox outside your house**, not in your hand. Check it whenever you leave home ([Outputlag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-make-money-fast/)).
- **Fences pay badly early.** The first buyers for jewellery and marked bills pay "very low" or "low" rates, so don't take big risks for small loot ([ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/gta-6-how-to-make-money-early-in-the-game/)).
- **Cash won't cover cops forever.** A big score brings heat; see [how to lose the cops](/news/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained/), and [keep your tank full](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/) before a getaway.
- **Heard about the money glitch?** A red-barn save-and-reload loop paid about $11,280 on its first run in Beebom's test, but many players are skipping it. [How it works and the catches →](/news/gta-6-money-glitch-red-barn-unlimited-cash/)
- **Cheats won't help yet.** There is no money cheat on day one, and cheats unlock only after the story. [Why →](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/)
- **No casino shortcut.** The casinos can't be entered and there's no gambling. [Why →](/news/gta-6-casino-cant-enter-gambling-rockstar-explains/)
- **Bank it or stash it.** Until your first bank account, you lose the cash you carry if you die or get busted. [Where to hide it →](/news/gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die-hide-cash-floorboards/)
- **Save before a risky job.** There's only one autosave slot. [How to save →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
- **A one-off $6,250:** break into Freddie Gibbs' doomsday bunker by Lake Leonida and choose to leave. [Compound guide →](/news/gta-6-freddie-gibbs-doomsday-compound-bunker-break-in/)
- **Collect trading cards.** Duplicates and rare Gold cards sell at pawn shops, and selling keeps your progress. [Trading cards guide →](/news/gta-6-trading-cards-480-collectible-cards-sell-pawn-shop-panini/)
- **Wondering what to spend it on?** You can't buy houses, only garages ($160,000 for all eight). [Property guide →](/news/gta-6-buy-property-garages-prices-real-estate-app/)
