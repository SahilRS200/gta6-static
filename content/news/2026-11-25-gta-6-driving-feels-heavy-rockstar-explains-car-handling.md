If your first hours of GTA 6 involved spinning Jason's truck into a ditch, you're not alone. Many players say the Chapter 1 truck handles badly and that they needed repairs several times in their first 10 hours. Rockstar North co-studio head **Rob Nelson** has now explained why driving feels the way it does ([GAMINGbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/rockstar-explains-gta-6-driving-mechanics-751466-20261124)). No story spoilers.

## What Rockstar says

- **It's deliberate.** Nelson called the change "nerve-wracking" to make and admitted some players will love it and some won't. He says it isn't there to frustrate anyone: players asked for more realism (GAMINGbible).
- **Cars have weight now.** Vehicles behave more like real machines, crumple more and wear out faster. Early builds let one or two hits total a car, so the team rebalanced damage (GAMINGbible).
- **Narrower roads, more traffic.** Roads are narrower than in GTA 5 and traffic is denser, including city jams, to make Leonida feel believable (GAMINGbible).
- **The goal:** GTA 4's weight with GTA 5's ease of control, Nelson said in an earlier interview ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/grand-theft-auto-6s-driving-mixes-the-weightiness-of-gta-4-and-gta-5s-accessibility)).

## How to live with it

- **Slow down for corners** and brake earlier than you would in GTA 5; heavier cars slide when you throw them in.
- **Repair instead of ditching the car:** fix damage at a petrol station, or from Chapter 2 through the Scooter Bros app for a fee (GAMINGbible). [Car wrecked or impounded? →](/news/gta-6-car-impounded-get-it-back-scooter-bros/)
- **Watch the fuel gauge** too: [cars need petrol →](/news/gta-6-fuel-petrol-system-how-to-refuel/)
- **Drive your best cars gently.** Every crash costs repair money you could keep (GAMINGbible).
- **Still hate it?** There's no handling slider, but [auto-drive and easier settings](/news/gta-6-difficulty-accessibility-settings-auto-drive/) can take the wheel on long trips.
