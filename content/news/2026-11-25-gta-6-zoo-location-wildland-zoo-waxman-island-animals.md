Filling GTA 6's wildlife journal? A few animals don't show up in the wild at all, and you'll only log them at the zoo. Here's where it is, spoiler-free.

## Where is the zoo?

- **Wildland Zoo is on Waxman Island**, east of Vice City and right next to Starfish Island. Its outline is easy to pick out on the map ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gta-6-zoo-location-guide-how-to-find-exclusive-animals/)).
- **Two ways in.** You can walk through the main gate and pay for a ticket, which TheGamer calls steep, or look for a sneakier route in if you'd rather keep the cash. TheGamer doesn't give the exact price.

## Which animals are zoo-only?

- **Chimpanzees and capuchin monkeys** appear to be found only at the zoo, TheGamer reports, so a visit is needed if you're trying to study every animal in Leonida.
- TheGamer's screenshots also show **tigers and parrots** in the enclosures.
- **How to log them:** hold L1 (LB), pick Binoculars, zoom in until "Study" appears and hold R1 (RB), the same as for wild animals. Full steps are in our [hunting and wildlife guide](/news/gta-6-hunting-guide-bret-target-decks-how-to-unlock/).

## Is there a second zoo?

Yes, a smaller one at **Emerald Springs in Keeley County, near Port Gellhorn**. TheGamer says it's not worth a special trip on its own, apart from one mission that takes you there.
