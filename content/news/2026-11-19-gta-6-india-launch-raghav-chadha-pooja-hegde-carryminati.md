GTA 6 unlocked in India at **12:00 AM IST today**, and it wasn't only gamers counting down. A Member of Parliament, a Bollywood and Telugu film star and India's biggest YouTuber all posted about the launch, according to [News Ei Samay](https://newseisamay.com/entertainment/entertainment-others/gta-6-finally-launches-fans-go-wild-as-celebrities-join-the-hype).

## Who joined the midnight hype

- **Raghav Chadha, Rajya Sabha MP:** posted a video of himself in front of his TV with a controller, watching the clock run down to midnight, then smiling at the camera as the game unlocked. His caption: "11:59 PM has never felt this long." He called it a glimpse of life beyond Parliament.
- **Pooja Hegde, actor:** shared her GTA 6 preload queue on Instagram Stories with the caption "All geared up."
- **CarryMinati (Ajey Nagar):** posted a reel looking back at his old GTA V videos, captioned with the classic "Aah shit, here we go again."
- **Mythpat and Anshu Bisht:** the gaming creators also posted launch content. Mythpat is one of the four Indian creators on [YouTube's 24-hour GTA 6 stream](/news/gta-6-youtube-24-hour-livestream-indian-creators/), which starts at 10:30 AM IST today.

## What Indian players are saying

On X, tech creator Mukul Sharma said streaming videos "don't do justice" to how the game looks, and other players posted that GTA 6 is "finally playable in India" after 13 years, reports [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/gaming/gta-6-india-launch-internet-erupts-with-video-clips-of-jason-and-lucia-committing-crimes-in-leonida-check-first-look.html). News Ei Samay adds that fans joked about taking leave, booking hotel rooms to play without interruption, and finally retiring the "we got X before GTA 6" meme.
