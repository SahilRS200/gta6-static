Rockstar has teased aliens in every GTA since San Andreas, and in GTA 6 you can actually watch a **UFO abduction** and earn a **hidden trophy** for it. It's a short night-time side event, not a story mission. Here is how to trigger it. **No story spoilers.**

## Update, 28 November: the full alien quest and its permanent stat boost

The abduction is only part one. Finish the chain and **both Jason and Lucia get permanent boosts to max Health, Stamina and Focus** ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-full-alien-ufo-questline-walkthrough/), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175841-how-to-get-abducted-by-aliens-and-complete-the-secret-quest-in-gta-6.html)). It's a side quest with no story spoilers.

1. **Sleep as the character who was abducted.** Sooner or later they have a nightmare aboard the UFO. Keep sleeping at a safehouse if it doesn't come the first night.
2. **Solve the dream.** Above the keypad, the code is drawn as groups of lines (two lines = 2, four lines = 4). VGC's code was 2431 but it can differ, so read your own. The light panel: four lights cycle off, red, blue. Set them all to red, then press buttons 2 and 4 once each to turn them all blue. For the milk puzzle, carry the milk from the **left** room to the machine in the **right** room; the middle door leads to radiation ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-full-alien-ufo-questline-walkthrough/)). VGTimes adds an egg room: crouch along the left wall so you don't break the eggs ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175841-how-to-get-abducted-by-aliens-and-complete-the-secret-quest-in-gta-6.html)).
3. **Find the real facility.** It's a fenced, angular building in the swamp **south of Hamlet in Pelican Valley**, just west of the main road north from Key Lento. Bring a truck or van, park at the back and climb the metal frame over the barbed wire. The keypad uses your dream code ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-full-alien-ufo-questline-walkthrough/)).
4. **Repeat the puzzles and free the alien.** Take the lift down, redo the light and milk puzzles, then carry the alien to the surface. Expect an alarm and gas inside and police outside; the UFO helps you escape ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/guide/gta-6-full-alien-ufo-questline-walkthrough/), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175841-how-to-get-abducted-by-aliens-and-complete-the-secret-quest-in-gta-6.html)).

**Save by hand before you go in**, since the escape can kill you. Neither guide gives the exact size of the boost.

## Before you go

- **You need Chapter 2.** Beebom says the event only appears once Chapter 2 is open ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ufo-location-trophy-guide/)).
- **Go at night, at 2:00 AM in-game.** games.gg says any time after 2:00 AM works and to **go alone** ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/where-to-find-the-ufo-in-gta-6/)). Use a safehouse bed to skip time: [how sleeping works](/news/gta-6-how-to-sleep-skip-time-day-length/).
- **Where:** rural roads in **Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn**, close to the **Starlet Motel** safehouse ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ufo-location-trophy-guide/)). Guides differ on the exact road, so drive the back roads near the motel and look for the car below.

## Step by step

1. **Find the parked car** with flashing lights, an alarm going and its doors open.
2. **Follow the trail of clothes** from the car into the trees until you see a white beam of light from the UFO.
3. **Walk into the light.** The UFO drops the woman and flies off, and the screen goes almost black.
4. **Help her:** press **Triangle** (PS5) or **Y** (Xbox), then pick a reply. Use the button the on-screen prompt shows; guides list different ones.
5. **Do what she asks:** she wants her car back from the road. Walk back to it and the event finishes, and the hidden trophy **"The Truth Is Out There"** pops ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ufo-location-trophy-guide/), [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/where-to-find-the-ufo-in-gta-6/)).

**No car there?** The event is random. games.gg says to drive away and come back to try again.
