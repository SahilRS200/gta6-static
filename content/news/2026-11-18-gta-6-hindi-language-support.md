"GTA 6 Hindi mein aayega?" is one of the biggest questions from Indian players this week. The short answer: **no, not at launch.**

## What the store listings show

GTA 6's PlayStation and Xbox listings show **13 language entries**. **Hindi is not one of them, and neither is any other Indian language** ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.in/gta-6-language-list-hindi/)).

The 13 entries, as compiled by [Gamemapa](https://gamemapa.com/en/gta-6/guides/gta-6-languages/):

- English, French, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese, Polish, Korean
- Chinese (Simplified) and Chinese (Traditional)
- Spanish (Spain) and Spanish (Mexico)
- Portuguese (Brazil)

## Only English is spoken

**Only English has voice acting.** Every other language gets translated menus and subtitles, not a dub. So even players in Germany or Japan hear the game in English.

## Will Hindi come later?

**Rockstar hasn't announced any plans to add languages**, Hindi included. [TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/editorials/gta-6-india-marketing-launch-editorial/) also points out that GTA 6 has had no India-specific campaign: the ads here are the global ones, and there's no Hindi version. Anything you see claiming a "Hindi patch" or "Hindi dub download" is not official. Don't install random files to get it. ## What to set tonight

- Audio will be **English** whatever you pick, so choose English for menus and subtitles too unless you read another listed language more comfortably.
- Turn **subtitles on** if you find fast American slang hard to follow. Rockstar games have plenty of dialogue while you drive.
