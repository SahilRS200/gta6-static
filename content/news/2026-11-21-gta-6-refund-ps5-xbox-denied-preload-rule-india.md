**Short answer: if your PS5 started downloading GTA 6, including the pre-load, Sony will almost certainly refuse a change-of-mind refund.** Since launch on 19 November, players unhappy with the 30fps frame rate, glitches or the gameplay have asked for their money back, and many requests are being denied under Sony's digital rules, The Blaze reports ([The Blaze](https://www.theblaze.com/tech/gamers-demand-gta-6-refunds)). The game was pre-loaded by most buyers, and that is what closes the door.

## PS5: what Sony India's policy says

- **14 days, but only if you haven't downloaded.** You can cancel a game purchase within 14 days, but only if you have not started downloading or streaming it ([PlayStation India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/)).
- **Pre-loading counts as downloading.** Pre-order cancellations also apply only if you hadn't started downloading or pre-loading the main game, and Automatic Downloads can start it for you ([PlayStation India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/)). Pausing or deleting the download doesn't reset this ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/can-you-cancel-a-gta-6-pre-order-refund-rules-for-ps5-and-xbox/)).
- **The exception is a faulty game.** Downloaded content can still be refunded if it is faulty ([PlayStation India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/)). If GTA 6 won't run at all, try our [crash fixes](/news/gta-6-crashing-freezing-ps5-overheating-xbox-fix/) and [won't-start fixes](/news/gta-6-locked-not-starting-ps5-fix-india/) first, then contact PlayStation Support.
- **How to ask:** open the order in your account's Transaction History and look for a refund option; if there isn't one, contact PlayStation Support with your Online ID, sign-in email and the purchase name ([PlayStation India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/)). Refunds usually go back to the original payment method or your PlayStation wallet ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/can-you-cancel-a-gta-6-pre-order-refund-rules-for-ps5-and-xbox/)).

## Xbox

- After release, Microsoft generally treats digital games as final, but you can still send a request through the Xbox refund page. It can take about 72 hours and approval isn't guaranteed ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/can-you-cancel-a-gta-6-pre-order-refund-rules-for-ps5-and-xbox/)).

## Bought the box from Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon or a shop?

- The "physical" GTA 6 is a [box with a download code](/news/blinkit-zepto-gta-6-delivery-10-minutes-india/), so returns follow **that seller's** policy, not Sony's. Check the terms before you open the box or redeem the code ([games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/can-you-cancel-a-gta-6-pre-order-refund-rules-for-ps5-and-xbox/)).
