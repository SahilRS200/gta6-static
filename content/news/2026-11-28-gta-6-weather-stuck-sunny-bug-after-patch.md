Been playing GTA 6 for hours and still haven't seen a proper storm? You're not imagining it. A week after launch, many players say Leonida is stuck on **clear, sunny skies** for long stretches, and some say they've barely seen weather outside scripted story moments ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-weather-system-broken/)). No story spoilers below.

## Isn't this the bug the patch fixed?

It's meant to be. Update **01.002.002** on 23 November listed a fix for weather not changing for "extended periods", which confirmed it was a bug and not a design choice ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/new-gta-6-update-patch-notes-november-23-2026/)). But players in a popular Reddit thread say it still happens for them after updating, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X, and some are asking Rockstar to say something publicly ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-weather-system-broken/)). Rockstar hasn't commented yet. [What the patch did fix →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

## The two-minute PS5 fix players are using

**Update, 28 November:** players have found a workaround that brings rain and storms back for many of them. It clears the PS5's system cache from Safe Mode, and GamesRadar+ reports the fix came from fans on social media after Rockstar's patch left them waiting ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-players-fix-the-weather-system-themselves-after-rockstars-latest-patch-left-them-hanging-its-super-easy-and-takes-like-two-minutes/)). The steps, as reported ([GameGPU](https://en.gamegpu.com/news/igry/igroki-nashli-prostoj-sposob-ispravit-bag-s-pogodoj-v-gta-6-na-konsoli-playstation-5), [Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/gaming/gta-6-weather-bug-fix-202611)):

1. **Turn the PS5 off completely** (not Rest Mode).
2. **Hold the power button** for about seven seconds, until the second beep. The console starts in Safe Mode.
3. **Plug the DualSense in with a USB cable** and press the PS button.
4. Pick option 6, **Clear Cache and Rebuild Database**, then **Clear System Software Cache**, and confirm the restart.
5. If the weather is still stuck, go back and run **Rebuild Database** from the same menu.

Both reports say clearing the cache doesn't delete installed games or your save files, and the whole thing takes about two minutes. It doesn't work for everyone, and Rockstar hasn't endorsed it.

## On Xbox

Shut the console down fully, **unplug the power cable for about two minutes**, then plug it back in and start the game ([Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/gaming/gta-6-weather-bug-fix-202611)). This is general troubleshooting rather than a tested weather fix.

## Before you try anything

- **Check you're on the latest version.** On PS5, highlight GTA VI and press **Options → Check for Update**; on Xbox, open **My games & apps → Manage → Updates**.
- **Remember weather is local.** Rockstar built weather that can soak one part of the map while another stays dry, so drive somewhere else before deciding it's broken.
- **Save by hand first.** A manual save before any restart costs nothing.

## Does it matter?

It doesn't block the story, but weather changes how the game plays: rain makes roads slippery and storms change how boats and planes handle. If you're chasing photos or the full experience, it's worth reporting to Rockstar Support with your platform and game version.
