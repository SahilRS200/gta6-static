GTA 6 has a quiet nod to **Michael De Santa** and his son **Jimmy** from GTA 5. It isn't a mission or a cameo you play: their names are printed in the credits of a fake movie poster you can find around Vice City. Players spotted it and **GamingBible** reported it on 25 November ([GamingBible](https://gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-confirms-what-happened-michael-jimmy-after-gta-5-easter-egg-585546-20261125)). **No GTA 6 story spoilers here.**

## How to see it

1. **Look for posters for *Righteous Slaughter Overkill*,** a film poster displayed around Vice City, GamingBible says.
2. **Zoom in on the small print.** Use the camera or [Photo Studio, the photo mode](/news/gta-6-photo-mode-photo-studio-selfie-how-to/), to read the credits at the bottom.
3. **Find the names.** Both **Michael De Santa** and **Jimmy De Santa** are listed. The poster doesn't make clear what each one did on the film; GamingBible says it's unclear whether they're producers, directors or something else.

A player on X first spotted the credits, and the find spread after DiscussingFilm shared it, GamingBible reports. Rockstar hasn't commented.

## Why it's a neat nod

- **Michael went into films.** In GTA 5, Michael ends up in the movie business in Vinewood, so a film credit fits.
- **Jimmy loved *Righteous Slaughter*.** In GTA 5, Michael can watch Jimmy play *Righteous Slaughter 7*, the series' spoof of Call of Duty. GTA 6 also has Slaughter Hood Ops T-shirts ([GamingBible](https://gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-confirms-what-happened-michael-jimmy-after-gta-5-easter-egg-585546-20261125)).
