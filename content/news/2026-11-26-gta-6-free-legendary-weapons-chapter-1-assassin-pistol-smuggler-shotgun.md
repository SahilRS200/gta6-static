You don't need Ammu-Nation money to get good guns early. GTA 6 hides **three legendary weapons you can pick up for free in Chapter 1**, two of them a short boat ride from where Jason starts, [GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-chapter-1-legendary-weapon-locations-2ea1) and TheGamer report. No story spoilers below, only places.

## 1. Assassin Nipper .38 — the silenced pistol

- **Where:** a beached wooden boat in the **Permit Keys**. GTA BOOM puts it on the narrow island west of Maroon Key; [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gta-6-legendary-assassins-pistol-unlock-guide-how-to-find-best-starting-weapon/) says it's the island near Southwind, close to Jason's starting point.
- **How:** scan the boat with the Waink app (it shows up as a **Derelict Spy Vessel**), climb aboard, use Focus to spot the hatch in the deck, and open it.
- **Why bother:** it comes with a **silencer already fitted**, which is rare this early without buying mods, TheGamer says.

## 2. Smuggler Gorse 425 — the shotgun

- **Where:** the **northernmost island** of the same Permit Keys chain, where the marsh trees meet the water. Look on the ground beside a small tilted boat, on the side it leans towards ([GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-chapter-1-legendary-weapon-locations-2ea1)).
- **Tip:** bring a flashlight if it's dark. [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gta-6-legendary-smugglers-shotgun-unlock-guide-location-how-to-find/) says it hits harder than the shotguns you can buy, and the pistol is close enough to grab both in one trip.

## 3. Kingfishers W-22 — the suppressed rifle

- **Where:** inside a lit shack at the **Fishing Shacks** on the middle island of **Wetman Bay**, west of Pelican Valley. It's water-only, so take Jason's fishing boat.
- **When:** the area opens after a few story missions, when Donnie gives you a side job to rob the Kingfisher gang, [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-legendary-rifle-kingfisher-w-22-rifle-location-how-to-get-where-to-find-gta-guide/) says.
- **How:** it's a guarded spot, so heal up first, bring a long rifle and a shotgun for the guards who rush you, fight to the large red house on the dock and take the rifle from the open case on the left table. It comes with a red-dot sight and a suppressor; good for quiet long shots and small game, weak on big animals.
- **Bonus:** loot the bricks in the room and the shed and sell them in Key Lento for early cash.
