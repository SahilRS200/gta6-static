Formula 1 driver **Max Verstappen** says GTA 6 ate about **40 hours** of his Las Vegas Grand Prix week, and he still won the race.

## What he said

- **"40 hours. I played a lot in the evening and the morning in Vegas,"** Verstappen said on Tuesday, according to BroBible ([BroBible](https://brobible.com/sports/article/max-verstappen-gta-6-george-russell/)).
- **He won the Las Vegas Grand Prix** ahead of Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri and Kimi Antonelli ([BroBible](https://brobible.com/sports/article/max-verstappen-gta-6-george-russell/)).
- **Earlier that weekend he hadn't had time to play.** "It's already pre-downloaded. I just didn't have the time yet to play," he said, as F1 drivers shared their GTA 6 plans ([F1-Fansite](https://www.f1-fansite.com/f1-news/verstappen-has-gta6-downloaded-as-f1-drivers-reveal-plans/)).
- He didn't say which missions or activities filled those hours. Some smaller sites claim he is 80% through the story; we couldn't confirm that.
