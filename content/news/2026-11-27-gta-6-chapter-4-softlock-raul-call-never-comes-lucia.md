Stuck in **Chapter 4** of GTA 6, waiting for **Raul to call Lucia** so the story can carry on? You're not alone. Players report that the call simply **never arrives**, however long they wait in-game, at roughly 72% of the story ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-story-softlock-bug-how-to-fix/)). No story spoilers below.

## Did the patch fix it?

**No.** Update 01.002.002 on 23 November fixed some story phone calls that didn't trigger, but players say this one still happens after updating. Rockstar hasn't commented on it, and the cause isn't known ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-story-softlock-bug-how-to-fix/)). [What the patch did fix →](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/)

## Workaround to try

This is a player-found workaround, **not guaranteed** to work for everyone ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-story-softlock-bug-how-to-fix/)):

1. **Finish Yasmani's jobs**, including the optional paid robbery tips and his jewellery store robberies if you haven't done them.
2. **Clear Lucia's phone:** open it and dismiss every pending notification and reminder.
3. **Split them up.** Make sure Jason and Lucia aren't hanging out together.
4. **Save by hand as Lucia** in their Vice City hotel room. [How manual saves work →](/news/gta-6-how-to-save-manual-save-crash-lost-progress/)
5. **Fully shut down the console** (PS5 or Xbox), not rest mode, then start it again.
6. **Load that save** and walk out of the hotel room as Lucia, on her own.

If it works, **a different contact may call first**. Do that job, then Raul's call should follow ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-story-softlock-bug-how-to-fix/)).

## Still stuck?

- **Report it** to Rockstar Support with your platform and game version, so it gets into the next patch.
- **Keep a spare manual save** before every big story step from now on, so a softlock never costs you hours.
