Can't stay up for the midnight unlock, or don't own a PS5 yet? You can still watch GTA 6 all day tomorrow. **YouTube is running a 24-hour GTA 6 launch livestream** on its official channel, and **four Indian creators are reportedly in the line-up** ([ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/technology/youtube-gta-6-launch-24-hour-livestream-payal-gaming-total-gaming-sharkshe-enn26111803910)).

## When to watch, in IST

- **Pre-show: 9:30 AM IST, Thursday 19 November.** Hosted by the US creator group RDCWorld.
- **Main stream: 10:30 AM IST, 19 November**, running for 24 hours until about **10:30 AM IST on Friday 20 November** ([Techgenyz](https://techgenyz.com/gta-6-youtube-livestream-india-time/)).
- **Where:** YouTube's official channel, not the creators' personal channels, unless they announce their own streams.

That's roughly **10 hours after the game unlocks in India** at 12:00 AM IST, so you can play first and tune in after you sleep. [Exact unlock time →](/gta-6-release-time/india/)

## The Indian creators

More than 30 creators are part of the global stream, according to reports. The Indian names:

- **Total Gaming** (Ajju Bhai)
- **Payal Gaming**
- **Sharkshe**
- **Mythpat**, added later as the fourth Indian creator, per a press note from his team reported by [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/mythpat-joins-youtubes-global-creator-line-up-for-gta-vi-launch-12820522)

Expect live gameplay, creator chats and other segments. The line-up comes from press reports, and exact slots for the Indian creators haven't been published, so check the stream's schedule on the day.

## ⚠️ Spoiler warning

A 24-hour gameplay stream will move through the story fast. If you want to go in fresh, watch the first hour at most, or stick to clips from the open world. Mute "GTA 6 ending" and "GTA 6 spoilers" on X, Instagram and YouTube for the first week.
