While India plays Vice City, a homegrown game is getting attention for doing the opposite: turning **Bengaluru traffic** into the open world. **Namma Benglooru**, an Indian-made browser game, is going viral amid the GTA 6 launch, [Oneindia reports](https://www.oneindia.com/bengaluru/bengaluru-gets-its-own-gta-style-game-namma-benglooru-goes-viral-amid-gta-6-buzz-8265025.html). To be clear: **it has nothing to do with Rockstar or GTA 6**.

## What it is

- **A stylised Koramangala**, built from OpenStreetMap data with real street names, full of potholes, honking, autorickshaws and crowded roads.
- You play **Arjun, a delivery worker**: street races, odd jobs, run-ins with the police and changing weather. One scene has him haggling over rent with a PG owner.
- **The free demo has 12 missions and 18 vehicle types.** It runs in a browser on a desktop or laptop, not on phones yet. Android and PC versions are in development, with no date.

## The numbers

According to [Oneindia](https://www.oneindia.com/bengaluru/bengaluru-gets-its-own-gta-style-game-namma-benglooru-goes-viral-amid-gta-6-buzz-8265025.html), about **4.5 lakh players** tried the demo in its first month, more than **32,000** signed up for updates in November, and the trailer has passed **4.3 lakh YouTube views**. Demo videos in Kannada, Tamil, English and Hindi add over 4 lakh more.
