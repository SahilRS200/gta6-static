## Update, 27 November: about 53 million in week one

- **About 53 million copies in the first week,** Alinea Analytics now estimates, worth **about $4.7 billion** ([Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/grand-theft-auto-6-a-week-in), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175989-gta-6-sold-an-estimated-53-million-copies-in-its-first-week.html)).
- **PS5 75.7%, Xbox about 13 million (24.3%).** Xbox's share has slipped from 25.8% on 21 November ([Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/grand-theft-auto-6-a-week-in)).
- **Nearly 7 million players are past 50 hours,** and about 250,000 past 100 ([Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/grand-theft-auto-6-a-week-in)).
- **The US is 33.5% of PS5 copies.** Again there's no India figure; the only India estimate is still Sensor Tower's 2.38 lakh.
- **Still unofficial.** Rockstar and Take-Two haven't announced a week-one count.

A new estimate puts a copies figure on GTA 6's first weekend. **Market research firm Alinea Analytics estimates Grand Theft Auto VI sold about 44.9 million copies between launch on 19 November and 22 November**, Beebom and GameSpot report ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-is-approaching-an-absurd-new-sales-milestone-report-says/)).

## Update, 26 November: Sensor Tower says 47.8 million

- **A second firm, a higher number.** Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 reached **about 47.8 million copies on PlayStation and Xbox by Sunday, 22 November**, about 24.6 million of them pre-orders ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/conormurray/2026/11/24/gta-vi-sold-47-million-copies-over-launch-weekend-and-siphoned-players-from-call-of-duty-fortnite/), [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/gta-vi-launch-weekend-pulled-players-away-from-call-of-duty-fortnite-and-more/)).
- **About 35% of active PS5 and Xbox Series X/S consoles have it installed,** Sensor Tower says ([KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/gta-vi-launch-weekend-pulled-players-away-from-call-of-duty-fortnite-and-more/), [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/gta-vi-crashing-ps5-2000845327)).
- **Players are deep in already:** by Sunday evening, **37% of players had logged more than 20 hours and 7% more than 40**, by Sensor Tower's count ([KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/gta-vi-launch-weekend-pulled-players-away-from-call-of-duty-fortnite-and-more/)).
- **Still an estimate.** Rockstar and Take-Two haven't given a post-launch copies figure. Sensor Tower is the same firm behind the [2.38 lakh India estimate](/news/gta-6-india-sales-238000-ps5-copies-3-days-sensor-tower/).

## The Alinea estimate

- **44.9 million copies in four days** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **PS5: about 33.7 million copies (75.1%). Xbox Series X/S: about 11.2 million (24.9%)** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **Over $4 billion in revenue**, more than $3 billion of it from PS5 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **About 40.8 million people played on Sunday, 22 November**, and nearly 9 million have already spent more than 20 hours in Leonida ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).

## Read it with care

- **This is an estimate, not Rockstar's number.** Alinea's method hasn't been independently checked ([Gadget Review](https://www.gadgetreview.com/gta-vi-launch-weekend-sales-estimated-at-44-9-million-copies)). The official figures are still Take-Two's **$3.79 billion** in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before launch. [The official sales →](/news/gta-6-official-sales-3-79-billion-day-one-record/)
- **It fits Sony's own count.** Sony says more than 39 million people played on PS5 in the same four days, its biggest launch ever. Players aren't copies, though: one copy on a shared console can count more than once. [Sony's numbers →](/news/gta-6-39-million-ps5-players-biggest-playstation-launch/)
- **There's no India figure** in Alinea's estimate.
