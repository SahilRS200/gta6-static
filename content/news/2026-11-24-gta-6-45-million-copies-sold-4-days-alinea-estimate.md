A new estimate puts a copies figure on GTA 6's first weekend. **Market research firm Alinea Analytics estimates Grand Theft Auto VI sold about 44.9 million copies between launch on 19 November and 22 November**, Beebom and GameSpot report ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-is-approaching-an-absurd-new-sales-milestone-report-says/)).

## The estimate

- **44.9 million copies in four days** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **PS5: about 33.7 million copies (75.1%). Xbox Series X/S: about 11.2 million (24.9%)** ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **Over $4 billion in revenue**, more than $3 billion of it from PS5 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).
- **About 40.8 million people played on Sunday, 22 November**, and nearly 9 million have already spent more than 20 hours in Leonida ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-45-million-copies-sold-in-4-days-since-launch/)).

## Read it with care

- **This is an estimate, not Rockstar's number.** Alinea's method hasn't been independently checked ([Gadget Review](https://www.gadgetreview.com/gta-vi-launch-weekend-sales-estimated-at-44-9-million-copies)). The official figures are still Take-Two's **$3.79 billion** in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before launch. [The official sales →](/news/gta-6-official-sales-3-79-billion-day-one-record/)
- **It fits Sony's own count.** Sony says more than 39 million people played on PS5 in the same four days, its biggest launch ever. Players aren't copies, though: one copy on a shared console can count more than once. [Sony's numbers →](/news/gta-6-39-million-ps5-players-biggest-playstation-launch/)
- **There's no India figure** in Alinea's estimate.
