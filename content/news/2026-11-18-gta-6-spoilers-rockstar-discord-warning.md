With New Zealand, Australia, Japan and Korea already playing, India is one of the last big regions still waiting. That makes the next few hours prime spoiler time. Rockstar has now stepped in on its own turf: a post on the **official GTA 6 Discord** asks members to be mindful of spoilers and says that **intentionally posting spoilers to troll others will get you removed from the server**, as reported by [IBTimes Singapore](https://www.ibtimes.sg/gta-6-spoilers-could-get-you-banned-rockstar-introduces-strict-discord-rules-96369) and [GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/rockstar-issues-an-official-gta-6-spoiler-alert-on-its-discord-as-launch-begins-16d7).

## What the rule actually says

- **Deliberate spoiler trolling = removal from the Discord server.** Accidental slips aren't the target.
- **Spoiler tags are required** for major story moments.
- **Story talk moves to dedicated channels.** Rockstar says it plans separate GTA 6 channels for characters, story and big moments; no opening time has been given ([TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/gta-6/rockstar-gta-6-spoiler-warning-discord/)).

## What it does not mean

This is a **Discord server rule only**. It is not a ban from playing GTA 6 and does not touch your PSN or Xbox account or your digital copy ([IBTimes Singapore](https://www.ibtimes.sg/gta-6-spoilers-could-get-you-banned-rockstar-introduces-strict-discord-rules-96369)). If you see posts claiming "Rockstar will ban your console for spoilers", that's not what was announced.

## How to stay spoiler-free until 12 AM IST

Gameplay clips from early regions are already all over X, as [Sunday Guardian](https://sundayguardianlive.com/trending/watch-viral-video-gta-6-finally-goes-live-after-13-years-first-gameplay-clips-flood-x-but-indian-fans-watching-the-clips-could-face-major-spoilers-324058) warned. A few minutes of setup helps:

- **X/Twitter:** mute "GTA 6 spoiler", "GTA 6 ending", "GTA VI story" and the main characters' names.
- **YouTube and Instagram:** skip Shorts and Reels for the evening; thumbnails are where most spoilers land.
- **Discord and WhatsApp groups:** mute GTA channels until you've played, or ask friends to use spoiler tags.
- **Reviews:** stick to spoiler-free ones. Our [Metacritic 99 roundup](/news/gta-6-reviews-metacritic-99/) has no story details.

Then go to bed early, or don't: India unlocks at **12:00 AM IST tonight**.
