One ordinary-looking house in GTA 6 hides one of its strangest secrets: a **garage turned into a conspiracy den by someone who has worked out he's living inside a video game**. Red string, scribbled notes and brochures cover the walls ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/theres-an-npc-in-gta-6-who-knows-hes-in-a-video-game-heres-how-to-find-his-house/)). It's a side secret, not a story mission. **No story spoilers here.**

## Where it is

- **Belville, in Leonard County,** a small town north of Vice City, not far from Lake Leonida ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-screenshot-hidden-in-san-andreas-back-in-2021-was-real-and-the-house-has-a-dark-secret-3429837/), [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/theres-an-npc-in-gta-6-who-knows-hes-in-a-video-game-heres-how-to-find-his-house/)).
- **Look for the house from the 2021 photo.** It's the suburban house with palm trees that appeared, unexplained, as a framed picture in *GTA: San Andreas – Definitive Edition* back in 2021. Fans mapping Leonida matched it on launch day ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-screenshot-hidden-in-san-andreas-back-in-2021-was-real-and-the-house-has-a-dark-secret-3429837/), [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-players-discover-our-real-first-look-at-the-game-was-likely-hidden-in-a-2021-remaster/)).
- **Get into the garage.** Dexerto says players break into it; none of the guides spell out the exact method, and VICE lists no unlock requirement.
- **Use Focus** to spot things you can interact with: [how Focus works](/news/gta-6-focus-ability-explained-how-to-use-level-up/).

## What's on the board

- **The big notes:** "IT'S A SIMULATION", "WHY CAN'T I LEAVE?" and "I NEED TO WAKE UP" ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/theres-an-npc-in-gta-6-who-knows-hes-in-a-video-game-heres-how-to-find-his-house/)), plus "THEY'RE WATCHING US" and "WHY DO PEOPLE DISAPPEAR WHEN I LOOK AWAY?" ([LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/grand-theft-auto-gta-six-npc-simulation-812954-20261122)).
- **Game jokes:** he has noticed NPCs who look identical, neighbours who can't enter their own homes, people glitching through walls and buildings with nothing behind the door ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-screenshot-hidden-in-san-andreas-back-in-2021-was-real-and-the-house-has-a-dark-secret-3429837/)).
- **He can't leave Leonida:** no plane ticket to Los Santos, no road past the border, so boat brochures suggest he plans to sail out ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-screenshot-hidden-in-san-andreas-back-in-2021-was-real-and-the-house-has-a-dark-secret-3429837/), [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/theres-an-npc-in-gta-6-who-knows-hes-in-a-video-game-heres-how-to-find-his-house/)). VICE points out that anyone stranded at sea may meet the [jet-ski dog](/news/gta-6-cool-dog-lucky-dog-jet-ski-1200-cash/).
- **"J" and "L":** notes mention two people who seem free of the world's rules, and the number 6 keeps coming up. Guess who.

Nobody knows whether Rockstar planned the garage back in 2021 or added it after fans obsessed over the photo; Rockstar hasn't said ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-screenshot-hidden-in-san-andreas-back-in-2021-was-real-and-the-house-has-a-dark-secret-3429837/)).
