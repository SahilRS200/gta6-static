If you pre-loaded GTA 6 last week, your console probably has more to download. **Two updates have gone out before launch.**

## What's been reported

- **Update 01.002.000** — landed on 13 November. Dataminers put it at **about 1 GB on PS5**.
- **Update 01.002.001** — the second one. About **602.5 MB on PS5** and roughly **1.6 GB on Xbox**, according to dataminers cited by VICE.
- **Full install:** Forbes reports the pre-load needs **up to 212 GB**, depending on your console.

Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for either update, and hasn't officially called them "day one" patches. Forbes notes there could still be a separate launch-day update, so leave some free space.

## What to do tonight

1. On PS5, highlight GTA VI, press **Options → Check for Update**.
2. On Xbox, open **My games & apps → Manage → Updates**.
3. Leave the console in **Rest Mode** (PS5) or **Sleep** (Xbox) with downloads allowed.
4. Keep at least a few GB free for anything that drops at midnight.
5. **PS5 owners:** also install system software 26.07-14.23.00 (about 1.3 GB). Sony says GTA 6 needs it. [How →](/news/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/)

On slower broadband, do this **now**, not at 11:55 PM.
