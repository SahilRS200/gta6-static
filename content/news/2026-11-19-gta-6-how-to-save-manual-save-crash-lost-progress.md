If you are planning a long GTA 6 session today, **save manually before you put the controller down.** The biggest launch-day horror story so far is US streamer **JasonTheWeen**, who reportedly lost **10 to 12 hours** of progress on PS5. Reports differ on whether the console crashed or was accidentally switched off, but when he got back in, the only save left was from the prologue ([GTA6Bible](https://gta6bible.com/jasontheween-loses-10-hours-of-gta-6-progress-after-ps5/), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-Xbox-crashes-are-losing-hours-of-progress-despite-autosave-system.1453724.0.html)). No spoilers below.

## Why it can happen

- **There is only one autosave slot.** The game autosaves after missions and open-world events, and each new autosave **overwrites** the last one ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-save-in-gta-6-manual-saves-autosave-and-safehouses/)).
- **Loading an older manual save also overwrites the autosave**, according to a roundup of player reports ([WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/gta-6-crash-and-lost-progress-reports-on-ps5-and-xbox-manual-save-tips.453135/)).
- **Some players report crashes too.** A few PS5 and Xbox Series X owners say repeated crashes sent them back through the prologue. These are individual reports; Rockstar has not confirmed a cause or announced a fix ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-Xbox-crashes-are-losing-hours-of-progress-despite-autosave-system.1453724.0.html)).

## How to save manually (PS5 and Xbox)

1. Finish or leave your current mission.
2. Open the pause menu (**Options** on PS5, **Menu** on Xbox).
3. Go to **Game → Save Game** and pick one of the **15 manual slots** ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-save-in-gta-6-manual-saves-autosave-and-safehouses/), [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-how-to-save)).
4. Wait for the save to finish before you quit or switch off.

You don't need a safehouse; saving works anywhere in free roam.

## When the game won't let you save

- **During the prologue**, until the opening section ends. Only the autosave runs until then.
- **During an active mission.**
- **After using any cheat.** Saving (and trophies) stay off until you fully close and restart the game. Cheats are mostly locked anyway: [how GTA 6 cheats work](/news/gta-6-cheats-locked-until-story-complete/).

## After a crash

Autosaves don't show up in the Save Game list, so check **Load Game** first before assuming everything is gone ([WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/gta-6-crash-and-lost-progress-reports-on-ps5-and-xbox-manual-save-tips.453135/)).
