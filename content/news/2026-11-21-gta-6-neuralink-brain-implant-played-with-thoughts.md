**Someone is playing GTA 6 without touching a controller.** On 20 November, a day after launch, Elon Musk's brain-chip company Neuralink posted a two-minute video captioned "Playing GTA VI with a Neuralink", showing a clinical-trial participant playing the game with its implant. It's been doing the rounds in India too ([Inshorts](https://inshorts.com/en/news/man-with-neuralink-implant-plays-gta-vi-using-only-his-thoughts-1795239084688)). No story spoilers here.

## What the video shows

- **Driving, flying and fighting.** The player steers a speedboat under a bridge and into a narrow channel to pick up a package, flies a plane and gets into fist fights ([Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/culture/neuralink-brain-implant-gta-vi-blindsight-approval), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28915/neuralink-shows-a-trial-participant-playing-gta-vi-with-its-brain-implant)).
- **On a PS5.** An on-screen DualSense graphic lights up as each input registers, next to a webcam feed of the player and a "live neural decoding" panel (Inven Global).
- **How he controls it.** The implant reads the part of the brain that controls hand movement. He imagines small movements: tapping his ring finger throws a punch, imagining sliding his right hand forward pulls the plane's nose up, and he has added a foot to cover more buttons (Inven Global).
- **Who it is.** Neuralink hasn't named the participant (Interesting Engineering).

## The fine print

- **Not a product you can buy.** Neuralink says its implants are investigational and not approved by the FDA or any other regulator (Interesting Engineering).
- **Not the first game.** In 2024, participant Noland Arbaugh played Civilization VI and Mario Kart with the implant (Interesting Engineering).
