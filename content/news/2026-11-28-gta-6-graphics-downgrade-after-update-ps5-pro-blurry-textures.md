Does GTA 6 look a little softer since you installed the first update? You're not the only one asking. A growing number of Reddit threads say Leonida looks worse on **PS5 and PS5 Pro** since update **01.002.002** arrived on 23 November, though not every player agrees ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-PS5-Pro-players-note-downgraded-graphics-following-first-update.1461721.0.html)). No story spoilers below.

## What players say changed

- **Blurrier textures and more pop-in,** with distant objects looking softer.
- **Weaker lighting:** sunrises and sunsets look less impressive, and global illumination looks flatter.
- **Pixelated clouds and hazy water reflections** after rain, mostly reported on PS5 Pro.
- **Less detail on faces and hair,** for main characters and NPCs alike.

All of this comes from players' own posts and comparisons ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-PS5-Pro-players-note-downgraded-graphics-following-first-update.1461721.0.html), [Sportskeeda](https://tech.sportskeeda.com/gaming-news/news-gta-6-latest-patch-allegedly-downgrades-texture-quality-lighting-effects)).

## Did Rockstar lower the graphics?

There's no evidence of that yet. The official patch notes for 01.002.002 list crash fixes, the weather bug and mission fixes, and **say nothing about graphics or performance**. Rockstar hasn't commented. Some players guess the game was dialled down to ease the load on consoles; on PS5 Pro, some suspect a change to the PSSR upscaling. Both ideas are unconfirmed ([Sportskeeda](https://tech.sportskeeda.com/gaming-news/news-gta-6-latest-patch-allegedly-downgrades-texture-quality-lighting-effects), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-PS5-Pro-players-note-downgraded-graphics-following-first-update.1461721.0.html)). Notebookcheck points out that Red Dead Redemption 2 drew similar lighting complaints after one of its patches, and a later update with HDR tweaks largely settled them.
