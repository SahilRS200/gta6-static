Streaming GTA 6 on YouTube has two traps in its first week: **streams cut for nudity**, and **copyright claims** on gameplay you recorded yourself. One of the streamers hit was from India. Here's what happened and how to keep your channel safe. No story spoilers.

## 1. Nudity can stop your stream, even if you blur it

- **What happened:** YouTube stopped several GTA 6 live streams over nude or sexual content, [TECHBOOK reports](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/nude-scenes-gta-6-pose-135500878.html). An **Indian streamer, Aman, had blurred explicit scenes** and still had his stream stopped over a character in a bikini. Streamer BYZE says a short nude scene early in the game interrupted his broadcast.
- **The rule:** YouTube's nudity policy covers games too, even 18+ ones. It looks at how long nudity is on screen and how prominent it is; some videos get age-restricted rather than removed.
- **What a strike costs:** usually a warning first. A strike after that blocks uploads and live streams for **one week**, and **three strikes in 90 days** can delete the channel.
- **YouTube admits mistakes:** it later said some videos were removed by mistake and should only have been age-restricted. If yours was, **appeal**.

**What to do:** skip the strip club and any nude scene live, or cut to a "be right back" screen; age-restrict uploads yourself; record first and edit before uploading if you're unsure. Some streamers have moved GTA 6 streams to Twitch or Kick.

## 2. "Mute licensed music" doesn't stop every claim

- **Turn it on anyway:** it's in Settings → Audio and mutes licensed radio songs.
- **The gap:** creators including Zack Scott and Casas Plays still got **Warner Music Group and Atlantic Records claims** over Overmono's track **"Welcome to Leonida"**, which plays as score and in menus, not on the radio, [GTA BOOM reports](https://www.gtaboom.com/the-mute-licensed-music-setting-in-gta-6-does-not-prevent-copyright-claims-2319). Rockstar hadn't addressed it when GTA BOOM reported it on 22 November.
- **What to do:** turn the car radio off too, keep menu time short in edits, and dispute claims on your own gameplay.

## 3. A singer's claims on GTA 6 gameplay were released

- **What happened:** a channel of Colombian singer **Frank El Santo** had GTA 6 footage in YouTube's Content ID, so streamers' gameplay, cutscenes and loading screens were auto-claimed, [Gametimers reports](https://gametimers.it/gta-6-streamer-colpiti-da-rivendicazioni-copyright-scoppia-il-caso-frank-el-santo-e-interviene-youtube/).
- **Fixed:** TeamYouTube said on 22 November that the problem was resolved and it was **releasing those claims**, and warned that misuse of copyright tools can end in channel termination ([TeamYouTube](https://x.com/TeamYouTube/status/2124570379729440892)). If a claim from that channel is still on your video, dispute it.
