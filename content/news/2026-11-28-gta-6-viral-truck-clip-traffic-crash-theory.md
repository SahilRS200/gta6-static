A short GTA 6 clip is doing the rounds this weekend. In **26 seconds**, a player is speeding down a highway when a **truck trailer swings sideways across the road** for no obvious reason. The Reddit post has passed **19,000 upvotes**, Notebookcheck reports ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-Viral-video-reignites-old-Rockstar-theory.1461486.0.html)).

## The theory

Fans have argued since GTA 5 that Rockstar makes traffic swerve into you on purpose when you drive too fast. The idea: forcing you to brake gives the game a moment to load the world ahead, a version of a common trick for hiding loading behind gameplay ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-Viral-video-reignites-old-Rockstar-theory.1461486.0.html)).

## What we actually know

- **No evidence.** Notebookcheck says nothing shows Rockstar uses such a trick in GTA 6, and Rockstar hasn't commented on the clip.
- **Maybe a physics bug.** Some Reddit users think it is a collision bug, and others say they've seen more airborne cars and strange crashes since the latest update ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-Viral-video-reignites-old-Rockstar-theory.1461486.0.html)).
- **Or just Florida.** The most upvoted joke: Rockstar simply recreated Florida traffic.
