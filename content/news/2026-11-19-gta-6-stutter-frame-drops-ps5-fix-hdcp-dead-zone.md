If GTA 6 feels jerky on your base PS5, you're not alone. Two different problems are going around, and only one of them is something you can do much about. No story spoilers below.

## Stutter vs frame drops: they're not the same

- **Frame drops in big fights** are the game itself. Every console targets 30fps, and on the base PS5 heavy action can pull it below that, TechRadar's launch-day live blog notes ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)). One X post claiming about 15–20fps in combat, with delayed button response, passed 2 million views ([PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2026/11/18/gta-6-players-report-fps-drops-ps5/amp)). No setting fixes this, and Rockstar hasn't announced a patch.
- **Stutter** (hitches while you drive or pan the camera) is the one players say they've reduced. TechRadar points out it is a separate issue from the frame-rate dips.

## What players are trying

These are player tips, not official fixes from Rockstar or Sony. They're safe to try and don't delete your saves.

1. **Clear the PS5 cache.** A Reddit tip quoted by TechRadar says clearing the cache and turning off HDCP "makes a massive difference" on base PS5. To clear the cache: turn the PS5 fully off, hold the power button until the second beep to start Safe Mode, connect the controller with a cable, then pick **Clear Cache and Rebuild Database → Clear System Software Cache** ([PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5-safe-mode/)). **Don't pick "Reset PS5"** — that wipes the console.
2. **Turn off HDCP** in **Settings → System → HDMI**. The catch: with HDCP off you can't use streaming apps or play Blu-rays, so turn it back on before Netflix or JioHotstar.
3. **Shrink the stick dead zone.** Some players say the default dead zone is too big, which makes aiming and steering feel sluggish; you can change it in the game's controller settings (TechRadar).
4. **Motion blur: your call.** Many players turn it off for a sharper picture, while others say it smooths motion on OLED TVs.
5. **Use your TV's Game Mode** to cut input lag, and keep the PS5 somewhere it can breathe. Hot consoles can throttle and switch off ([our PS5 fixes](/news/gta-6-locked-not-starting-ps5-fix-india/); game crashing or freezing? [crash and overheating fixes →](/news/gta-6-crashing-freezing-ps5-overheating-xbox-fix/)).

## Will a patch fix it?

**Update, 27 November:** not yet. The first update, [01.002.002](/news/gta-6-first-update-01-002-002-patch-notes-size/), fixed crashes, an autosave bug and stuck wanted levels, but its notes say nothing about stutter or rendering. A viral X post claiming Rockstar had apologised for the lag and would ship a big performance patch on 25 November was **fake**: no such statement exists on Rockstar's Newswire or support site, and no update arrived that day ([PC Games Hardware](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/GTA-6-Spiel-55239/News/GTA-6-Is-a-new-update-to-fix-stuttering-coming-today-1561060/)).
