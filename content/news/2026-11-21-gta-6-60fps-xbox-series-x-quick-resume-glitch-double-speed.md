GTA 6 is **capped at 30fps on every console** with no performance mode ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-pro-is-not-powerful-enough-to-run-gta-6-at-60fps-rockstar-explains-frame-rate-decision)). Now an Xbox Series X player has found a glitch that removes the cap, and two outlets, [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175321-gta-6s-xbox-series-x-fps-glitch-speeds-up-gameplay-alongside-the-frame-rate.html) and [OtakuPT](https://www.otakupt.com/jogos/gta-6-60-fps-xbox-series-x-glitch-dobro-velocidade/), have written it up. The catch makes it a curiosity, not a fix.

## What players found

- A Reddit user shared a method that uses the Xbox's **Quick Resume** feature: start the game with the console set to 60Hz, switch the display to 120Hz after the intro, open another game from the Guide without closing GTA 6, then switch back. The frame rate is then unlocked ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175321-gta-6s-xbox-series-x-fps-glitch-speeds-up-gameplay-alongside-the-frame-rate.html)).
- It needs an **Xbox Series X and a 120Hz TV or monitor**. It was tested on game version 01.002.001; whether it works on the **Series S** or later patches is untested ([OtakuPT](https://www.otakupt.com/jogos/gta-6-60-fps-xbox-series-x-glitch-dobro-velocidade/)).

## The catch: everything speeds up

- Outside cutscenes, GTA 6's gameplay speed is tied to its frame rate, so above 30fps **the whole game runs faster**, about **double speed** at 60fps, OtakuPT reports. Cutscenes still play normally ([OtakuPT](https://www.otakupt.com/jogos/gta-6-60-fps-xbox-series-x-glitch-dobro-velocidade/)).
- **Rockstar and Microsoft haven't commented**, and there's no sign of an official 60fps mode ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175321-gta-6s-xbox-series-x-fps-glitch-speeds-up-gameplay-alongside-the-frame-rate.html)).

## Should you try it?

**No.** A game running twice as fast makes driving and gunfights harder, not smoother, and a glitched state isn't worth risking a save on. Keep a manual save if you experiment. PS5 players can't do this at all.
