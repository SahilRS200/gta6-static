## Update, 26 November: the vote failed

- **75% voted no.** Rockstar's UK developers voted about three to one against a recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union, in the ballot that ran from 12 to 26 November ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rockstar-games-union-vote-fails-as-75-percent-of-devs-vote-no-2001000621)).
- **Rockstar's view:** head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe said the result reflects the team's support for keeping direct relationships and open dialogue with staff ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rockstar-games-union-vote-fails-as-75-percent-of-devs-vote-no-2001000621)).
- **The union's view:** the IWGB said hundreds of Rockstar workers voted yes and that it will "double down" and keep organising without formal recognition ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/following-gta-6-release-rockstar-workers-vote-against-union-recognition-deal-but-those-in-favor-vow-to-keep-fighting/)).
- **The tribunal is separate.** The case over the sacked developers still has no judgment, so the vote doesn't settle whether those dismissals were lawful ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rockstar-games-union-vote-fails-as-75-percent-of-devs-vote-no-2001000621)).
- **For players: nothing changes.** Your game, saves and online services are not affected.

The biggest game launch in history is happening in the same week as a big moment for the people who made it. Rockstar's UK staff voted on whether to recognise a union, and a tribunal over last year's sackings has just finished hearing the case.

## The union vote (before the result)

- **Ballot dates:** voting opened on 12 November and closed on **26 November 2026** ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/gta-6-release-rockstar-workers-tribunal-union-vote/)).
- **Who it covers:** a yes vote could bring up to about **1,600 Rockstar employees** under the union, the IWGB ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/gta-6-release-rockstar-workers-tribunal-union-vote/)). It is a vote on union recognition, **not a strike**, so it does not affect your game or servers ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-will-hold-union-vote-right-on-top-of-grand-theft-auto-6-release/)).
- Union representative Nestor Uriarte said the union will keep going "whatever happens in this ballot". Kotaku notes he also wrote a Guardian opinion piece asking for better pay and conditions ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/live-updates-on-gta-6-the-biggest-launch-in-gaming-history-2000758030)).

## The tribunal

- A UK employment tribunal is deciding whether **31 workers** sacked last year were let go unfairly over union organising ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/gta-6-release-rockstar-workers-tribunal-union-vote/)).
- **Rockstar denies it.** Its lawyer argued the dismissals were for "blameworthy conduct", and Rockstar has said they were about leaks of confidential information, not union membership.
- **No ruling yet.** Both sides have made their closing statements. The judge said she could not give a timeframe for the judgment, so it may take weeks or months.

## Does this change anything for players?

No. GTA 6 is out and playable on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, and nothing here affects your copy, saves or online services. We'll update this post when the tribunal ruling comes out.
